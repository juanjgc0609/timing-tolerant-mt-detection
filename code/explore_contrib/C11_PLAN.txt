2. CANDIDATES (station terms per station and phase, fixed per network; code c11_lib.py, constants c11_lib.P)
  S0    registered +SC (station_corrections.estimate_station_corrections on the 20-event catalogue; +-1 s joint P/S
        read around the catalogue prediction, energy gate 25, median).
  S1    SC-R, c10_screloc.sc_reloc, unchanged (3 passes, level = c10_lib.sigma_catalogue with S0).
  S2    JHD on the 20-event catalogue (c11_lib.s2_terms).  State: terms c (ns, 2), hypocentre (x, t) per event.
        Start: c = S0, events at the catalogue hypocentres.  3 passes; pass 1 uses the registered read (the S0 data);
        passes 2 and 3 RE-READ every event (joint P/S read) within +-0.5 s of the prediction of its current
        hypocentre and the current terms, with energy gate 12.  In each pass:
          (a) alternation, at most 8 times, stop when no term moves by more than 0.002 s:  residual scale = 1.4826 MAD
              of all gated residuals (floor 0.05 s);  each event with >= 4 gated arrivals is relocated (MAP: Huber
              misfit, constant 1.345, of its gated arrivals in homogeneous straight-ray times + Gaussian prior N(0,
              1 km) per coordinate and N(0, 0.1 s) on the origin around the CATALOGUE hypocentre; iteratively
              reweighted Gauss-Newton; depth clipped to 1-20 km; events with fewer gated arrivals stay at the catalogue
              hypocentre);  gauge: the median origin shift of the relocated catalogue events is subtracted from every
              origin;  term of a (station, phase) = MEDIAN over events of (read time - travel time - origin).
          (b) joint stage: at most 15 Gauss-Newton iterations over all terms and all relocatable hypocentres at
              once, Cauchy weights (constant 2.385, scale re-estimated each iteration, floor 0.05 s), the same priors;
              then the same gauge.
        Deviations from the wording of the task, decided on the development set / validation and stated here:
          - the gauge is NOT a zero mean per phase: the true statics are not zero-mean and a common offset moves the
            origin time, which the strict hit rule sees; the absolute time is tied to the catalogue origins and the
            other weak directions are held by the catalogue prior (1 km / 0.1 s, the known catalogue error);
          - relocation by prior-regularised Gauss-Newton instead of a grid search (the prior makes it unimodal);
          - joint Gauss-Newton stage added after the noise-free validation showed that the alternation alone leaves
            0.02-0.08 s (it converges slowly along directions coupling terms and hypocentres).
  S3    self-calibration from the detector's own atoms (c11_lib.s3_terms, c11_run.calibrate).  MT-FW (cap 6) + T3 with
        the S2 terms runs over the 30 unlabelled windows (TAG 4103, kmax 5).  Atoms whose selection score is >= the
        0.80 quantile of all atom scores of the stream are kept (30 atoms per network).  Each is an extra event with
        prior N(0, 3 km) per coordinate and N(0, 0.3 s) on the origin around the refined atom, read within +-0.5 s of
        its own prediction with the S2 terms (gate 12), used only if it has >= 6 gated arrivals.  The catalogue events
        (in their S2 state) and the atoms then go through 2 more passes of the S2 procedure (re-read, alternation, joint
        stage; the gauge uses the catalogue events only).  One iteration (no second detection pass).
  S4    S2 with the first 40 and the first 80 events of the catalogue stream (S2@40, S2@80; start = the +SC median of
        the same events).  Calibration quality is also reported for the plain median with 40 / 80 events (S0@40, S0@80).
  ORA   true statics (c10 MT-ORA: timing level = c10_lib.sigma_oracle).
  No truth enters S0-S4.

4. DETECTORS AND RUN SET (code c11_run.py; loops of c10_lib.py unchanged)
  MT-FW (cap 6) and BOMP-PK, 3 km x 3 depths lattice, origins 5.0-8.0 s step 0.1, +-0.25 s hard peak rule, 5 km
  exclusion; T3 = C10 refinement, frozen (1 km lattice, radius 10 km, origins within 1 s, penalised peak search with
  variance sig^2 + cell term, record score = selection statistic). Timing level of T3 for a term set: the C10 rule
  c10_lib.sigma_catalogue recomputed with that term set (its own catalogue for S2@40 / S2@80); ORA: sigma_oracle.
  MT family: S0, S1, S2, S3, S2@40, S2@80, ORA, each hard and +T3 (14 runs per window).
  BOMP family (cost): S0, S2 hard and +T3, ORA +T3 (5 runs per window). BOMP with S1, S3, S4 and ORA hard: not run.
  HUBER-N: not run (not cheap: every term set needs its own operator tables in c8_lib).
  CEIL-GD: not run.
  Budget rule: two processes (thin, strong), networks in increasing j, cells inside j. Expected 2.7 h. If the runs are
  not finished at 21:00 UTC they are stopped and the analysis uses the network indices complete in BOTH regimes and
  all three cells; nothing else changes.

5. ENDPOINTS AND ANALYSIS (c11_analyze.py; c10_analyze.Boot)
  (1) rms error of the terms against the true statics (median removed), per regime and cell; |offset|.
  (2) recall at <= 0.1 and <= 0.25 realised false events per window, exact FROC, operating point re-chosen per
      replicate; hit rules LOC+T (epicentre within 5 km and |origin error| <= 0.5 s; PRIMARY here) and LOC (registered);
      paired differences to S0 (hard) and to S0+T3; hierarchical bootstrap, strata regime x cell, networks then
      windows, 2000 replicates, 95% percentile intervals; pooled and per regime (strong clutter reported separately:
      the catalogue peaks are contaminated there).
  (3) fraction of the oracle gain, ratio bootstrapped: hard (X - S0) / (ORA - S0); T3 (X+T3 - S0+T3) / (ORA+T3 -
      S0+T3); total (X+T3 - S0) / (ORA+T3 - S0).
  (4) median epicentral and |origin-time| error of the hit atoms (LOC rule, all atoms; bootstrap over networks).
  (5) runtime per window and per calibration.
  PRIMARY CANDIDATE, by rule: the estimator among S2 and S3 with the smaller sum of pooled development medians; a
  difference below 0.01 s goes to S2 (simpler, no detection pass).  S2: 0.173; S3: 0.172  ->  S2.
  PRIMARY CONTRAST: MT@S2+T3 - MT@S0+T3 (the effect of the terms with the detector fixed), STANDARD windows, LOC+T,
  <= 0.25 false events per window, regimes and cells pooled.
  Named secondary contrasts (same endpoint): MT@S2 - MT@S0 (hard); MT@S2+T3 - MT@S0 (total, terms + refinement);
  BOMP@S2+T3 - BOMP@S0+T3; BOMP@S2+T3 - BOMP@S0; the same for S3, S1, S2@40, S2@80 in the MT family; all of them under
  LOC, at 0.1, per regime and in DENSE windows.
  JUDGEMENT (fixed now): "plausible contribution" if
    (A) primary contrast >= +0.05 with a 95% interval excluding 0, AND no loss in dense windows (the same contrast in
        DENSE windows, LOC+T and LOC at 0.25, point estimate >= -0.01); or
    (B) fraction of the oracle gain (T3 form, STANDARD, LOC+T, 0.25) >= 0.5 as a point estimate with the interval of
        its numerator (the primary contrast) excluding 0.
  Otherwise "not shown". The total contrast (S2+T3 against the registered detector S0 hard) is reported next to it
  because C10 measured SC-R that way (+0.123), but it mixes the terms with T3 and is not the criterion.
  No multiplicity correction; every contrast other than the primary one is descriptive.
