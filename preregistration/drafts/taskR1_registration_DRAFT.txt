DRAFT - NOT REGISTERED. Proposed text for Task R1, the registered real-data test that follows the gate met in
exploration C18j (Appendix Y.32; log entry "Compuerta de la proxima prueba real: resultado en CAL"). It is appended to
PREREGISTRO_1j_1k.txt (in Spanish, ASCII, with the English copy line-aligned) only if the author approves it, with the
wall-clock time in the first line and the SHA-256 of the log delivered through the chat before any analysis touches the
waveforms. The points marked [AUTHOR DECISION] must be resolved first. No waveform of these data has been read by any
analysis of the project; only the file list, the checksums, catalog.dat, the README and the first lines of INPUT.in have been opened
(DetectedFinal.dat has not been opened; its count of 140 detections comes from results/taskR0/REPORT_REALDATA.md).

Task R1 ([DATE AND WALL-CLOCK TIME AT REGISTRATION]), written before any detector, picker or associator reads the
waveforms of the Hi-net swarm.

Question. With the timing corrections that met the gate on the Ridgecrest development segment (layered medium, station
terms read over +-2 s, 2 Hz matched filter) and an uncapped moment-tensor statistic, does a pick-free detector recall
catalogued events of a dense real sequence, with origin times matched, at a rate comparable to PhaseNet with the
published associators, on data never used for development?

Data. Geolab-USTC/MatchLocate2, commit bac5ccb00816a4ec0aaab215ea1c342c730d412f, folder example/ (GPL-3.0; NIED Hi-net
data): continuous three-component records of 7 Hi-net stations (files
Trace/20120902/N.<STA>.{E,N,U}; stations ATKH, INWH, NAZH, ONIH, THTH, TSTH, YNZH) of the Fukushima-Yamagata swarm of 2 September 2012 from 03:00 (README), 14 JMA events
(catalog.dat, M 2.1 to 3.2, 03:22:25 to 03:48:23, depths 6.3 to 8.4 km) and 140 Match&Locate detections
(MultipleTemplate/DetectedFinal.dat). sha256 of every file in data/checksums/hinet_matchlocate2_example.sha256.
Declared properties: 100 Hz, prefiltered (energy in 1-15 Hz), no instrument response (counts), 1 Hz borehole sensors
with known horizontal orientation errors, stations at 12 to 30 km from the swarm.

[AUTHOR DECISION 1: ground truth and segments.] (a) JMA catalogue only: CAL = origins before 03:34:00 (5 events, for
station terms and development checks), EVAL = the remaining 9 events; very low power, the result is descriptive at best.
(b) Match&Locate detections as the target set (template-matching detections, derived from another detector and biased
toward events similar to the 14 templates), JMA events for station terms: CAL = before 03:30:00, EVAL = after, targets =
M&L detections with relative magnitude >= 1.5 in EVAL. (c) Both, (a) as primary and (b) as secondary. Recommended: (c).

[AUTHOR DECISION 2: velocity model.] The candidate of C18j used the Hadley-Kanamori model of southern California. For
north-eastern Japan the registered model must be a published 1-D model of the region, entered from the literature
(for example JMA2001, or the model of Hasegawa et al. for the Tohoku arc) and frozen before the waveforms are read; or,
as a fallback, a linear gradient fitted pick-free on the CAL events with the C18a procedure (search +-2 s). The model
has not been chosen and no number is invented here.

[AUTHOR DECISION 3: horizontal components.] The moment-tensor statistic needs three components with known orientation.
Options: (a) use the horizontals as recorded; (b) correct them with published Hi-net sensor orientations, if available
from the literature without downloading data; (c) use the vertical component only for the pick-free detectors (which
changes the method). Recommended: (a) as primary, with the orientation error declared as a limitation.

Preprocessing (as R0, adapted): counts, demean, detrend, zero-phase band-pass 0.5-6 Hz (order 4), resampling 100 -> 16
Hz, components (E, N, -U), per-station scale by the robust deviation of CAL; local flat frame centred on the mean of
the CAL epicentres; stations at their coordinates from the SAC headers (elevations ignored).

Methods (frozen code of R0 with the C18j changes, which will be frozen as a module before registration):
  P1 (primary) MT-FW without cap, layered medium [DECISION 2], catalogue station terms with a +-2 s search, 2 Hz filter,
     hard +-0.25 s read, kmax 6, 40 s windows every 3 s owning origins in [5, 8) s, de-duplication 5 km / 1 s.
  P2 the same with LOG6; P3 the registered R0 detector M1 (cap 6, homogeneous VP 6.0, +-1 s terms); P4 BOMP-PK with the
     P1 timing. Comparators: PhaseNet (original model, as R0), GaMMA and PyOcto on the R0 grids adapted to the frame and
     to the model of DECISION 2, raw and with +SC; BESTPICK = the best of the four in each replicate, as in R0.
Endpoint (as R0): recall of the target events at <= 30 false detections per hour, exact FROC, LOC+T (epicentre within
5 km and origin within 0.5 s) primary and LOC secondary; operating point per method on EVAL, and a deployable threshold
fixed on CAL as secondary; 60 s block bootstrap, 2000 replicates.

[AUTHOR DECISION 4: rules.] R0 measured -0.383 for the registered detector. Proposed: R1-a (primary) P1 - BESTPICK,
LOC+T, 30/h: lower bound of the 95 % interval > -0.20 (P1 recovers at least a substantial part of the pick-based recall);
R1-b P1 - P3 > 0 (the C18j corrections transfer to unseen data); superiority only if the lower bound of P1 - BESTPICK
> 0. With DECISION 1(a) the interval is too wide for any rule; this is declared before running.

Development allowed before freezing: only on CAL of the Hi-net segment (origins before the CAL limit): checks of units,
polarity, alignment and cost; no constant is tuned for recall. Freeze: sha256 of the code and of the data list appended to
the log before the first computation on EVAL; single run; independent verification as for 2M and R0.

Declared limits: one swarm, 33 to 60 minutes, 7 stations, catalogue of 14 events (or a detector-derived catalogue), no
instrument response, sensor orientation errors, a candidate selected after about twenty configurations on 16 Ridgecrest
events (the selection effect that this test exists to remove); a negative is reported as such and closes the
pick-free real-data line of the thesis.
