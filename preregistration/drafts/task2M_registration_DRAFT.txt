Quality control (NEW with respect to C16; [AUTHOR DECISION]). The new days have flat stretches (repeated identical
values: telemetry fill) and digital spikes that C16 did not have to handle; without control, the amplitude
rule gives factors of up to 1e15 (a channel with more than half of the day flat has a median level ~0) and dead stations
and digital spikes would be injected, which would artificially favour the methods with bounded influence. Rule qc1,
on the raw 25 Hz counts of each channel: every sample in a run of >= 25 identical consecutive values (>= 1 s)
is invalid, and so is every sample with |x - median| > 1e4 robust deviations (1.4826 MAD, computed without the
flat runs); invalid samples are treated like the gaps of C16 (no slot with an invalid sample from
any station of the set within 60 s is eligible). Station rule (t2m_stations.py): S = all
stations with files for the day; while |S| > 8, remove the station whose removal most increases the eligible slots
E(S), only if E increases by at least 20 %; the day is used if |S| >= 8 and E(S) >= 300. Both rules were written after
seeing quality and level statistics of the noise of the new days (without injecting events or running any detector)
and are declared as such. Applied to the C16 day they keep the 14 stations but remove 394 of its 2037 eligible
slots (19 %: flat stretches of MURB, BADI and ATPC), that is, C16 injected some segments with flat fill; C16 is not
reanalysed.
