to 13 % for that non-inferiority rule, and margins are a scientific choice the data cannot fix. A
superiority rule needs no margin. Simulated with the target counts of this design, an exact one-sided
McNemar test on the discordant target events (hit by one method and not the other) at 0.025 keeps the
false-positive rate at 0.000 to 0.018 when the true difference is 0, with power 0.13 to 0.17 at +0.10 and

1. Alignment of the matched-filter terms (changes the registration). With the registered reading (+-2 s,
joint P and S, gate 25) the P terms of ATKH, TSTH and YNZH came out +1.66, -1.70 and +1.60 s,
incompatible with the pick terms of the same stations (-0.21, +0.17 and +0.23 s). At ATKH and YNZH (12
km, S-P 1.9 s) the +-2 s P window reaches the S arrival, which in these borehole records carries one
hundred to one thousand times the P energy; at THTH and TSTH the P energy of the CAL events is near or
below the gate. Correction, chosen by an alignment criterion and not by recall: readings searched within
+-min(2 s, half the S-P time predicted by JMA2001) and matched-filter terms = pick terms + the lag of
the energy peak behind the onset, one per phase, equal to the median over the station-phases with at
least 3 readings above the gate of (median reading - pick term); on CAL 0.265 s for P and 0.244 s for S
(r1_run.mf_terms). The terms of P1 thus depend on the CAL picks; the detection of P1 remains pick-free.

with VS = VP / 1.73; the pick-free engine and the hybrid use the 0.25 km layers of c18_tt. It is the same model.
