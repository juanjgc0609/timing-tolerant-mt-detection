# Independent audit of the doctoral proposal

**Object:** `Doctoral_Proposal.md` (7,271 lines, ~84,000 words), with the evidence from `/home/claude/work` (preregistration `PREREGISTRO_1j_1k.txt`, `task2a_report.txt`, `analyze_2a_out.txt`, `task2b_report.txt`, `analyze_2b_out.txt`, `rft_report.txt`, `station_corrections_report.txt`, `qm_report.txt`, `gen_realistic_report.txt`, `certified_fw_report.txt`) and the companion files in Spanish (`artifact_manifest_and_error_log.md`, `executive_summary.md`).

**Stance:** external reviewer who did not take part in producing the work. The calibration of a doctoral proposal is applied (not that of a finished paper): the audit evaluates whether the claims are supported at the level at which they are stated, whether the plan can deliver what it promises and whether the evidence so far is honest and sufficient. Volume does not score. Claims with neither their own support nor a solid reference are marked *"Unproven speculative assertiveness"*.

**Reading performed:** Abstract, Sections 1 to 4, 7.5, 13 to 17, 19, 20 and 23 in full; Appendices Y.11 to Y.19, Z and AA in full or nearly in full; Sections 5, 6.1, 7.1 to 7.4, 12, 18, 21, 22, Appendices A to F and T.1 skimmed. Numbers were checked against the result files and references were verified on the web.

---

## 1. Editorial verdict

- **Overall score:** 56 / 100
- **Simulated decision (doctoral proposal committee):** **Major revision.** Approvable as a programme only after restructuring and after closing three evidence gaps before the first milestone (closest comparators, statistics with the network as the unit, and a first result on real data or real noise).
- **Verdict:** The proposal is exceptionally honest in its accounting of errors and in the verification of its own numbers: all the figures checked match the output files, and the preregistration with timestamps, the independent verifier and the bit-for-bit checks are practices above the average of a thesis. But the central claim (G1: the coherent moment-tensor detector detects more events than the state of the art at an equal false-alarm rate) rests exclusively on synthetics generated by the same code, with a modest medium mismatch, sources inside the hypothesis class, five networks per cell, an operating point chosen on the evaluation itself, and without running the two closest antecedents (block-sparse inversion [129] and mechanism-corrected stacking [122], [133]). Moreover, the method that the thesis claims to defend (off-the-grid group BLASSO solved by Frank-Wolfe) has not been implemented: all the evidence is from MT-FW, a greedy, capped relative of the *block OMP* of [129]. The document is internally contradictory about what the primary question is, what the primary endpoint is and what counts as a contribution, and its 48-month plan and its publication plan do not contain the method that the same text declares to be the thesis hypothesis. The dominant sin is not numerical *overclaiming* (that is well controlled) but the **dispersion of the programme** and the **gap between the framework (constrained optimisation governed by PDEs) and what has actually been tested (a greedy subspace detector on a homogeneous straight-ray operator).**

## 3. The 10 most important weaknesses, by severity, with feasible correction

**1. The central claim has only synthetic evidence from a generator of the same code, with modest mismatch.** The generator "that the detector does not know" (Y.14) is a 1D linear-gradient medium whose time error against the homogeneous model has a median of -0.007 s (P) and -0.012 s (S), p95 +0.24 s (`gen_realistic_report.txt`); the noise is white at 16 Hz; the source is a point double couple inside the 6-dimensional subspace of the detector (L6201); the clutter consists of isolated Ricker pulses. The phrases "a medium ... the detector does not know" (L67-68, L2851) suggest a robustness that has not been put to the test with 3D structure, real noise or non-DC sources. *Feasible correction:* before any new synthetic experiment, (a) inject the synthetic waveforms into real noise windows from the RSNC (Task 1p), (b) replace the synthesis operator with 3D Green's functions from a published model (SPECFEM or Instaseis SGT) while keeping the detector homogeneous, (c) include sources with CLVD and isotropic components and a finite source of 1-2 s, and (d) bring the real decimation test (G1') forward to semester 1. All of this fits in the current budget because MT-FW is cheap.

**2. The closest comparators are missing, and MT-FW is at its core a relative of the antecedent.** The text itself admits it (L514-518, L1861-1862, L6227-6231): the block-sparse inversion of Vera Rodriguez et al. [129] and the mechanism-corrected stacking [122], [133] have not been run; nor have MALMI [110] or [112]. As long as that does not exist, the advantage over QuakeMigrate and GaMMA says nothing about the contribution relative to the relevant state of the art (WEAK BASELINE in the sense of the skill). *Correction:* preregister a Task 2C on the same 1,200 windows of 2A with (i) *block OMP* with full refitting of all atoms over the same dictionary (an implementation of a few hundred lines, given that MT-FW already builds $G(\theta)$), (ii) polarity/mechanism-corrected stacking in the form of [133] or [122], and (iii) MALMI with its published configuration, all at an equal realised false-event rate and with the same decision rule.

**3. The method the thesis defends is not implemented; the evidence is from another algorithm.** Section 7.5 and Appendix AA define the group BLASSO (16a) and its solution by (sliding) Frank-Wolfe with a certified oracle; all the evidence is from MT-FW, which does not refit atoms, does not penalise, clips the per-station norm, excludes 5 km and has no rate (AA.4(e)-(h), E29). The propositions of AA do not apply to what was measured. Moreover, neither the schedule (Table 8, L2684-2691) nor the publication units (L2791-2797) include the development of the BLASSO solver, the sliding step or the detector paper. *Correction:* implement fully corrective Frank-Wolfe on (AA.3) with the duality gap reported per window (AA.4(d)) and the sliding step of [116] with amplitudes in $\mathbb R^6$; compare it with MT-FW on the windows of 2A; add an explicit T/E work package and a publication unit "group BLASSO detector" as paper 1.

**4. The statistical inference does not respect the unit of replication.** Five networks per cell and two seeds; per-window intervals that ignore the clustering by network; rules decided on point estimates with threshold 0.05; the per-network bootstrap leaves 4 of 12 intervals of 2A-a with zero at 0.1 (`task2a_report.txt` §4.7) and rule 2B with weak clutter holds in only 0.59-0.62 of the replicates (`task2b_report.txt`). Section 14 declares that the independent unit is the complete realisation (L2445-2446) and then does not apply it. *Correction:* raise to >=30 networks per cell (cost ~1.5 h of compute per seed and regime according to the report itself), declare as primary analysis a mixed-effects model or a hierarchical network->window bootstrap, decide on the lower bound of the interval and not on the point, and apply Benjamini-Hochberg to the set of rules, as Section 14 promises.

**5. Contradictions about what the thesis is and what its primary endpoint is.** Primary question = minimal physical information (L553-566) against "The dissertation hypothesis is G1" (L741) and the box at L654-656; primary endpoint = false-event rate at non-inferior recall (L232, L2410; App. D L3017 "G1 false-association reduction") against recall at fixed false-event rate (L736, L1024-1026, L3052); "four results" (L89) against "three things" (L2844); P5 as a primary claim (L2781) and objective 7 (L1102) against "not a contribution of the thesis" (L104-105, L2739-2740); objective 3 "formulate silence as unique-continuation exclusion" (L1095) against "motivated by unique continuation but not derived from it" (L436). *Correction:* a single primary hypothesis (G1 in the form G1'), a single primary endpoint (recall at realised false-event rate with a frozen threshold, see 8), everything else declared secondary or exploratory; rewrite 4.1, 4.5, 13.4, App. D, Table 8 and 20.0 so that they say the same thing; move family F to an appendix of options.

**6. The 2B ablation does not isolate what the proposal needs to isolate, and suggests that the effect is polarisation (already prior art).** Replacing $G$ by the identity removes at the same time the sign of the pattern, the polarisation constraint and the amplitude geometry (L6405-6412); MT-FW-FREE+SC inherits the cap of 6, the 5 km exclusion and the deflation tuned for the MT statistic (L6417-6421), so that it is a weakened rival; and with strong clutter FREE falls below WAVE-PT@20 in all cells (L6398-6403), which suggests that what pays off against strong clutter is polarisation, which already enters the migration of [112]. The reading "the radiation operator carries the advantage with strong clutter" (L2733-2734, L2857) is therefore stronger than what was measured. *Correction:* preregister (i) FREE-PT (free amplitude along the ray for P and in the transverse plane for S), (ii) MT with signs randomised per station (preserves polarisation and amplitudes, destroys the sign pattern), and (iii) FREE with its own cap and exclusion calibrated on seed 999.

**7. Fairness of comparators: the effective contrast is against a single method, and there are omissions.** The STA/LTA, in white noise at 16 Hz, detects 9.6-16.8% of the arrivals, and only 20% of the events receive three picks (L6166-6170); GaMMA and PyOcto stay almost at zero and the best comparator is QuakeMigrate in 24 of 24 cases. QuakeMigrate was recalibrated only with weak clutter, in two cells, 44 sources (L6178-6181); half of the events are in two-source windows with origin times less than 2 s apart, which a single-trace trigger does not separate (L6195-6198). With optimistic picks, GaMMA-DY+SC beats MT-FW+SC at 0.1 in 4 of 6 weak-clutter cells in the full sample (see inconsistency 8), and the text only reports the tie at 0.25. *Correction:* realistic noise with microseism so that a deep picker operates in its domain (or retrained via SeisBench); recalibrate QuakeMigrate per regime and cell; report the subset K<=1 as co-primary; report the comparison with GaMMA-DY at both caps.

**8. No deployable false-alarm control has been demonstrated.** The headline "0.25 realized false events per window" (L64, L2849) is obtained with the operating point chosen on the evaluation windows themselves (L6010-6012), which the abstract and the conclusion do not say. The deployable rule 2A-e fails under the strict reading: the frozen threshold realises 0.22-0.23 against a limit of 0.20 (L6138-6149). The conformal guarantee requires exchangeability and breaks under the change of regime (Y.17: 17-22 times the nominal rate under strong clutter); nothing has been calibrated on real noise. *Correction:* make the threshold frozen on disjoint networks the primary endpoint; calibrate on noise-only windows from the RSNC and report false events per day on continuous data; evaluate adaptive or per-regime conformal prediction (stratified by hour, season of the year and cultural activity).

**9. Disproportionate scope and a budget misaligned with the hypothesis.** The budget (Table 5, L2192-2210) is taken by the conditional representation branches (up to 1,800 of 2,870 GPU-h) that the text itself declares not to be a contribution; the allocation is not confirmed; F2-F5 remain as objectives. The velocity-model gate (L2551-2563) prevents waveform claims at 2 Hz in Colombia, and polarisation is useful at 1 of 5 stations (T.1). *Correction:* define a minimum viable thesis of three papers: (i) theory and synthetic benchmark of the detector with comparators [129]/[133]; (ii) real test by decimation on the RSNC with the SGC catalogue and SeisMonitor; (iii) observability certificate as a design criterion validated with real data (real B3). Move optimal transport and learned representation to future work and reallocate the compute.

**10. Framing of theoretical novelty beyond what has been demonstrated.** "Constraint generation from PDE control theory; no prior found" (Table 1, L541) and P1 "five families ... each derived" (L2757-2761): C-II is not derived from unique continuation (L436), the certificate of 6.1 is the Fisher matrix of arrival times with slowness at the source (classical in Geiger-type location), and the link with the geometric control condition is not demonstrated. The "certified" search is exact on a grid that dense search already traverses exactly. *Unproven speculative assertiveness.* *Correction:* present the contribution as a unification (BLASSO-GLR-observability) and seek a result that a Fisher or GDOP analysis cannot give: for example, prove Conjecture AA.1 for $K=2$ with a separation bound as a function of the weighted observability, or validate on real data that the observability map predicts the false-event rate of the operational catalogue (real B3).

## 4. Strengths

1. **Exemplary accounting of errors.** The register E1-E31 documents the cause, detection, scope and correction of each error, including those that reversed results (E15 ties, E24 pure-SV generator, E26 undetectable clutter delivered to GaMMA, E28 unmeasured EEC guarantee). Few proposals withdraw their own positives in this way.
2. **Real preregistration, with timestamps and seed control.** 2A was registered before evaluation; seeds 2024 and 7331 had not been used with the generator, the picker or the QuakeMigrate wrapper; the frozen values of 2A-e were written before the first evaluation output.
3. **Independent verification with re-derivation of tables.** The verifier reproduced the figures with its own FROC implementation, checked the sign of the corrections with oracle statics, positive controls of GaMMA and PyOcto (0.88 and 0.69), absence of leakage, and documented the deviations (D1-D8).
4. **Meaningful negative controls and ablations.** The control with the geometry of another node (NODE), the control with the directions of another hypothesis and the 2B ablation within the same solver are the kind of control that is usually missing in detection methods.
5. **Honesty in the qualifiers.** Y.18a enumerates what 2A does not establish; Y.19 says that 2B does not isolate the sign; the text acknowledges that QuakeMigrate is the effective competitor and that strong clutter breaks every threshold calibrated on Gaussian noise.
6. **Well-crafted Appendix AA.** Explicit hypotheses, code-theory gaps listed, conjecture separated from proposition, and the warning that nothing bounds false events in windows with events (L7270-7271).
7. **Useful and correct threshold study (Y.17).** It shows that the EEC of a $\chi^2_6$ field does not describe the implemented statistic (mean of $2T$ 12.6-13.5 against 6; KS 0.52-0.58) and that conformal and GEV calibrate within the predictive band in 24/24 combinations with Gaussian noise.
8. **Computational reproducibility.** A bit-for-bit check that detected a change of OpenBLAS kernel and resolved it without relaxing tolerances.
9. **Correct positioning after E30.** Sections 3.2 and 7.5 cite and acknowledge [129], [130], GRiD MT, [121]-[138] and declare absence in targeted searches, not priority.
10. **Numbers faithful to the files.** All the spot checks (section 5) match to the printed decimal.
