M1. Supplement Y.32, C18a: "Between 37 and 47 per cent of the readings of the larger events are off by more
    than the read tolerance whatever the medium (27 to 46 per cent after the event term)".
    The 37-47 range is taken over all five models (1-0.627 U8 HOMfit to 1-0.531 N6 HOM6). The parenthesis
    uses HOM6 only. Over all five models with the event term removed, the fractions within 0.25 s go
    from 0.4615 (N4 HKr) to 0.736 (U8 HOMfit), so the share outside is 26 to 54 per cent.
    Source: C18/c18a_N4_M2.json "_evterm" HKr frac_025 0.4615; C18/c18a_U8_M2.json "_evterm" HOMfit
    frac_025 0.736 (HOM6 only: 0.625, 0.728, 0.594, 0.538 -> 27 to 46 per cent).
    Correction: "(26 to 54 per cent after the event term)", or "(27 to 46 per cent with the registered
    medium after the event term)".

    On N6, HKr gives 0.5918 against 0.5306 for HOM6, a gain of 0.061.
    Source: C18/c18a_summary.txt:3. Correction: "No model gains more than about 0.06", or "at most 0.061".
    Minor: it rounds to 0.06.

    window was about 75 in score units)".

    conclusion does not change. The other numbers in the example are correct: 2c = 12;

    sqrt(2*75) = 12.25.
