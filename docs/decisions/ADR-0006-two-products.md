# ADR-0006: The undergraduate thesis and the doctoral line are separate products

Date: 18 October 2026. Status: adopted as the default of the review of the plan
(`docs/reviews/2026-10-18_plan_review.md`, Section 2.6); the author can reverse it. It amends ADR-0001.

Context. The work is formally an undergraduate thesis (trabajo de grado) at Universidad Icesi. ADR-0001 fixed the
doctoral rubric as the target and stated that the thesis is not concluded until the confirmatory phase (phase 6). The
roadmap of phases 3 to 6 needs new data, a GPU and work of the order of a doctoral programme. Tying the degree to it
can delay the degree by many months without improving the doctoral work.

Decision.
1. **Product A, the undergraduate thesis.** It is delivered with its present scope (benchmark, G1-R, R0, R1), once the
   integrity items of phase 2 are applied (hit rule, duplicate detections, Section 6.2 of the thesis, the
   double-couple bias, E52 in 2C and 2D). Its conclusion does not wait for phases 3 to 6.
2. **Product B, the doctoral line.** Phases 3 to 7 of the roadmap continue as a separate research programme: a
   doctoral proposal, a preprint on the failure atlas, then the new model and the confirmatory tests. The doctoral
   rubric of ADR-0001 applies to product B.
3. The audit score is a diagnostic, not a target. Progress of product B is measured by external milestones: a human
   director, a preprint submitted, a registered test anchored under ADR-0004, a peer review.

Consequences. ADR-0001 stays in force for product B. The sentence "the thesis is not concluded until the confirmatory
phase of the roadmap is complete" applies to the doctoral thesis, not to the trabajo de grado. Nothing registered changes.
