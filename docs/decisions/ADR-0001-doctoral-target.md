# ADR-0001: The work is assessed at doctoral level

Date: 18 October 2026. Status: accepted (decision of the author). Amended by ADR-0006: it applies to the doctoral line;
the undergraduate thesis is a separate product.

Context. The project is formally an undergraduate thesis (trabajo de grado). Audits 6 and 7 were recalibrated to that
level and reported 67.5 and 70.3 out of 100; on the doctoral rubric the same work scored 53.5 and 53.3. The author
stated that the undergraduate framing is context, not a lower target.

Decision. All work is planned and audited against the doctoral rubric of `docs/protocol/RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md`,
Section 9. The undergraduate scores of audits 6 and 7 are kept as history and are not targets. The thesis is not
concluded until the confirmatory phase of the roadmap is complete.

Consequences. The baseline is 53.3/100. Progress is measured by new evidence, theory and novelty, not by text
corrections alone.
