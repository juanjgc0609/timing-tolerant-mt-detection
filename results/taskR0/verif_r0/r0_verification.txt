6. Step 5 - adversarial checks

  6.1 Time alignment. All HH traces start at 18:59:59.9983 (WBM 19:00:00.0031); r0_lib rounds the 100 Hz offset,
  error <= 3.1 ms; no gaps (one trace per channel). resample_poly keeps sample 0 at t = 0; window k starts at sample
  round(ts/DT), exact for the 3 s step; engine sample = floor((d/V + tau + corr)/DT) and the atom's absolute origin
  is window start + tau, so no offset is introduced. Catalogue times are UTC like the waveforms. PhaseNet pick
  residuals against the catalogue (homogeneous VP 6.0, VS 6.0/1.73, diag_alignment.py) are positive at every
  station (P +0.22 to +0.87 s, S +0.47 to +1.38 s), identical between CAL and EVAL within 0.1-0.2 s: there is no
  clock or segment offset, only a slow medium relative to 6.0 km/s. Origin error of the nearest detection within
  5 km of each target (median, det - catalogue): PyOcto +0.13 (N8), +0.02 (N6); GaMMA +0.21, +0.32; M2 +0.92,
  +0.64, +1.41, +0.17 (N8, N6, N4, U8); M1 +1.01 to +1.31 except U8 +0.39. The pick-free detectors are late by about
  1 s; the cause is the timing terms (6.6), not an alignment bug: with the S2 first read widened to +-2 s the M2
  bias goes to -0.26 s (N8) and -0.07 s (U8).

  6.2 Local frame and catalogue conversion. The same equirectangular frame (111.19 km/deg, cos lat0) is used for
  stations, catalogue, detector grid and associators, with x = east, y = north. Against WGS84 geodesics
  (diag_projection.py) station-event epicentral distances differ by +0.018 km on average and at most 0.107 km
  (<= 0.031 s of S travel time). Station elevations (667-1839 m) are ignored and the stations sit at z = 0 for all
  methods alike. All 45 targets are within x -5.6..+7.7, y -9.5..+8.1 km, inside the +-21 km grid of the detector
  and the +-21.4 km box of the associators.

  6.3 Units and polarity of Z. Counts divided by the per-component scalar sensitivity, demean, linear detrend;
  per-station normalisation by the CAL robust sd makes the absolute units irrelevant for the detector. Components
  are (E, N, -Z), consistent with x = east, y = north, z = depth in the generator. PhaseNet receives raw (E, N, Z up)
  at 100 Hz, as its training convention requires. POST HOC sensitivity (verif_zflip.py, N8, frozen code with +Z):
  M2 gets worse (any-score LOC+T hits 1 vs 5 targets, recall 0.000 at every rate and rule, median nearest-atom
  distance 21.1 vs 12.8 km). The registered -Z is the better choice; polarity does not hide a positive.

  6.4 Station selection. diag_subnets.py re-derives the registered rule from stations.csv and the registration
  centroid (35.6773 N, 117.5418 W): the 8 closest HH stations are exactly N8 (N6, N4 = its 6 and 4 closest); the
  narrowest azimuthal sector holding 8 HH stations is 256-6 degrees (110 degrees) with exactly the U8 stations.
  r0_lib.stations reads the HH rows only and each selected station has a single HH location code.

  6.5 CAL/EVAL separation. CAL catalogue (origins < 900 s) is used for the frame centre, the station scale (CAL
  segment of the traces), +SC, S2 and sigma; EVAL catalogue rows enter only r0_analyze (and my scripts). Detections
  and targets are both restricted to [900, 3560) s; EVAL windows start at 895 s and only own origins >= 900 s. The
  first EVAL window reads 5 s of CAL waveform, which is data, not labels. No EVAL information reaches any detector
  or comparator.

  6.6 Information symmetry. Detector and comparators share the stations, frame, VP/VS, search region and the CAL
  +SC corrections (the +SC comparator arms). The comparators receive no catalogue information. PhaseNet carries
  prior training on northern California data, which is the registered comparator. The design is, if anything,
  favourable to the comparators in one respect (BESTPICK = envelope over 25 configurations x 2 pick versions vs one
  configuration for M2, declared in the amendment) and unfavourable in another: their score is the integer number
  of picks and r0_analyze keeps only detections strictly above the threshold score, so all detections tied at the
  threshold are dropped; the tie-break by the associator's own score stated in the registration is not implemented
  (the GaMMA score is stored but unused; PyOcto gives none). This penalises the comparators, not the detector.

  6.7 Could an implementation error have hidden a positive?
  (a) Ranking/threshold: the any-score ceiling of M2 (fraction of targets with any LOC+T hit, i.e. its recall under
  a perfect ranking with unlimited false budget) is N8 0.111, N6 0.044, N4 0.000, U8 0.089 (diag_ceiling.py), so
  pooled M2 - BESTPICK cannot exceed -0.328 at 30/h whatever the scores; under LOC -0.306; with 10 km and 1.5 s
  -0.417. Removing the de-duplication changes nothing (ceilings identical, recall identical).
  (b) Station terms: diag_corrections.py (CAL only, N8) shows that the frozen +SC estimator (peak search +-1.0 s)
  truncates the S delays: SRT, CCC, TOW2 S terms +0.16, +0.12, +0.22 s with a +-1 s search vs +1.74, +1.78, +1.91 s
  with +-2 s, against PhaseNet S-onset residuals of +1.1 to +1.4 s; several S delays sit at the +-1 s edge. M2 does
  not use +SC directly: POST HOC verif_widesc.py (+SC with +-2 s) gives M2 atoms bit-identical to the frozen ones,
  because the S2 joint estimation re-reads the catalogue from scratch. But S2 has the same +-1.0 s first read
  (c11_lib.P search0). POST HOC verif_wides2.py (both reads +-2 s, N8 and U8): S terms become consistent with the
  pick residuals (SRT 1.75, CCC 1.76, TOW2 1.95 s), the origin bias disappears (6.1), any-score LOC+T targets rise
  N8 5 -> 8 and U8 4 -> 6, LOC 10 -> 11 and 10 -> 14, but recall at 30/h is N8 0.000 and U8 0.022 (LOC+T) against
  BESTPICK 0.600 and 0.289; its perfect-ranking ceilings (0.178, 0.133) are still far below BESTPICK. This is a
  design limitation inherited from the synthetic studies (statics of 0.3 s sd), declared in substance in
  R0_DEV_LOG (time level 0.35-0.41 s not covered); it is not a coding error, and correcting it does not come close
  to reversing the negative.
  (c) Polarity: 6.3. (d) Frame/timing: 6.1, 6.2. (e) M2 path: M2 = coarse selection on the S2 engine (hard
  +-4-sample read), relocation on the 1 km lattice with the S2 prior, as documented; methods do not share state
  across calls (mt_loop copies MFn), and running M2 alone (this verification) or with M1/M5/M6 (original) gives
  identical recalls.

7. Defects and deviations found (none changes the registered decision)

  D1 (design, against the detector) The +SC estimator and the S2 first read search only +-1.0 s around the
  homogeneous prediction; on these data the S delays reach 1.7-1.9 s, so the station terms of several S phases are
  wrong by ~1-1.7 s and the pick-free origins are ~1 s late. Correcting it post hoc (6.7b) does not change the
  conclusion. The +SC comparator arms are also degraded by it (under LOC+T at 30/h PyOcto+SC is at or below PyOcto on every subnet), which does
  not matter because BESTPICK takes the raw arms.
  D2 (code vs registration, against the comparators) Tie-break by the associator's own score registered but not
  implemented; strict threshold drops integer ties (6.6).
  D3 (code vs registration, neutral) r0_analyze computes the false-event cap from 45 blocks x 60 s = 0.75 h, the
  registration from 44.33 min; identical at 10 and 30/h, cap 45 instead of 44 at 60/h (section 5).
  D4 (environment) The R0 environment needs numba and tqdm for GaMMA, which venv311 lacks and R0_FREEZE.txt does not
  pin; reproduced here with numba 0.60.0 / tqdm 4.66.5. PhaseNet/TensorFlow are not in R0_FREEZE.txt either
  (pinned only in real_picker.py docstring and data/README.md).
  D5 (scope) The descriptive origin bias of the pick-free detectors (6.1) means LOC+T penalises them more than LOC;
  but under LOC the contrast is -0.450 and the ceiling -0.306, so the rule choice is not decisive.
