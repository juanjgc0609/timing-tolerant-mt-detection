Task R1, amendment 1 (__FECHA__, wall clock), written after the development on CAL and before the freeze and the run; no detector, picker or associator has read EVAL samples (the development runs read the waveforms up to 880 s). Its SHA-256 is sent to the user in the chat before the run.
1. Alignment of the matched-filter terms (changes the registration). With the registered reading (+-2 s, joint P and S, gate 25) the P terms of ATKH, TSTH and YNZH came out +1.66, -1.70 and +1.60 s, incompatible with the pick terms of the same stations (-0.21, +0.17 and +0.23 s). At ATKH and YNZH (12 km, S-P 1.9 s) the +-2 s P window reaches the S arrival, which in these borehole records carries one hundred to one thousand times the P energy; at THTH and TSTH the P energy of the CAL events is near or below the gate. Correction, chosen by an alignment criterion and not by recall: readings searched within +-min(2 s, half the S-P time predicted by JMA2001) and matched-filter terms = pick terms + the lag of the energy peak behind the onset, one per phase, equal to the median over the station-phases with at least 3 readings above the gate of (median reading - pick term); on CAL 0.265 s for P and 0.244 s for S (r1_run.mf_terms). The terms of P1 thus depend on the CAL picks; the detection of P1 remains pick-free.
2. Model of the associators. PyOcto's VelocityModel1D does not accept the 0.25 km layer representation; PyOcto-1D and GaMMA-1D receive the JMA2001 (depth, VP) points down to 40 km, which both interpolate linearly, with VS = VP / 1.73; the pick-free engine and the hybrid use the 0.25 km layers of c18_tt. It is the same model.
3. Definitions the registration did not fix. Pick terms: hyb_dev2.pick_terms with minimum magnitude 1.0, the nearest pick within +-2 s, median when there are at least 2. BESTPICK in the McNemar tests: the configuration with the highest recall on EVAL (the first of the grid on ties) and its targets hit at its operating point; in the bootstrap the envelope is recomputed in each replicate. JMA-only reading: targets = truth events that coincide with a JMA event (1.0 s and 5 km); a detection hitting another truth event is not false. Deployable threshold: the score threshold of the CAL operating point (LOC+T, 30/h) applied to EVAL for HYB, BASE and P1. Run: PhaseNet once on the full record; terms from CAL only; BASE chosen on the CAL detections of that same run; P1 on CAL and EVAL; hybrid lists and analysis with r1_queue.sh.
4. Independent code audit of 17 October (E51 to E55 of the error log). For R1: reading at the floor sample (E52) shifts the centre of the peak window by half a sample, the same for every pick-free reading; it is not corrected so as not to change the engine of the registered results. The development readings on CAL are in-sample (E53); EVAL is out of sample. The +SC arms of the R0-style comparators subtract the peak-onset lag from onset picks (E54); they are descriptive.
5. Development readings on CAL (in-sample, 30 targets, LOC+T at 30 per hour): BASE = GaMMA-1D (min_picks 6, sigma 1.0), 16 of 30; HYB 16 of 30 (17 with the registered terms); P1 2 of 30. The highest-scoring atoms of P1 are ghosts of the S coda of the large events: 6 to 15 km to the south-west, at 3 km depth, 2.7 to 3.9 s after the origin.
