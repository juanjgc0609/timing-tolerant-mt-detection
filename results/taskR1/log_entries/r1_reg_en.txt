Task R1 (__FECHA__, wall-clock time), written before any detector, picker or associator reads the waveforms of the Hi-net swarm (only the file list, checksums, SAC header metadata, catalog.dat, DetectedFinal.dat, the README and the first lines of INPUT.in have been opened). Its SHA-256 is delivered to the user through the chat. Author decisions of 17 October 2026: (1) ground truth with both catalogues, (2) a published 1-D model of the region, (3) horizontal components as recorded, (4) the decision rule, which the author asked to validate; the validation changed (1) and (4) as stated below.
Origin. The gate of 16 October was met on the Ridgecrest development segment (C18j: layered medium, station terms read over +-2 s, 2 Hz, MT-FW without cap, 4 and 5 of 16 events). On the same segment (development only, hyb/hyb_dev2.py, hyb/hyb_comp.py) the pick-based comparators given the same layered model and pick-based station terms recalled 13 of 16 on N8 and U8 at 30 false detections per hour with origin time, far above the pick-free detector, and a hybrid that adds pick-free candidates to the best associator and scores every candidate by the PhaseNet picks it explains recalled 14 and 13 of 16 (and 12 against 10 of 16 on U8 at 10 per hour). R1 tests that hybrid, not the pick-free detector alone, against the associator it extends.
Question. On unseen data, does adding moment-tensor pick-free candidates to a pick-based associator, with every candidate scored by the PhaseNet picks it explains, recall more catalogued events at the same false-detection rate than the associator alone, both given the same velocity model and station terms?
Data. Geolab-USTC/MatchLocate2, commit bac5ccb00816a4ec0aaab215ea1c342c730d412f, example/: continuous 100 Hz three-component Hi-net records of 7 stations (ATKH, INWH, NAZH, ONIH, THTH, TSTH, YNZH), 2012-09-02 03:20:00 to 03:53:20 UTC, Fukushima-Yamagata swarm; sha256 in data/checksums/hinet_matchlocate2_example.sha256. Catalogues: 14 JMA events (catalog.dat, M 2.1 to 3.2) and 140 Match&Locate detections (MultipleTemplate/DetectedFinal.dat, relative magnitude -0.41 to 3.2, including the JMA events), with location and origin time.
Segments. CAL = origins before 03:34:00 (840 s; development, station terms, choice of the associator configuration); EVAL = origins from 03:34:00 to 03:53:20 minus 40 s at the end. Development before the freeze only on CAL (units, polarity, alignment, cost, that the methods find CAL events); no constant is tuned for recall except the registered choice of the associator configuration below.
Truth and targets (decision 1, revised by the validation). Truth list = the Match&Locate detections (they contain the JMA events). Targets = truth events in EVAL with relative magnitude >= 0.5 (about 55 events by the catalogue alone); a detection that hits only a truth event of magnitude < 0.5 is neutral; one that hits nothing is false. The JMA-only reading (10 EVAL events) is descriptive: the simulation below shows that with 9 to 14 events no rule has a false-positive rate within 2.5 % and useful power, so it cannot be primary. Limitation declared: the Match&Locate list comes from template matching with the 14 JMA templates and favours events similar to them.
Velocity model (decision 2). JMA2001 P velocities from the 28-point approximation published by Y. Maeda (Nagoya University opentools documentation, create_1DvelocityStructure_JMA2001_Vp): depth (km) / VP (km/s) 0.0 4.800, 1.5 5.220, 2.5 5.450, 4.0 5.720, 5.0 5.820, 15.0 6.220, 22.0 6.544, 34.0 7.188, 41.0 7.494, 44.0 7.598, 48.0 7.688, 52.5 7.750, 65.0 7.845; linear between points, represented by layers of 0.25 km; VS = VP / 1.73 (JMA2001 S values not obtained; declared). The same model for every method: first-arrival tables of c18_tt for the pick-free engine and the hybrid, PyOcto VelocityModel1D and the GaMMA eikonal model for the associators.
Components (decision 3). E, N, U as recorded (Hi-net borehole sensors; orientation errors declared as a limitation), detector frame (E, N, -U); stations at the SAC coordinates, elevations and borehole depths ignored.
Preprocessing as R0: counts (no response), demean, detrend, zero-phase band-pass 0.5-6 Hz order 4, resampling 100 -> 16 Hz for the pick-free engine, per-station scale by the robust deviation over CAL; PhaseNet on the 100 Hz counts (original model, threshold 0.3, as R0). Frame centre = mean of the CAL JMA epicentres. Pick-free grid and windows as R0 (coarse grid, depths 3, 7, 11 km; 40 s windows every 3 s owning origins in [5, 8) s; kmax 6; de-duplication 5 km / 1 s).
Station terms. Matched-filter terms (pick-free engine): c18j_layered.corrections over the CAL truth events with magnitude >= 1.0, search +-2 s, energy gate 25. Pick terms (associators and hybrid): hyb_dev2.pick_terms over the same events, search +-2 s; picks given to the associators are corrected by them.
Methods. BASE = the associator configuration with the largest CAL recall at 30 false per hour (LOC+T) over the grids of Task 2A of PyOcto-1D and GaMMA-1D on the corrected picks (ties: fewer CAL detections, then PyOcto, then the first of the grid). HYB = hyb_dev2 PICKSCORE-UNION: candidates = BASE events and P1 atoms; every candidate scored by the number of PhaseNet picks within 0.5 s of its predicted arrivals (layered times plus pick terms; each pick supports one candidate, BASE events claim first), plus 0.5 times the percentile of its waveform score; candidates within 5 km and 1.5 s of a higher-scored one removed. P1 = MT-FW without cap with the layered engine and matched-filter terms (C18j). BESTPICK = envelope over every configuration of both associators (with the same model and terms), as in R0 (optimistic for the comparators). R0-style comparators (homogeneous VP 6.0, uncorrected picks) descriptive.
Endpoint. Recall of the targets at <= 30 false detections per hour of EVAL, exact FROC, operating point per method on EVAL (as R0); LOC+T (epicentre within 5 km and origin within 0.5 s of a truth event) primary, LOC (5 km, 3.0 s) secondary; 10 and 60 per hour secondary; deployable threshold fixed on CAL secondary.
Rules (decision 4, set by validation, hyb/sim_r1_rules.py, hyb/sim_r1_rules_out.txt). The provisional margin of -0.20 was not justified: a percentile bootstrap with 9 events gives a false-positive rate of 5 to 13 % for that non-inferiority rule, and margins are a scientific choice the data cannot fix. A superiority rule needs no margin. Simulated with the target counts of this design, an exact one-sided McNemar test on the discordant target events (hit by one method and not the other) at 0.025 keeps the false-positive rate at 0.000 to 0.018 when the true difference is 0, with power 0.13 to 0.17 at +0.10 and 0.49 to 0.69 at +0.20 for 40 targets, and 0.35 to 0.53 at +0.10 and 0.92 to 0.99 at +0.20 for 100 targets; the percentile bootstrap of the paired difference has a false-positive rate of 0.020 to 0.025 at 40 to 100 targets but up to 0.034 with 9 to 14. R1-a (primary): HYB - BASE, LOC+T, 30/h: exact one-sided McNemar p <= 0.025 on the discordant targets and point difference > 0. R1-b (secondary): HYB - BESTPICK, the same test. R1-c (descriptive): P1 - BASE, the JMA-only reading, 60 s block-bootstrap intervals of every contrast (2000 replicates), the 10 and 60 per hour readings, LOC, the deployable threshold.
Declared prediction (from development): a small gain of HYB over BASE, of the order of one or two events in sixteen, so the power of R1-a is low; a negative R1-a is the more likely outcome and does not by itself refute the hybrid. P1 below BASE.
Interpretation. R1-a met -> pick-free candidates add detections to a pick-based associator on unseen real data at equal false-detection rate (one swarm, seven stations). Not met -> no demonstrated gain; reported as such, with the discordant events listed. In either case P1 alone is expected below BASE, and that is reported.
Freeze and run: code r1_*.py and hyb_*.py frozen with sha256 after development on CAL and an amendment recorded before the freeze; single run on EVAL; analysis with r1_analyze.py; independent verification by a separate agent.
Declared limits: one swarm, 33 min 20 s, 7 borehole stations with orientation errors, no instrument response, prefiltered data; truth list derived from template matching; operating point chosen on EVAL for every method (as R0); VS from VP / 1.73; the hybrid design was selected on 16 events of another sequence.
