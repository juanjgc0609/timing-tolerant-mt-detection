and two thirds in DENSE).

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SET-VALUED ARGMAX HYPOTHESIS: REFUTED
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
With the hard rule, the statistic of a hypothesis depends only on the samples read; if shifting the origin does not
change any reading, the statistic is identical and np.argmax returns the earliest origin of the plateau. Measured:
the maximum is unique in 97.8% of the accepted atoms (mean plateau size 1.023). The exact plateau does not explain the
error. Reporting the plateau midpoint (MID) does not change the recall (differences +0.000).

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. SECOND-STAGE RELOCATION FROM THE READINGS (without changing the detection)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Estimators (they only replace the atom's (x, tau); scores, exclusion and deflation untouched):
  LSO   weighted median of the delays read at the node; LSOs with sub-sample parabolic interpolation
  LSX   robust (Huber) Gauss-Newton step in (x, y, z, tau) with a prior centred on the node
  EDT   equal differential time likelihood (Font et al. 2004; Lomax 2005), independent of the origin and
        robust to outlying readings, on a +-6 km (0.5 km) grid and depths 1-17 km (1 km), sigma 0.2 s
  EDT2  EDT, re-reading of the energy peaks within +-0.25 s of the times predicted at the new location, EDT again
        (2 rounds)
Paired differences against BASE, LOC+T recall with <= 0.25 false events per window [95%], hierarchical bootstrap B = 100:

  LSOs    +0.028 [-0.012, +0.079]     +0.003 [-0.008, +0.021]
  LSX     +0.042 [-0.005, +0.075]     +0.005 [-0.017, +0.037]

Under LOC none of them changes anything (-0.009 to +0.007). No interval excludes zero, and these are development
figures (C14 showed shrinkages of 3 to 6 times when moving to new networks). Reason: with 8-16 readings per atom, about
half of which are noise or another event, the location cannot be refined; with the true location the origin comes out
right because it is a single parameter and the median absorbs the outliers.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. CYCLIC BLOCK RE-ESTIMATION (NOMP-type feedback; Mamandipoor, Ramasamy and Madhow 2016)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
After the greedy selection, each atom is returned to the residual and (node, origin) is chosen again with the others
deflated, outside 5 km of the others; 1, 2 or 4 cycles; score of the last cycle (CYC) or the greedy one (CYC2g).
Networks 1800 and 1802 complete (1801 was stopped halfway because the result was null; 13 parts of 18).
  LOC+T <= 0.25:  STD  CYC1 -0.032 [-0.105,+0.034]  CYC2 -0.064 [-0.109,+0.044]  CYC2g -0.048  CYC4 -0.043
                  DENSE CYC1 +0.022 [-0.009,+0.050]  CYC2 +0.011  CYC2g -0.003  CYC4 +0.001
  LOC <= 0.25:    STD  -0.037 to -0.085;  DENSE -0.017 to -0.033 (CYC2g -0.026 [-0.067,-0.003])
Cost: about 3-5 times the greedy detector. Verdict: NULL or NEGATIVE; when the competition is removed, low-score false
atoms rise in score.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. VERDICT AND CONSEQUENCE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NULL for recall. The useful piece is diagnostic: in this generator the origin error of the well-located atoms is
a LOCATION error (not a reading error, nor a model error, nor the argmax plateau), and the readings of a single atom in
a network of 4-8 stations carry no information to correct it. Together with C9 (Cramer-Rao bounds: the time channel is
the only one that locates), C10 (the remaining margin is calibration and amplitude), C12-C14 (DC statistic, decoys,
self-interference), this indicates that the detector is close to what the information of this generator allows and that
algorithmic refinements within the same benchmark yield few points. No confirmation is proposed.
What would change the evidence is leaving the generator: real noise with injected events (second noise model and
real geometry) and the test with real data.
