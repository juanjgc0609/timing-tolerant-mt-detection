0. SUMMARY AND VERDICT
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(1) NO decoy gives valid control in ALL cells with both hit rules and with useful power. The
    result depends on the hit rule and on the construction of the decoy.
(2) In windows WITHOUT events all well-constructed decoys are exchangeable with the target (D1p, D2s, D2r, D3c:
    permutation p between 0.04 and 1.0 in the 6 strata, only one of 48 below 0.05; tail ratio
    0.75-1.33). D3 and D1o, as they were fixed, are NOT exchangeable in strong clutter (D3: p 0.001-0.011 and 1.5-1.75
    times more targets than decoys in the tail): a defect of the circular delay over the whole trace, corrected in D3c.
(3) In windows WITH events the false detections of the target are not "clutter": they are generated by the events
    themselves. Against the twin of the same window without events (same clutter and noise), the number of false atoms
    per window above the threshold t95 is 12 times larger in STD [9, 18] and 45 times larger in DENSE [33, 63]; at
    more than 10 km from any event, 6.7 [4.9, 10.3] and 24 [17, 37] times. Hence a decoy only works if it preserves the
    energy of the events within the samples read and reproduces partial coincidences between stations.
(4) LOC rule: D1p (permuted stations) controls. tdc with D1p, realised FDR in the pooled lines (batch 120 /
    batch 20): q = 0.05 -> 0.02-0.05 / 0.00-0.02; q = 0.1 -> 0.07-0.10 / 0.02-0.08; q = 0.2 -> 0.14-0.16 / 0.13-0.16.
    No individual stratum violates q = 0.2. Caveat: the cell unilateral_c40 lies above the nominal level at q <= 0.1
    with batches of 120 (DENSE: 0.10 [0.07, 0.16] at q = 0.05 and 0.15 [0.11, 0.19] at q = 0.1); with batches of 20,
    0.04 and 0.12. D2r also controls under LOC with somewhat less recall; D2s controls but accepts almost nothing; D3c
    stays at 1.3-2 times the nominal level (0.10 / 0.16-0.18 / 0.21-0.26); D3 and D1o fail (0.26-0.53).
(5) LOC+T rule (the primary one of C10-C11): only D2s controls (pooled FDR 0.00-0.10 at q = 0.05-0.2; the
    unilateral cell in DENSE reaches 0.26 [0.14, 0.41] at q = 0.2) and at the cost of almost all recall. D1p fails:
    0.08-0.19 at q = 0.05, 0.18-0.29 at q = 0.1, 0.28-0.37 at q = 0.2 (batch 120), and worse in the unilateral cell
    (0.34-0.43 in DENSE). Reason: a real event located within <= 5 km but with a wrong origin (> 0.5 s) counts as a
    false event, and no moveout decoy imitates it; there are 0.2-1.2 such atoms per window with events, depending on
    the stratum.
(6) Recall cost of the decoy threshold against the oracle threshold with the SAME realised false-event rate: null
    with batches of 120 windows (D1p, LOC: loss between -0.02 and +0.04 in the pooled lines, intervals that
    contain 0); with batches of 20 windows in STD, +0.04 to +0.08 at q <= 0.1 because 88% of the batches accept nothing.
    The real cost is in the level: at q = 0.1 (LOC, batch 120) the recall is 0.195 in STD and 0.152 in DENSE, against
    0.436 and 0.253 at the benchmark point (<= 0.25 false events per window), whose FDP is 0.41 and 0.20. The
    benchmark operating points are NOT "false-positive-free" points: 13-41% (LOC) or 31-52% (LOC+T) of what they
    accept is false.
(7) Deployable operating point equivalent to the benchmark one (fpw with D1p, LOC, batch 120): realised rate of
    false events per window 0.09 [0.07, 0.12] (STD) and 0.12 [0.09, 0.16] (DENSE) for c = 0.1; 0.21 [0.17, 0.25] and
    0.23 [0.19, 0.28] for c = 0.25; recall 0.355 / 0.181 and 0.453 / 0.245, that is, the oracle's (0.331 / 0.161 and
    0.436 / 0.253) within +-0.03. Under LOC+T the same rule lets through 1.3-3 times the nominal rate; D2s meets it
    (0.05-0.06 for 0.1; 0.12-0.14 for 0.25) with a recall loss of 0.02-0.05 against the oracle.
(8) Deflation: yes. In windows with ONE event already hit and deflated, the subsequent false atoms at more than 10 km
    are 1.9 [1.6, 2.4] times (t80) and 5.0 [3.1, 8.8] times (t95) those of the event-free twin. D3 does not predict
    this; D1p and D3c over-predict at t80 (ratio false events / decoys 0.67-1.06) because they see the event undeflated.
(9) bh (conformal p-value + Benjamini-Hochberg) = tdc, number for number. comp (paired competition by rank) is
    invalid with any decoy (D1p, LOC, q = 0.1: 0.23-0.32 against 0.07-0.10 for tdc): atom r of the decoy
    is not the null hypothesis of atom r of the target.
Verdict: PROMISING WITH CONDITIONS. There is a deployable and cheap calibration (one extra pass of the detector with
the stations permuted) that reproduces the benchmark operating point and controls the FDR at q = 0.2 under the LOC
rule in the three cells, in standard and dense windows, with thin and strong clutter. There is NO theoretical
guarantee (exchangeability only holds without events), it does NOT hold for the LOC+T rule, it falls short in the
unilateral geometry at q <= 0.1, and the goal "no false positives and no false negatives" remains far away: with FDR
0.05 the recall is 0.09-0.10. Sections 9-11.

1. EXPERIMENT PLAN (code: c13_lib.py, c13_run.py, c13_run_d3c.py, c13_twin.py; analysis c13_analyze.py)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Idea. The detector is run twice on each window: once with the real hypotheses (TARGET) and once with DECOY
hypotheses that cannot correspond to a real source. If the false detections of the target behave like those of the
decoy, the number of decoys above a threshold estimates the number of false target detections above that threshold,
without labels. Each window receives a new draw of each decoy (own stream, TAG 1405). Decoys:
  D1p  moveout with permuted stations: the time table of station i is that of station pi(i), pi a random
       derangement (no fixed point); the station term follows the physical station; amplitude operators
       (G, Q) unchanged, so that the Gram structure per node is the same.
  D1o  moveout with offsets: each column (station, phase) of the sample table is shifted by its own circular
       offset over the 40 s of trace, offsets separated by at least 1 s from one another; G, Q unchanged.
  D2s  sign-flipped templates: half of the 3-component (station, phase) blocks change sign in G
       and Q; times unchanged.
  D2r  templates with scrambled polarisation: one random 3D rotation per station applied to the blocks of both phases
       in G and Q; times unchanged. (Time reversal of the template is not a decoy here: the detector reads
       traces filtered with a symmetric wavelet.)
  D3   data surrogate: each station trace is circularly shifted by its own random delay over the whole
       trace (delays separated by at least 3 s); hypotheses unchanged. Equivalent to D1o with one offset per station
       (it preserves the P-S pair of each station).
  D3c  D3 with delays restricted to [-7.5 s, +3.5 s] and separated by at least 1 s (POST HOC, see sections 2 and 4).
Threshold calibrations over a batch of windows (targets and decoys pooled within the batch):
  tdc  separate search: targets with record >= t* are accepted, t* the smallest t with
       (1 + #decoys >= t) / max(1, #targets >= t) <= q.
  bh   conformal p-value p = (1 + #decoys >= s) / (n_decoys + 1) of each target atom and Benjamini-Hochberg at
       level q over the m target atoms of the batch. Since n_decoys = m, it is algebraically tdc with the factor
       m / (m + 1); it was computed and gives the same numbers as tdc in all lines (table TH).
  comp paired competition by rank (knockoff+): atom r of the target against atom r of the decoy of the same window.
  fpw  (added during the run, section 2) decoy-based estimate of the false events per window: threshold =
       decoy score exceeded by at most c x (windows of the batch) decoys; it is the deployable version of the
       benchmark operating point (<= c false events per window).
  Levels: q = 0.05, 0.1, 0.2 (and 0.3, 0.5 as reference); c = 0.1, 0.25.
Batches: 20 windows = the 20 windows of one type from one network (72 batches per window type); 120 windows = 6
networks of the same cell and regime (random partitions of the 12 networks into two groups; point estimate averaged
over 20 partitions). Limitation: the batch of 120 mixes 6 network geometries of the same cell, they are not 120
windows of one network.
Metrics: FDP of a batch = false accepted atoms / max(1, accepted); realised FDR = mean of the FDP over batches
(a batch that accepts nothing counts 0); realised false events per window; recall = distinct events hit /
events; "oracle" = the best single threshold chosen with the labels on the same windows with at most the same
number of false events (for fpw, also the benchmark point at <= c). A duplicate of an event already hit counts
neither as a false event nor as a new hit (as in the exact FROC).
Uncertainty: hierarchical bootstrap (networks within cell x regime, then windows within network; 400 replicates;
thresholds, batches and oracle point recomputed in each replicate), 95% percentile intervals. The exchangeability
p-values are obtained by permuting, window by window, the target and decoy lists (1000 permutations).

9. JUDGEMENT
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Answers to the questions of the assignment.
  (a) Does any decoy give valid control in ALL cells, including the dense ones with strong clutter? With the LOC rule,
      yes at q = 0.2: D1p (FDR 0.02-0.24 per stratum, 0.14-0.16 pooled, no mark) and D2r; at q = 0.05-0.1, D1p
      meets the level in the ladder cells and falls short in the unilateral one with batches of 120 (up to 2 times the nominal). With the
      LOC+T rule, no: only D2s, which is valid because it is very conservative (its decoys feed on the real events
      with the correct time) and leaves no recall with tdc (0.00-0.02 at q = 0.1). Dense + strong is not the worst case;
      the unilateral geometry is.
  (b) How much recall is lost against the oracle threshold? With the same realised false-event rate, nothing measurable with batches of
      120 windows and 0.04-0.08 in STD with batches of 20 at q <= 0.1. Against the benchmark point, FDR control at
      0.1 costs more than half of the recall in STD (0.195 versus 0.436) because the benchmark operates with 41% of
      false detections; fpw with D1p reproduces the benchmark point without labels and without loss under LOC.
  (c) Does deflation after strong events inflate the false detections more than the decoys predict? It inflates them (1.9 to 5 times
      those of the twin, far from the already deflated event), and an incoherent decoy without event energy (D3, D1o)
      underestimates it by a large factor. D1p and D3c, which see the energy without deflating, do not underestimate it at moderate
      thresholds; in the tail D3c does (FDR 1.3-2 times the nominal) and D1p only in the unilateral cell.
Limitations that must travel with any number.
  (i)   No guarantee. Exchangeability is demonstrated without events; with events D1p works because the
        partial coincidences of a permuted moveout resemble the real lobes, and that is empirical and depends on
        the geometry (it fails earlier in the unilateral network of 8 stations, where a derangement preserves less structure).
  (ii)  It depends on the hit rule. Under LOC+T, the primary rule of the recent registered tasks, the
        estimator lets through 1.3-3 times the nominal. A well-located event with the wrong origin is not a "false
        discovery" in the sense of a null without an event; no moveout decoy is going to count it.
  (iii) One seed, 12 networks per cell and regime, 20 windows per network. The batches of 120 mix 6 networks of the same
        cell; a batch of 120 windows from a single network was not tested.
  (iv)  The detector is hard MT-FW with +SC terms; it was not tested with T3 or with the JHD terms of C11, or with BOMP-PK.
  (v)   Event rate: STD has 0.82 events per window and DENSE 4. With sparser seismicity the batch needed to
        accept anything grows (with 20 STD windows nothing is accepted any more at q = 0.05).
  (vi)  D3 and D1o, as they were fixed before seeing data, had a construction defect; D3c is post hoc and D1o was not
        corrected. The conclusion "incoherent decoys underestimate" rests on D3c (1.3-2 times the nominal), not on
        the much worse numbers of D3 and D1o.
  (vii) The percentile intervals of the FDR are biased upwards; the standard-error ones are also given.
  (viii) Uncorrected multiplicity; the choice of D1p as candidate is made on these same data.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. NOVELTY
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Not new: target-decoy competition (proteomics), knockoffs, conformal p-values with BH, nor the use of
permutations or time shifts between stations to estimate chance coincidences (common practice in
phase association and in coincidence detectors). What would be new if confirmed: (a) a label-free operating point
for a pick-free moment-tensor subspace detector, calibrated with permuted-moveout
hypotheses, with its validity measured cell by cell alongside the oracle point; (b) the demonstration with twins that the
false detections in windows with events are generated by the events (including the deflation residual) and not by the clutter, and that
for that reason the surrogates that destroy coherence underestimate; (c) the explicit limit of the idea under a hit rule
with origin time. It is not a new detector; it is a calibration rule and its domain of validity.

11. PROPOSED FROZEN CONFIGURATION AND CRITERIA FOR A PREREGISTERED CONFIRMATION (none of this has been run)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Only what works here is proposed: LOC rule, decoy D1p. For LOC+T no validity claim is proposed.
Configuration C13-F (no free constants; code c13_lib.py as it stands):
  detector   MT-FW cap 6, +SC, hard mode (the registered one), kmax as in the benchmark;
  decoy      D1p, one draw per window from its own stream, same kmax, record = cumulative minimum;
  batch      120 windows of the SAME network and window type (in C13 they were 6 networks x 20);
  rules      fpw with c = 0.25 (primary) and c = 0.1; tdc with q = 0.2 (primary) and q = 0.1;
  hit        LOC for the claims; LOC+T is reported without a rule, together with fpw-D2s as a conservative alternative.
Data: two never-used seeds (to be chosen and checked with grep before registering; those of drafts C9-C11 are already
compromised), the three cells, thin and strong regimes, >= 20 networks per cell and regime with 120 STD windows and
120 DENSE per network from new TAGs; hierarchical bootstrap (networks, then windows; 2000 replicates) and, for the FDR, also
mean +- standard error across networks.
Criteria (each in BOTH seeds, pooling cells and regimes, per window type):
  2M-a rate validity: realised false events per window of fpw-D1p with c = 0.25, one-sided 95% upper bound
       <= 0.3125 (1.25 c). Pilot: STD 0.207 [0.174, 0.250], DENSE 0.227 [0.192, 0.276].
  2M-b FDR validity: tdc-D1p with q = 0.2, one-sided 95% upper bound <= 0.25. Pilot: STD 0.14 [0.11, 0.23],
       DENSE 0.16 [0.13, 0.20] (percentile); 0.13 [0.09, 0.17] and 0.16 [0.13, 0.19] by standard error with batches of 20.
  2M-c cost: recall of fpw-D1p (c = 0.25) minus recall of the oracle at <= 0.25 false events per window, one-sided 95%
       lower bound > -0.03. Pilot: +0.017 (STD) and -0.008 (DENSE), with intervals of +-0.03.
  2M-d per-cell guard (descriptive with threshold): no cell with point realised rate > 1.5 c or point FDR
       > 1.5 q; if the unilateral cell violates it, the claim is limited to the ladder cells. Pilot: unilateral
       DENSE 0.28 for c = 0.25 (1.1 c) and 0.18 for q = 0.2; at c = 0.1 and q = 0.1, 0.18 and 0.15 (1.8 c and 1.5 q): that is why the
       primary levels are 0.25 and 0.2 and those of 0.1 remain as secondary without a rule.
  Claim permitted if a, b, c are met: "a decoy pass with permuted stations fixes without labels the operating
  point of the detector at the nominal false-event rate and without loss of recall, under the location rule". No
  claim of "guarantee" or of validity under LOC+T is registered.
Approximate power (bootstrap SD of the pilot, 12 networks per stratum, scaled with the square root of networks): 2M-a, SD 0.019-0.021
  in the pilot and 0.015-0.016 with 20 networks: it is met with probability > 0.95 if the true rate is <= 0.28 and fails
  with probability > 0.8 if it is >= 0.33. 2M-b, SD 0.02-0.03: it is met with > 0.9 if the true FDR is <= 0.20. 2M-c,
  SD 0.015-0.02: it is met with > 0.9 if the true loss is <= 0.
Estimated cost: 2 passes per window, 0.2-0.45 s per window; 2 seeds x 120 networks x 240 windows = 57600
windows, 4-6 h of CPU.
Assessment of the draft: registrable only if the thesis accepts LOC as the rule of the claim. If the primary rule
remains LOC+T, this result serves as a diagnostic (section 5) and as a conservative alternative (fpw-D2s), not
as a contribution.
