0. SUMMARY AND VERDICT
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(1) G1-R (bounded influence) SURVIVES with real noise, and becomes stronger. Dense windows, LOC rule, <= 0.25
    false events per window, REAL arm:
        MT@S0 (cap 6) - BOMP@S0         +0.259 [+0.131, +0.321]   (recall 0.314 versus 0.055)
        HUBER-N@S0 - BOMP@S0            +0.294 [+0.178, +0.355]   (0.349 versus 0.055)
        HUBER-N@S0 - MT@S0              +0.035 [-0.020, +0.097]   (non-inferior; 2I-b required bound > -0.03)
    LOC+T: +0.085 [+0.030, +0.124] and +0.133 [+0.082, +0.174]. At <= 0.1, LOC: +0.183 [+0.134, +0.248] and +0.233
    [+0.144, +0.293]. In the GEN arm (same geometry and signals, Gaussian noise + thin clutter): +0.069 [-0.009,
    +0.158] and +0.113 [+0.052, +0.199]. REAL - GEN difference of the contrast (joint bootstrap): +0.190 [+0.037,
    +0.276] and +0.181 [+0.037, +0.264]. Mechanism: BOMP-PK (uncapped energies) collapses with real noise (recall
    DENSE LOC 0.312 -> 0.055; STD LOC 0.813 -> 0.248), because the transients of real noise (excess kurtosis of the
    filtered noise: median 3.0, 90th percentile = 42; max |MF| > 10 standard deviations in 41% of the windows) give it
    false atoms with huge scores (99th percentile of the first atom in windows without events 7536 versus 33 with
    generator noise). MT-FW with cap 6 and HUBER-N, whose influence per station and phase is bounded, lose much less.
    This is exactly the property that G1-R asserts, now with a type of contamination (real transients) that the
    generator did not have.
(2) G1-C (calibration) IS NOT CONTRADICTED BUT IS NOT REPRODUCED at the resolution of this sample. Standard windows,
    LOC+T, <= 0.25, REAL arm:
        MT@S2+T3 - MT@S0+T3             +0.049 [-0.083, +0.149]   (0.464 -> 0.513; bootstrap sd 0.060)
        oracle MT@ORA+T3 - MT@S0+T3     +0.095 [-0.035, +0.205]
    GEN arm: +0.023 [-0.058, +0.106], oracle +0.023 [-0.075, +0.135]; REAL - GEN of the contrast +0.026 [-0.134,
    +0.154]. DENSE LOC+T: REAL +0.019 [-0.029, +0.059] (oracle +0.067 [+0.016, +0.119]); GEN -0.033 [-0.081, +0.044].
    Under LOC in STD the contrast is -0.037 [-0.095, +0.048] (REAL). The registered sign (+) is preserved in the REAL arm
    in STD and DENSE, but every interval includes 0: with 36 networks x 12 windows the deviation of the contrast (0.060) is
    five times that of 2L (0.012), and the registered effect (+0.06) cannot be resolved. What is seen: (a) S2 improves
    the terms with real noise as with the generator (median rms error S0 0.153 -> S2 0.080 s REAL; 0.115 -> 0.052
    GEN; better than S0 in 83% and 92% of the networks); (b) in this real geometry (stations at 5-31 km from the
    sources, not at 10-45) the margin of the oracle over S0+T3 in STD is small even with generator noise
    (+0.023), so that the geometry, not the noise, limits what G1-C can deliver here.
(3) TRANSFER OF THE OPERATING POINT. With the threshold fixed on the windows with events of the REAL arm, the realised
    false events in real-noise windows WITHOUT events are: MT family 0.000 (DENSE, LOC), 0.007-0.009 (STD, LOC+T, at
    <= 0.25) and 0.014-0.023 (STD, LOC, at <= 0.25), far below the nominal budget (the budget of the operating
    point is spent by detections induced by the events, as in C14); BOMP@S0 0.12-0.20 and HUBER-N@S0 0.03-0.10
    per window, which are pure-noise detections. With the threshold fixed on the SYNTHETIC benchmark (GEN arm) and
    applied to real noise (X): MT 0.000 (DENSE), 0.014-0.060 (STD LOC+T at 0.25), 0.13-0.19 (STD LOC at 0.25); HUBER-N 0.06-0.29; BOMP 0.29-0.86 per
    window. With generator noise the windows without events give 0.000 for all methods. The operating point
    of the capped detector transfers to real noise reasonably (the excess is <= 0.06 per window with the primary
    rule); that of BOMP-PK does not transfer (noise false events of 0.3 to 0.9 per window at a nominal level of 0.25).
(4) The signals are the same and their per-arrival SNR with real noise is almost that of the generator noise (paired
    ratio REAL / GEN median 0.96, quartiles 0.88-1.08, 5-95% 0.73-1.49): the amplitude rule worked. Even so the
    recall drops for all methods with real noise (MT@S0 DENSE LOC 0.381 -> 0.314, -0.067 [-0.126, -0.001]; STD LOC 0.810 ->
    0.666), which measures the cost of the non-Gaussianity and non-stationarity of real noise at equal in-band level.
VERDICT: the G1-R ordering survives the second noise model and the real geometry (and real noise
reinforces it, through a mechanism that is that of the hypothesis itself); the G1-C ordering keeps the sign but can be
neither asserted nor denied with this sample (intervals that include 0, and a small oracle margin in this geometry).
The operating point of the capped detector transfers to real noise with a small excess; that of BOMP-PK does not.

1. REAL DATA AND THEIR TREATMENT (c16_data.py, executed with python -I; output out/c16_noise/)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Data: /home/claude/realdata/pympa37_altotiberina_100723 (treated as untrusted data; read-only with obspy
1.5.1): 24 h of 2010-07-23, 14 three-component stations of the IV network (ATBU, ATCA, ATCC, ATFO, ATLO, ATPC,
ATPI, ATVO, BADI, FRON, MURB, PARC, PIEI, SSFR; EH* or HH*), 25 Hz, without gaps (checked: 0 samples filled in
the 42 channels). Coordinates from inv.IV (StationXML, station level; epoch active that day; ATCA and PIEI are not in
stations.list). Catalogue: the 32 rows of templates.zmap for that day (M -0.8 to 1.9, almost all from the cluster at 43.315 N,
12.388 E at 3 km) and the 8 detections of 200.100723.cat (one, 22:32:10, is not in templates.zmap): 40 origins.
Treatment, fixed before any detection:
  (a) per channel: mean and linear trend removed; Butterworth high-pass 0.5 Hz (order 4) and anti-alias low-pass 6.5 Hz
      (order 8), both zero-phase at 25 Hz; resampling 25 -> 16 Hz (= 1/DT of the project, task0_common) with
      scipy.signal.resample_poly(16, 25). The analysis band of the project is that of the matched filter (Ricker of 2 Hz,
      xc.matched / task1o_consistent._KER; useful energy between ~0.7 and ~4 Hz) and the generator pulse (Ricker of 1.4 to
      2.8 Hz); 0.5-6.5 Hz contains it entirely. The high-pass removes the microseism (0.1-0.3 Hz), which the matched filter already
      attenuates strongly but not completely at broadband stations.
  (b) components to the generator frame: x = East, y = North, z = DOWN (-Z).
  (c) coordinates: flat earth around 43.315 N, 12.388 E and shifted by the centroid of the 14 stations
      (fixed shift), so that the source box of the generator ([-15, 15]^2 km, 5-13 km depth) and the
      detection grid (+-21 km) lie inside the real network. The stations lie between 5 and 31 km from the origin
      (in the synthetic generator, 10-45 km). Elevations IGNORED (z = 0 for all, like the generator stations),
      consistently in signal, catalogue and detectors.
  (d) slots of 40 s (640 samples = NT of the project) starting at 0, 40, 80 ... s. Eligible = does not overlap
      [t - 60 s, t + 60 s] of any catalogued origin and is more than 60 s from the ends of the day: 2037 of 2160.
  (e) AMPLITUDE RULE (one, fixed before any detection): per channel, s_ch = median over the 2037 eligible
      slots of the robust in-band level of the slot (median |MF| / 0.6745, MF = the matched filter of the project);
      the channel is multiplied by SD_GEN / s_ch, SD_GEN = the in-band level of the generator white noise (0.14 per
      sample). Thus the TYPICAL level of each channel over the day is that of the generator; the variations of the
      level over the day, the transients, the spectral colour within the band, the non-Gaussianity and the
      correlation between components are preserved, and the gain differences between instruments are lost (in counts; without
      instrument response they are not comparable). Relative level of the slots with respect to the median of their channel:
      quantiles 5/25/50/75/95/99 % = 0.41 / 0.78 / 1.00 / 1.24 / 2.34 / 6.35.
  No synthetic clutter or Gaussian noise is added in the REAL arm.

2. DESIGN (c16_lib.py, c16_run.py, c16_analyze.py; all fixed before the freeze)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Signal. Events generated exactly as in the project (c8_lib.generate_site = gen_realistic.generate_real, literal
logic, split into two functions: c16_lib.gen_signal = signal + coda up to the end of the event loop;
c16_lib.add_gen_noise = clutter + Gaussian noise in the same order): same sources (xc.draw_sources for STD, 0-2
events, kmax 5; c2_run.draw_dense for DENSE, 3-5 events within 9 km, kmax 7), random double pairs, Ricker pulse
1.4-2.8 Hz with phase rotation, medium with gradient, coda, lognormal site factors (0.5, drawn per window),
polarisation deviation of 10 degrees, statics N(0, 0.3 s) per station and phase fixed per network.
Arms, on the SAME networks and the SAME signals (paired):
  REAL  signal + real-noise segment (no clutter, no Gaussian noise). The clutter and noise draws of the
        generator are consumed and discarded, so that the sources of each subsequent window and of each catalogue
        candidate are the same as in GEN.
  GEN   signal + clutter of the thin regime (amplitude 0.35; 35 spikes per window in ladder, 40 in unilateral) + generator
        Gaussian noise = the standard chain of the project on the real geometry (bit-for-bit identical to
        c8_lib.generate_site; section 3).
Networks (rules fixed before any detection; subset stream [999, 5165, ci, j]):
  ladder_4 / ladder_6   uniform subset of 4 / 6 of the 14 stations.
  unilateral_c40        one-sided coverage: direction theta ~ U(0, 2 pi) and the 8 stations whose azimuth (seen from
                        the centroid) is closest to theta. With 14 real stations this leaves a maximum azimuthal
                        gap of 167-202 degrees (in the synthetic c40 cell, >= 234): it is less unilateral than the synthetic
                        cell, and several subsets repeat (8 distinct in the 12 evaluation networks).
  In ladder_4 networks 2403 and 2404 drew the same subset (chance; different hours, statics and signals).
  NEW network indices: development j = 2400, 2401; evaluation j = 2402-2413 (12 per cell). New TAGs: 5161 STD, 5162
  DENSE, 5163 catalogue, 5164 statics, 5165 subsets, 5166 windows without events of the GEN arm. Prior grep over
  *.py, *.sh, *.txt, *.md of the working directory: none of 2400-2413 or of 5161-5166 appears as an index or TAG

  1401-1406, 1651-1652, 1851-1852, 2003, 2610-2613, 2701-2714, 3001-4105) are not touched.
Noise per network: the 2037 eligible slots, in temporal order, are split into 14 consecutive blocks (1.6-1.8 h
  each); network j uses block j - 2400 in the three cells (the cells share hours; the networks of a cell do not).
  Within the block, the slot at position p goes to: p % 4 = 0 catalogue candidates, 1 STD windows, 2 DENSE windows,
  3 windows without events (36-37 slots per use). The development networks use the hours 00:00-03:35, the
  evaluation ones 03:35-24:00 (never seen in development).
Catalogue of each network: 20 events generated in the same way (logic of xc.catalogue_cfg: uniform source, energy gate 25
  on >= 3 arrivals, recorded errors 1 km and 0.1 s) with the noise of the arm; from it come +SC (S0), S2 (c11_lib, pick-free
  JHD, constants of C11) and the time levels of T3 (catalogue rule of C10). Oracle: true statics.
Windows per network: 12 STD + 12 DENSE + 12 WITHOUT EVENTS (kmax 7; for the STD thresholds their first 5 atoms are used,
  identical to those of kmax 5 because the greedy loop does not depend on kmax). 36 networks per arm: 432 + 432 + 432 windows.
Methods (code paths unchanged): MT@S0 (MT-FW cap 6, hard rule +-0.25 s, +SC; c10_lib.mt_loop), MT@S0+T3,
  MT@S2, MT@S2+T3, MT@ORA+T3 (true statics and oracle level), BOMP@S0 (BOMP-PK +SC; c10_lib.bomp_loop),
  HUBER-N@S0 (c8_lib.mtfw_general "huber_n", c = 2.7955, +SC).
Measure (as C11/C14): exact FROC, recall at <= 0.1 and <= 0.25 realised false events per window (operating point
  per method, re-chosen in each replicate), rules LOC+T (epicentre <= 5 km and |origin error| <= 0.5 s) and LOC
  (<= 5 km); hierarchical bootstrap (strata = cell; networks and then windows; c10_analyze.Boot), 1000 replicates,
  95 % percentile intervals, paired differences. The REAL - GEN difference of a contrast is resampled
  jointly (same networks and windows).
Primary readings (C16_FREEZE.txt): G1-R = DENSE, LOC, <= 0.25, REAL arm: MT@S0 - BOMP@S0 and HUBER-N@S0 - BOMP@S0.
  G1-C = STD, LOC+T, <= 0.25, REAL arm: MT@S2+T3 - MT@S0+T3. Descriptive rule for "survives", fixed at the
  freeze: point with the registered sign and interval that excludes 0; "not contradicted" if the point has the sign and the
  interval includes 0; "inverted" if the sign is the opposite with an interval that excludes 0.
Transfer of the operating point: score threshold t of the operating point (<= 0.1 / <= 0.25) fixed on the
  windows with events of the arm (DENSE with LOC; STD with LOC+T and with LOC) and false events per window in the windows without
  events of the same arm with that t; in addition X = the REAL windows without events with the threshold of the GEN arm (synthetic
  operating point applied to real noise). Bootstrap of networks (same draw for windows with and without events) and
  windows, 500 replicates.

9. LIMITATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  (i)   One site (Alto Tiberina), one day (2010-07-23), 14 stations; the cells share hours (network j of each
        cell uses the same block of 1.6-1.8 h), so that the bootstrap, which treats the cells as independent
        strata, somewhat underestimates the uncertainty of the pooled lines.
  (ii)  25 Hz resampled to 16 Hz with a 0.5 Hz high-pass and a 6.5 Hz anti-alias; no instrument response: the
        amplitude rule equalises the daily median level per channel and erases the level differences between stations
        (real and gain-related at the same time). Mix of EH (short-period) and HH (broadband) sensors.
  (iii) Uncatalogued events: the real network records microearthquakes below the catalogue (the cluster at 43.315 N had 32
        catalogued events that day, most of M < 1); those that fall in the windows cannot be separated from the
        noise. They affect all methods of a window equally and both sides of each paired contrast; they inflate the
        "false detections" of the windows without events and may lower the recall. The +-60 s exclusion only covers what is
        catalogued (and 200.100723.cat).
  (iv)  Sources, mechanisms, pulses, coda, gradient medium, site factors and statics remain synthetic
        (the same generator family); only the noise and the geometry are real. The test does not cover wrong
        velocity models or real waveforms.
  (v)   The REAL arm has no synthetic clutter and the GEN arm does (thin): the REAL - GEN difference mixes "real noise" and
        "no clutter". Since thin clutter is weak (amplitude 0.35), most of the difference is the noise.
  (vi)  Real geometry: stations at 5-31 km from the origin, closer than in the generator (10-45 km); the real
        "unilateral" cell leaves an azimuthal gap of 167-202 degrees (synthetic: >= 234) and repeats subsets (8
        distinct in 12 networks); in ladder_4 two networks share a subset.
  (vii) Small sample: 12 networks per cell and 12 windows per design and network (budget); the bootstrap deviation of the
        G1-C contrast is 0.060 (2L: 0.012), which is why G1-C remains unresolved. G1-R has intervals far from 0.
  (viii) One seed; no multiplicity correction outside the primary readings.
  (ix)  Post hoc (declared): the paired SNR ratio in DENSE (the frozen one gave nan), the correlations and
        percentiles of the first atom in section 5, and the levels per network. None changes a primary reading.
