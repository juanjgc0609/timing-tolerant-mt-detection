1. DESIGN (code: c7_lib.py; runner c7_run.py; analysis c7_analyze.py)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Generator pulse (gen_realistic): w = cos(phi) r_fc - sin(phi) H[r_fc], fc ~ U[1.4, 2.8] Hz, phi ~ U[-45, 45] deg; the
detectors correlate with a fixed 2 Hz Ricker (MF).
Pulse bases (orthonormal rows on a +-2 s support, 65 samples):
  Q1   the registered MF kernel KER/sqrt(E_r) (q = 1).
  PH   {Ricker 2 Hz, its Hilbert transform} (q = 2): spans every constant phase rotation of the 2 Hz Ricker exactly.
  PHF  first q right singular vectors of the unit-norm design family (57 fc x 37 phi). q was fixed BEFORE any detection
       run by the rule "smallest q whose worst-case captured energy over the family is >= 0.95": captured energy
       (worst / mean) q=1 0.358/0.740, q=2 0.716/0.914, q=3 0.845/0.980, q=4 0.975/0.995 -> q = 4. For comparison
       the 2 Hz MF captures 0.366/0.734 and PH 0.732/0.906 of the family at zero lag. PHF3 (q = 3) is run as a
       declared sensitivity arm.
Whitened model (the task2c identities extended to q pulses): y = D sqrt(E_r)/sd; filter outputs F_j = y (x) psi_j
(F_1 = MFn for Q1); an atom column (k, j) read at sample I_bi is the data waveform G_bi[:, k] psi_j(t - I_bi), its
F_j' image is G_bi[:, k] X[j', j](s - I_bi) with X the cross-correlation kernels of the basis; the exact Gram between
columns is G.G X[j, J](I_a - I_b) (P/S and inter-atom terms included).
Detector BOMP-PK-Q (everything else identical to BOMP-PK+SC: grid, origins, corrections, +-4-sample search, 5 km
exclusion, kmax, running-minimum score, hit rule):
  peak search: argmax of the ENVELOPE energy sum_j ||F_j,i(:, k)||^2 in the +-4-sample window (phase-invariant);
  statistic: V (6ns x q) = residual filter outputs at the peak samples, Y = Q^T V (Q orthonormal basis of G);
    R1 (primary, the physical rank-1 model u = m c^T, one moment tensor and one source time function per event):
    e = lambda_max(Y^T Y) = max_{|c|=1} ||Q^T V c||^2;  FULL (relaxed 6q block): e = ||Y||_F^2. Score e/2.
    No P/S cross term in the statistic (as MT-FW; see section 6 for why this is exact to 1e-12 here).
  refit: joint least squares over all active atoms with the exact 6q Gram; R1 by alternating (bilinear) least squares
    of u_a = m_a c_a^T from c_a = top eigenvector of the entry statistic; FULL unconstrained.
  cap (optional, selection statistic only, as BOMP-PK-CAP of c2_ablate): each station/phase 3q-vector clipped at norm
    CAPV = 6 (the MT-FW value, not tuned).
Variants: BOMP-PK (registered code), BOMP-PK-NX (c7 code, q = 1: control), BOMP-PK-CAP (NX + cap), BOMP-PK-PH and
BOMP-PK-PHF (PRIMARIES, R1), BOMP-PK-PH-FULL, BOMP-PK-PHF-FULL (relaxations), BOMP-PK-PHF3 (sensitivity),
BOMP-PK-PH-CAP, BOMP-PK-PHF-CAP (the requested combination with the C2 cap), MT-FW (dense, c6_lib), MT-FW-PH /
MT-FW-PHF (dense MT-FW with q filters: envelope peak search, 3q cap, R1 statistic, sequential rank-1 deflation from the
top singular pair of the capped Y), CEIL-GD (verif_2e ceiling, c6_ceil: truth-start refined oracle deflation with the
2 Hz continuous atom, HOM BOMP-PK statistic).
Out-of-family pulses (c7_lib.OOFPulse; the generator's fc and phi draws are unchanged in rng consumption, the phase
range is widened to +-90 deg, and each pulse is scaled to the energy of the design Ricker of the same fc, so the SNR
design and the coda energy are unchanged):
  GABOR90   exp(-(t/s)^2) cos(2 pi f0 t + th), s = 0.6/f0 (narrower band, more cycles than a Ricker), th in +-90 deg.
  BERLAGE90 causal t^2 exp(-2 pi f0 t) sin(2 pi f0 t + th), t >= 0 (onset at the arrival, asymmetric), th in +-90.
  At zero lag with +-4-sample shifts they are captured by the bases as (Q1 / PH / PHF4): Gabor f0 = 2, th = 90:
  0.774 / 0.958 / 0.960; Gabor f0 = 1.4: 0.448-0.567 / 0.567 / 0.917; Berlage f0 = 2.8, th = 90: 0.642 / 0.662 /
  0.838 (a genuine out-of-family test: the subspaces capture more than the MF but not all).

8. INTERPRETATION AND JUDGEMENT
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Why invariance does not help (evidence-based, the mechanism part is an interpretation):
  (a) The pulse is a small part of the model error in realistic windows. Noise-free, with the generator's full
      realism, the true block captures only about 50% of the signal energy with the 2 Hz MF; pulse invariance adds
      1.4 (PH) to 5.0 (PHF) percentage points (section 2C). The rest is site amplification, polarisation deviation,
      medium and residual statics - consistent with C6b, where removing the pulse mismatch alone did not close the
      BOMP-PK headroom (PULSE variant within +-0.05).
  (b) The extra pulse degrees of freedom are paid for in spatial selectivity, not in noise. Empty-window maxima do not
      rise for the rank-1 statistic. Noise-free, the global maximum is within 5 km for every detector in >= 97% of
      windows (PH is even mislocated slightly less often, 0.013 vs 0.027), but the margin over nearby wrong nodes is
      thinner (sidelobe ratio +0.6 to +2.4%). With noise, clutter and overlapping events the first atom then lands
      beyond 5 km more often (STD first-atom hit 0.649 -> 0.609 PH / 0.577 PHF). Since almost all false alarms at the
      operating point are event-induced, this costs recall directly. This link is plausible but not proven: the
      noise-free diagnostic shows only a small resolution change, and the size of the recall loss is larger than it
      alone would suggest.
  (c) The relaxed 6q block (FULL) additionally raises the noise scores (PHF-FULL +42%) and is no better.
  (d) The cap (C2) and pulse invariance do not combine: PH-CAP = CAP within 0.01 in DENSE and worse in STD.
Criterion (plausible real contribution: >= 0.05 recall with an interval excluding 0 AND surviving the out-of-family
test): NOT MET by any variant in any design. Several variants are significantly WORSE than BOMP-PK in the design
family (STD pooled PH -0.084 [-0.148, -0.023], PHF -0.080 [-0.142, -0.005] at 0.25). Headroom captured is negative
(STD) or about zero (DENSE, OOF). C7 is NOT A CONTRIBUTION in this benchmark. It is a useful negative: the source time
function is not where the detector's headroom is; together with C6 (velocity model: null) it indicates that "more
physics in the atom" does not pay here, while robust per-station statistics (the cap) do.
Selection caveats: all 13 variants are reported; the two primaries and the q rule were fixed before any detection run;
PHF3 and FULL were declared as sensitivity/relaxation arms. While testing the analysis script I looked at a partial STD
analysis (115 windows per regime, 30 bootstrap replicates); nothing in the methods was changed afterwards. The
resolution and false-atom split diagnostics (sections 2C, 5) were added after seeing the STD results and are
descriptive. CEIL-GD uses the 2 Hz continuous atom by definition (verif_2e); it is a ceiling for the 2 Hz grid block
detector, so a pulse-adaptive detector could in principle exceed it - none came close.

9. DRAFT CONFIRMATORY PREREGISTRATION (Task 2H; to be appended to PREREGISTRO_1j_1k.txt and hashed BEFORE any code for
   it is run on seeds 4242/8888; nothing below has been run on those seeds)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Recommendation first: do NOT spend a confirmatory budget on C7 as a positive hypothesis; the pilot excludes a +0.05 gain
in the design family (STD upper bounds -0.023 / -0.005 at 0.25). If the thesis wants the negative claim ("pulse-
invariant subspaces do not improve, and may degrade, the moment-tensor detector") on registered data, the cheapest
route is to add the C7 arms as SECONDARY/descriptive comparisons to Task 2F (section 7.1 of REPORT.txt), whose dense
windows and networks already exist in its plan (cost about +0.35 s/window). A stand-alone draft follows.
Question (H-C7): does replacing the 2 Hz matched-filter block by the phase-invariant rank-1 block (BOMP-PK-PH) change
  recall relative to BOMP-PK+SC at equal realised false-event rate, in standard 0-2-event windows?
Data: seeds 4242 and 8888 (independent replications), FRESH networks task2a_confirm.network(seed, cell, j) disjoint from
  any 2F/2G allocation (e.g. j = 120..219), 3 cells, regimes thin and strong, 5 windows per network drawn with
  draw_sources from a TAG not used before; +SC from 20 catalogue events as in 2A; default gen_realistic.
Methods (code frozen and hashed: c7_lib.py, c7_run.py with its BASES/BOMPS definitions, task2d_methods.bomp_pk_record):
  BOMP-PK+SC (comparator), BOMP-PK-PH (primary), BOMP-PK-PHF (q = 4, secondary), BOMP-PK-CAP and BOMP-PK-PH-CAP
  (interaction with the cap, secondary), MT-FW (reference). Registered grid, origins, +-0.25 s tolerance, 5 km exclusion,
  hit rule, kmax 5.
Endpoint: recall at <= 0.25 realised false events per window (primary), <= 0.1 (secondary); exact FROC; operating point
  per method; hierarchical bootstrap (strata regime x cell, networks then windows, 2000 replicates).
Rules (two-sided, per seed, pooled over regimes and cells):
  2H-a (gain): BOMP-PK-PH - BOMP-PK lower 95% bound > 0 and point >= 0.03 in BOTH seeds -> pulse invariance helps.
  2H-b (harm): upper 95% bound < 0 in BOTH seeds -> pulse invariance degrades the detector (the pilot's direction).
  2H-c (equivalence, TOST margin 0.05): both one-sided 95% bounds inside [-0.05, +0.05] in both seeds -> no effect of
  practical size. Otherwise inconclusive. Holm over the 2 seeds x 2 caps for the strict reading.
Out-of-family arm (descriptive, required for any positive claim): the same networks with the BERLAGE90 and GABOR90
  generators (c7_lib.OOF, new TAGs), 40 networks per cell and regime; a 2H-a pass counts only if the pooled OOF point
  estimate is >= 0.03 in the same direction.
Sample size (c7_power.py -> out/c7_power.txt; pilot seed 999, 20 networks per cell and regime, 5 windows each, pooled
  bootstrap SD of BOMP-PK-PH - BOMP-PK at 0.25 = 0.0306, at 0.1 = 0.0461): n = n_pilot (SD (z_0.975 + z_pow)/delta)^2.
  delta 0.05, power 0.95 per seed: 196 networks per cell over both regimes = 98 per cell and regime; delta 0.03, power
  0.90: 220 per cell and regime. Recommended: 100 networks per cell and regime per seed (power 0.95 at delta 0.05,
  0.59 at 0.03 for the gain/harm tests; the equivalence test at margin 0.05 has power about 0.9 if the true effect is 0,
  and none if it is -0.08 as in the pilot, in which case 2H-b decides). 600 networks x 5 windows = 3000 windows per seed;
  about 1.2 s/window for the six methods (BOMP-PK can use the BOMP-PK-NX implementation, identical to 1e-12, if that is
  registered in advance; otherwise +0.4 s) -> about 1 h per seed on one core; OOF arm 2 x 1200 windows, about 0.8 h.
Interpretation: 2H-b passes -> registered negative: pulse invariance costs recall; 2H-c passes -> no practical effect;
  2H-a passes (unexpected) -> re-examine with the OOF arm before any claim. Nothing is changed after seeing 4242/8888.
