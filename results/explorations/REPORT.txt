Contrast: in STANDARD 2A-type windows (0-2 events) MT-FW and BOMP-PK are equal (C6, 600 windows: +0.017 [-0.061,
+0.092] / +0.002 [-0.050, +0.055]; leak2 thin +0.045/-0.013, strong -0.014/-0.007). The gain is specific to overlaps.

Why MT-FW beats BOMP-PK. The two detectors differ in exactly three ways (the running-minimum "prefix" score is the same
in both, so it cannot be the cause):
  (1) MT-FW clips each station/phase peak vector at norm CAPV = 6 (energy 36) before projecting on the 6-D moment-tensor
      subspace (statistic and amplitude fit); BOMP-PK does not;
  (2) MT-FW's statistic ||Q^T v||^2/2 has no P/S cross term in the Gram; BOMP-PK uses h^T K(I)^+ h with AC(t_S - t_P);
  (3) MT-FW deflates sequentially (subtracts only the new atom's fit); BOMP-PK refits all atoms jointly (OMP).

  MT-FW-NOCAP and BOMP-PK-SEQ (BOMP-PK with sequential deflation) produce IDENTICAL hit sequences in 360/360 windows:
  without the cap, MT-FW is BOMP-PK with sequential deflation, i.e. difference (2) is immaterial here, and difference (3)
  does not change recall (hit sequences differ in 84/180 thin windows but recall moves by <= 0.007).
Conclusion: the per-station cap is the whole effect. Removing it from MT-FW brings MT-FW exactly down to BOMP-PK;
adding it to BOMP-PK's selection statistic recovers most of the gap (the rest is plausibly the cap in the amplitude fit
and deflation, which BOMP-PK-CAP does not use). Interpretation: in overlapping sequences a few near-station or
site-amplified arrivals (site factors are lognormal, sigma 0.5) dominate the unclipped statistic and pull atoms to wrong
nodes; clipping makes the statistic demand agreement across many stations (a robust, Huber-like statistic).
Runtime: MT-FW 0.105 s/window vs BOMP-PK 0.662 s (thin), 0.147 vs 0.941 (strong).
Judgement: plausible real contribution (pooled +0.07 at both caps, CI excluding 0, mechanism identified). Novelty
caveat: MT-FW and its cap already exist in the thesis (1t/1x); clipping or normalising per-station contributions is
standard robust practice in stacking/back-projection detectors (per-trace normalisation, clipped or sign stacking).
The contribution would be the demonstration that, inside a moment-tensor subspace detector with deflation, per-station
clipping is what makes it resolve dense overlapping sequences (+0.07 recall) while costing nothing in isolated events,
and the negative results that joint refitting (OMP vs sequential) and the P/S Gram term do not matter.

5.5 Why the physics correction does not help (diagnostics)
  (i) Size of the medium mismatch (c6_ttresid.txt): over sources uniform in the target volume, |tt_gradient - tt_hom|
      averages 0.12 s; a per-station/phase constant removes almost none of the variation (mean 0.118 s, P95 0.30 s
      remain). The mismatch is real and of the order of the +-0.25 s tolerance, yet correcting it exactly (ORA) does
      not change recall: the peak search already absorbs it.
  (ii) Attribution run C6b (networks 20-29, 5 windows each, TAG 3008, 150 windows per regime x 5 generator variants
      sharing sources/mechanisms/statics/clutter/noise; each variant removes ONE mismatch). Thin regime, headroom
      CEIL-GD - BOMP-PK:HOM at 0.25: DEF +0.129 [-0.037, +0.228], homogeneous medium (MED) +0.194 [+0.081, +0.312],
      matched 2 Hz pulse (PULSE) +0.177 [+0.045, +0.308], no site/polarisation perturbation (SITE) +0.153 [+0.047,
      +0.288], no coda (CODA) +0.242 [+0.032, +0.366]. Removing any single mismatch does not close the headroom; with
      an exact medium the detectors still miss what the genie finds. Variant - DEF effects on BOMP-PK:HOM are all within
      +-0.05 at 0.25 in the thin regime (CIs +-0.15); MT-FW gains +0.13 [0.00, +0.30] with a matched pulse. Strong:
      headroom about 0 in every variant; MED raises BOMP-PK by +0.147 [-0.018, +0.250] at 0.25. ORA - HOM within the
      PULSE and SITE variants: +0.03 to +0.06 at 0.25 (CIs include 0).
      Reading: the 5-10 km mislocations behind the headroom are not caused by the travel-time model; they persist with
      exact travel times and are more plausibly the greedy detector's own ambiguity (sparse 4-8 station geometries, a
      +-0.25 s tolerance that lets a node 7 km away explain the same peaks, clutter). The genie avoids it only because it
      knows the source positions. The one clearly measurable physics lever is the station-static error left by a
      20-event catalogue (ORA-TS vs ORA, +0.06 at 0.25).
  (iii) Methodological note on the ceiling: deflating the genie at the TRUE arrival times with the 2 Hz atom
      (CEIL-GD-TT, kept in the records) gives a much lower ceiling (thin 0.562 vs 0.786 at 0.25) than the verif_2e
      definition with refined shifts: the per-event pulse (1.4-2.8 Hz, +-45 deg phase) moves the matched-filter peak
      away from the true arrival, so a genie that does not adapt the shifts leaves large residual energy. CEIL-GD values
      above use the verif_2e definition.
5.6 Runtime
  Per window (4 processes on 2 cores): BOMP-PK 0.80-0.83 s, dense MT-FW 0.13 s, for every model (tables precomputed; the
  model does not change detection cost). Setup per network: operators 0.6 s per model; JHD 0.4 s (H), 0.7-1.2 s (G),
  12-13 s (two-layer, bisection ray tracing). Single-process validation: dense MT-FW 0.070 s vs certified MT-FW 0.200 s.
5.7 Judgement
  Not a contribution in this design. The oracle medium captures -0.11 to +0.10 of the headroom (intervals from about -1
  to +0.8), JHD-calibrated gradient or two-layer models capture nothing and hurt when the catalogue is clutter-
  contaminated (strong regime). Only exact station statics help (+0.07, CI just excluding 0, pooled), and they are not
  attainable. Novelty caveat: minimum 1D models and station terms from JHD (VELEST-type) are standard in location; the
  novelty would have been doing it inside the pick-free moment-tensor detector and its certificate and quantifying the
  gain. The quantified gain is about zero, which is itself a useful negative for the thesis: "more physics" in the
  travel times is not where the detector's headroom is. If the thesis wants a physics lever, the evidence points to
  (a) better station statics (more or better catalogue events; ORA-TS - ORA about +0.06), and (b) the source pulse /
  radiation-amplitude model (C6b PULSE and SITE: MT-FW +0.10 to +0.13 when removed), not the velocity model.
