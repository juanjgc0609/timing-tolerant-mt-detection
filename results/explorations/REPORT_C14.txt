0. SUMMARY AND VERDICT
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(1) DIAGNOSIS. At the benchmark operating point (<= 0.25 and <= 0.1 false events per window) the false-event budget
    under LOC+T is spent mainly by a class that is NOT deflation self-interference: the detection of a real event,
    well located (median 3 km) with the origin shifted by a median of 0.8 s, almost always early (73-75%) and almost
    always WITHOUT another correct detection of the same event (86-92%). It is 55-58% of the false events in STD and
    80-95% in DENSE (development). Deflation residual 0-7%, coda 0%, cross phase 0-5%, copies of an already hit event
    0-11%, missed mislocated event 0-11%, clutter-dominated 0-25% (these still carry 24-28% of live event energy).
    The deflation residual only matters at low thresholds (12-23% of the false events above the twin's q95).
    Ground-truth ceiling: PERFECT deflation (subtracting the true contribution, direct + coda, of each hit event)
    gives +0.051 [-0.024, +0.111] (STD) and +0.052 [+0.012, +0.105] (DENSE) of LOC+T recall at <= 0.25 in development.
(2) THE REMEDIES DO WHAT THEY PROMISE ON THE FALSE EVENTS... On the unseen networks, R1 (hard exclusivity), R2

    pair (DENSE, LOC+T, <= 0.25: -0.002 to -0.005; R4 -0.011 [-0.019, -0.001]). But at ANY score (what the list of

    only appears at low score; if the benchmark moved to lower thresholds, that damage would pass into the recall.

    duplicates (the event has no other correct detection in ~90% of the cases); R4 does not change the STD recall and
    lowers it somewhat in DENSE. T3 (local refinement, reference) DOES improve the LOC+T recall in STD, +0.056 [+0.015,

(6) Development versus evaluation: the gains of the development winners shrank by a factor of 3 to 6 (STD

    -0.002). This is the winner's curse with 3 development networks; the freeze protocol did its job.
VERDICT: NEGATIVE for the stated objective. The starting hypothesis ("the false-positive budget of the operating
point is spent on self-interference; reducing it will raise the recall") is false in its second half: the
self-interference exists and the remedies halve it (and far from the events they almost eliminate it), but the
operating-point budget under LOC+T is spent by something else, the origin-time bias of well-located real
detections. No C14 remedy improves the LOC+T recall at <= 0.25 distinguishably from 0 in any window
type (STD +0.016 with CI up to +0.048; DENSE between -0.011 and -0.002), and the two most aggressive (R1h8, R3fir2) cost
25% of the overlapping events found at low score. The only thing left standing: R2a64 (CFAR normalisation of
the local scale of the residual after each deflation) is harmless for close pairs, divides the event-induced false
detections by 1.8 and gives +0.056 [+0.010, +0.086] under LOC at <= 0.1 in STD (not under LOC+T and not in DENSE). I do
not recommend preregistering any C14 remedy as a recall contribution; the next objective should be class a1 (origin
time: JHD terms of C11 + T3, or a better velocity/time model), which is where the budget is.

1. DESIGN (code: c14_lib.py, c14_run.py; analysis c14_analyze.py)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Base detector (BASE): MT-FW with cap 6 per station, registered +SC terms, hard rule +-0.25 s (4 samples),
3 km grid x 3 depths (675 nodes), origins 5.0-8.0 s step 0.1, epicentral exclusion of 5 km, greedy
deflation with the 6-parameter moment-tensor fit (c10_lib.mt_loop, unchanged). c14_lib.mt_loop14 is the
same loop with hooks for the remedies; with an empty configuration it reproduces c10_lib.mt_loop atom by atom (position,
origin and statistic bit-identical): checked on the 360 development windows (with the decomposition enabled)
and on the first two windows of each type of each part of round 2 and of the evaluation.

Remedies (all without ground truth at run time; they only use the residual and the atoms already accepted):
  R1  arrival exclusivity. After an atom is accepted, the samples within +-Wc of its read sample at each station (P and S)
      become CLAIMED. Hard version (R1h): the residual read by later candidates is 0 there, so that the
      statistic of a candidate is that of its unclaimed evidence. Soft version (R1s): weight rho^p, rho = min(1,
      residual energy / original energy) of the claimed window measured right after the deflation. The weights are
      applied to the residual with which peaks are read and the statistic is computed; the deflation always uses the
      unweighted residual.
  R2  time-varying noise scale (self-interference CFAR). Per station and component, running robust scale
      of the CURRENT residual (moving median of |R| over L samples / 0.6745) divided by the scale of the complete
      original trace of that station and component, with floor 1; the residual read by the candidates is divided by it.
      R2a: only after the first deflation (the first atom is that of BASE); R2f: from the start.
  R3  adaptive deflation. After the 6-parameter fit, each station-phase is subtracted with the best least-squares
      fit, over the deflation window, from a small family around the template (autocorrelation
      wavelet): 'amp' predicted polarisation and free scalar amplitude with the best shift within +-L
      samples; 'vec' free 3-component vector with the best shift; 'fir' free vector on each of the
      2L+1 shifted copies (short per-component shaping FIR filter). The guard against removing a second
      event is the filter length (L = 2 samples = 0.125 s) and the recall on close pairs (section 6).
  R4  merging of duplicates in origin time: a candidate within <= X km (epicentre) and <= Y s (origin) of an accepted
      atom is the same event and is excluded, in addition to the registered exclusion of 5 km at every origin.
  T3  local refinement from C10/C11 (c10_lib.mt_loop with EF: selection by the hard rule, relocation on the
      1 km grid within <= 10 km and <= 1 s with the independent time loading). It is not a self-interference remedy; it
      is included because the brief asks to see what it does with the "right place, wrong origin" atoms.

Diagnosis (ground truth used only here). Each window is regenerated in parts with clones of the generators (as in
c13_twin): per event, direct waves and coda; clutter + noise (the event-free twin of C13); the sum reproduces the
trace (assert). The statistic of an atom, s0 = v . P v / 2 (v the residual vectors with cap at the read
samples, P the projector of the node), is linear in v and is split EXACTLY (assert on the sum) among: direct waves
of each event, coda of each event, clutter + noise, and minus the deflations already applied. The direct part of an
event in a reading is assigned to "residual" if the arrival of that event closest to the reading (P or S) was already
claimed (an earlier atom read that station within <= 0.4 s of it; the deflation is added to the residual), to "live same
phase" if it was not and the phase matches, and to "live cross phase" if it does not match. Classes of the false atoms (LOC+T):
  a1  within <= 5 km of an event with |origin error| > 0.5 s (right place, wrong origin);
  otherwise, the largest of the five parts decides:  live same phase -> a2 (copy elsewhere of an event that some atom
  does hit) or e (only detection, mislocated, of an event that nobody hits); live cross phase -> b; residual -> c;
  coda -> d; clutter + noise -> f.
Literal measure of the brief: f_arr = fraction of the statistic that comes from readings within <= 0.4 s of a real arrival
(any part of the signal in that reading); f_acc = the same restricted to arrivals of already accepted events (an
earlier atom within <= 5 km); dom.acc = the event contributing most had already been accepted.
Ground-truth ceilings (development only): NOCODA = BASE on the trace without the coda of the events; ORAdir / ORAall = BASE
with oracle deflation: when an accepted atom falls within <= 5 km of an event not yet removed, the TRUE
contribution of that event (direct, or direct + coda) is subtracted instead of the fit. ORAall is the ceiling of any
R1/R3-type remedy: perfect deflation.

Reading of the diagnosis.
(i)  At the operating point fixed by the benchmark, the false-event budget under LOC+T is NOT spent by the deflation: it
     is spent by class a1, which is the detection (almost always the only one) of a real event, well located at ~3 km, with the
     origin biased by about 0.8 s, mostly towards earlier times. It is 55-58% of the false events in STD and 80-95% in DENSE. Under the
     LOC rule those atoms are hits. It is not "self-interference" in the sense of duplicates: the event has no other
     correct detection in 86-92% of the cases.
(ii) The deflation residual (c), the coda (d) and the cross-phase ghosts (b) are minor at the operating point
     (c 0-7%, d 0%, b 0-5%) and grow towards low thresholds (c 12-23% above the twin's q95). The coda is almost never the
     dominant part (<= 2%), although it contributes 5-8% of the statistic on average.
(iii) The "clutter-dominated" false events (f) still carry 24-28% of live event energy at the operating point:
     they are coalitions of clutter arrivals with fragments of real arrivals. This explains the ratio with the twin
     (STD 6.8 [4.9, 11.0], DENSE 10.7 [7.6, 17.5] at the twin's q95; at more than 10 km from every event 3.8 [2.6, 6.2] and 6.8
     [4.6, 12.3]) without there being a
     single class to target.
(iv) Ground-truth ceilings (development, B = 300, paired differences against BASE at the 0.25 point, LOC+T): perfect
     deflation of direct + coda (ORAall) +0.051 [-0.024, +0.111] in STD and +0.052 [+0.012, +0.105] in DENSE; direct
     only (ORAdir) +0.058 [0.000, +0.107] and +0.019 [-0.018, +0.041]; removing the coda (NOCODA) +0.036 [-0.053, +0.121] and
     +0.012 [-0.039, +0.065]. Under LOC: ORAall +0.007 (STD) and +0.071 [+0.020, +0.129] (DENSE). That is: even with
     perfect deflation, what can be gained in LOC+T recall by removing self-interference is of the order of +0.05.
     No R1/R3-type remedy can exceed that ceiling except through effects that are not deflation effects (e.g. changing
     which readings win the peak).

8. FROZEN CONFIGURATION AND POWER ESTIMATE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Frozen configuration evaluated (c14_frozen.json, C14_FREEZE.txt): R1h8 = {r1: hard, Wc 8 samples}; R2a64 = {r2:
L 64 samples (4 s), from the second iteration}; R3fir2 = {r3: fir, L 2}; R4x10t10 = {r4: 10 km, 1.0 s}; C23 = R2a64 +
R3fir2; T3 as reference.
The result is not promising for the primary criterion, so I do NOT propose a confirmation. For whoever wants to
decide with numbers, the power that would be needed (80%, two-sided alpha 0.05, assumed effect equal to the point
estimate here, n scaled from the bootstrap standard deviation of the paired difference with 12 networks per cell and regime and
16 windows per network: n = 12 (2.8 sd / effect)^2):
  R2a64, STD, LOC+T, <= 0.25: effect +0.016, sd 0.013 -> 62 networks per cell and regime (372 networks, 6 times C14); with
         half the effect, 250 per cell and regime. DENSE: effect -0.002, no power possible.
  R2a64, STD, LOC, <= 0.1 (the only CI that excludes 0): effect +0.056, sd 0.020 -> 12 networks per cell and regime; with
         half the effect (what is to be expected after selection; the LOC rule was not the primary one), 48.
  T3 (not a C14 remedy; reference from C10/C11), STD, LOC+T, <= 0.25: effect +0.056, sd 0.022 -> 15 networks; with
         half, 58. Consistent with C11, where T3 + JHD terms was the contrast that worked.
If despite everything something from here were registered, the only defensible option is R2a64 as a normalisation of the
local scale (harmless for close pairs, +30% cost) with the LOC criterion at <= 0.1 in standard windows, new seeds, 48
networks per cell and regime, and the close-pair recall at any score as a non-inferiority criterion
(margin -0.02).

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. LIMITATIONS AND WHAT THIS DOES NOT SAY
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  (i)   One seed; 12 evaluation networks per cell and regime, 16 windows per type and network; 3 development networks
        (the chosen constants were overfitted, section 0 (6)).
  (ii)  The diagnosis is from development (360 windows) and of the BASE detector; the classification by the largest part of the
        statistic is a convention (the parts are exact; the class is not). The fractions in the table have a sampling
        uncertainty of about +-0.07 at the operating point (44 and 46 false atoms).
  (iii) The remedies were tested one per family with 2-4 constants; soft R1 with other weight forms,
        R3 with the pulse basis of c7_lib, and R2 with other windows in evaluation were not tested.
  (iv)  The threshold of the ratios with the twin is the quantile of the twin scores of the method itself (not that
        of the CLUT windows of C13); the BASE ratios are therefore smaller than those of C13 (6.2 and 10.0 versus 12 and 45).
  (v)   The gains under LOC in STD (+0.05 at <= 0.1) are concentrated in the unilateral cell and in the thin regime; I did not
        correct for multiplicity (6 methods x 2 rules x 2 levels x 2 window types).
  (vi)  The interaction with the decoy calibration of C13 was not tested. Untested hypothesis: since the remedies
        bring the far false events down to the clutter level (C23: far ratio 1.0 in STD), a D1p decoy could end up
        better calibrated with them; under LOC+T it would still fail because of class a1, which no decoy imitates.
