0. SUMMARY
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(1) Travel-time-free channels cannot relocate atoms in this benchmark, and the reason is information, not algorithm.
    Bayesian Cramer-Rao bound on the epicentre (section 6): arrival times with the +-0.25 s tolerance give 0.4-0.7 km;
    P polarization gives 7.8-9.2 km, amplitudes 7.8-9.2 km, the S/P ratio 10.5-11.8 km (no information: the prior alone
    is 12.25 km), everything travel-time-free together 4.4-5.6 km. A joint estimator can improve the time-only bound
    by 4-5% (7-9% with zero polarization deviation and zero amplitude factors). The limiting factor is not the 10 deg
    deviation or the lognormal 0.5 factors but the waveform noise: the P arrival is 1.5-1.8 noise sd at these distances
    (20-45 km), i.e. 32-39 deg of angular noise per station.
(2) Measured on 600 single events (section 4): with the vectors read at the TRUE arrival samples, median epicentral
    error is 12.7-15.2 km for P-polarization triangulation, 8.2-9.8 km for P+S polarization geometry, 7.7-8.4 km for
    the moment-tensor projection with free times, 12.5-19.4 km for 1/r amplitudes (guessing the centre: 11.8 km),
    against 0.5-0.7 km for arrival times. Fraction within the 5 km hit radius: 0.15-0.18, 0.26-0.27, 0.29-0.33, 0.04-
    0.11 against 1.00. With zero deviation and zero amplitude factors the best channel reaches 5.4-7.5 km. Without
    travel times the arrivals cannot even be identified (a blind reader finds the P peak at 20% of the stations).
(3) Fusion as relocation (section 5): every relocation rule, post hoc or inside the loop, with the atom's own vectors or
    with a +-1.5 s re-read, LOSES recall: dense windows -0.11 to -0.18 at 0.25 (e.g. MT-FW in-loop relocation by the
    moment-tensor projection -0.107 [-0.149, -0.077]), standard windows -0.23 to -0.48. It moves 43-58% of the hits
    out of the 5 km radius and converts 21-46% of the near misses, which is what a random move inside a 9 km cluster
    does. The oracle variants do not rescue it: true amplitude factors -0.116 [-0.158, -0.081]; world with zero
    deviation and zero factors -0.138 [-0.187, -0.089]. Median error of hits grows from 2.6-2.7 to 3.1-3.2 km.
(4) Fusion as re-ranking / extra term: the only positive signal is NOT a location effect. The fraction of the read
    energy that is polarization-admissible at the node (P along the ray, S transverse; rho_pol) separates hits from
    false atoms (AUC 0.68 dense, 0.78 standard). Used as a factor of the MT-FW selection statistic (MT-FW+SELPOL, a
    POST HOC variant, see section 2):
        dense    - MT-FW  +0.046 [+0.007, +0.070]    - BOMP-PK  +0.045 [+0.003, +0.086]    headroom 0.12 [0.01, 0.24]
        standard - MT-FW  +0.045 [-0.002, +0.089]    - BOMP-PK  +0.002 [-0.041, +0.064]
    Post hoc re-ranking of MT-FW atoms by score x rho_pol: dense +0.028 [+0.005, +0.043]; of BOMP-PK atoms +0.010
    [-0.006, +0.031]. The re-ranking gain depends on the polarization scatter: +0.031 at 0 deg, +0.028 at 10 deg,
    +0.003 [-0.011, +0.023] at 20 deg, -0.015 [-0.025, +0.002] at 45 deg; SELPOL at 45 deg -0.014 [-0.029, +0.005].
    No candidate reaches +0.05 with an interval excluding 0; the best is the best of about 50 and was added after a
    first look. None captures more than about a tenth of the CEIL-GD headroom.
Judgement: travel-time-free LOCATION evidence is not a plausible contribution in this benchmark at any noise level
close to the nominal one (it would need about 10 times the signal-to-noise ratio and <= 2 deg of polarization scatter
to reach 1 km). Polarization-admissibility weighting is a small, plausible false-alarm discriminator, conditional on
polarization scatter <= 10 deg at most stations; on the real network (polarization reliable at 1 of 5 stations) the
measured dependence predicts no gain. See sections 7 and 8.

1. DESIGN (code: c9_lib.py; c9_standalone.py, c9_fisher.py, c9_run.py, c9_extra.py; analysis c9_standalone_analyze.py,
   c9_analyze.py, c9_pairs.py)

Travel-time-free statistics of a candidate position x, computed only from the per-station P and S peak vectors
v (2, ns, 3) in matched-filter noise units (n_xi = ray from x to station i in the detectors' homogeneous model, G_x the
registered radiation operator, Q_x its orthonormal basis):
  P1  azim    closed-form weighted least-squares intersection of the horizontal lines through the stations along the
              horizontal projection of the P vector (back-azimuth only; a line has no sign, so the polarity plays no
              role in this form)
      grid    argmax over x of - sum_i w_i sin^2 angle(v_Pi, n_xi), w_i = min(1, |v_Pi|^2 / 9) (back-azimuth and
              incidence; also with the circular rays of the generator's gradient medium: "gradmodel")
  P2  mt      ||Q_x^T v||^2: moment-tensor projection energy with the times free (1/r spreading, radiation pattern, P
              and S polarization at once); also with |v| capped at 6
      amp     - sum over phases of var_i (log max(|v_bi|, 1) + log d_xi): 1/r spreading only, no radiation pattern
  P3  Strans  sum_i (|v_Si|^2 - (v_Si.n_xi)^2): S transverse to the ray
      pol     sum_i (v_Pi.n_xi)^2 + Strans: P along the ray and S transverse (P1 + P3 in energy form)
      gainfree  explained energy of v_i ~ a_i G_i(x) m with a free gain a_i >= 0 per STATION (S/P amplitude ratio and
              P/S polarization geometry, no inter-station amplitudes); alternating, 3 rounds
  Remark on P3: in this generator P and S share the ray and there is no attenuation, so the S/P amplitude ratio does
  not depend on distance at all; it constrains the position only through the radiation pattern (take-off directions).
Vector readers: TRUE = energy peak within +-2 samples of the true arrival (oracle read, isolates the channel from the
  identification problem); BLIND = per station the pair of samples kP + 12 <= kS maximising E(kP) + E(kS) over the
  whole window (no travel time); WIDE (P4) = energy peak within +-1.5 s of an atom's read sample, P before the P/S
  midpoint and S after it. TIME reference: least-squares location from the same peak samples, homogeneous model,
  origin time profiled out, with ("truestatics") or without the true station delays.
P1-P3 standalone: 20 networks x 10 single events x 3 cells = 600 events, thin clutter (35 or 40 clutter arrivals,
  amplitude 0.35), 1 km grid (|x|, |y| <= 21 km, depths 5, 9, 13 km). Each event regenerated from identical streams
  under 7 generator settings (dir_deg, site_sd): (0, .5) (5, .5) (10, .5) (20, .5) (10, 0) (10, .25) (0, 0); the
  settings are paired. Intervals: bootstrap over networks, 500 replicates.
P4 fusion, on the atoms of BOMP-PK+SC and MT-FW+SC cap 6 (loops c9_lib.bomp_c9 / mtfw_c9, identical to the registered
  ones; they also return each atom's read samples and residual vectors):
  (a) re-ranking   RR-mt: score x rho_mt, rho_mt = ||Q^T v||^2 / |v|^2; RR-pol: score x rho_pol, rho_pol = pol / |v|^2
                   (vectors capped at 6; no running minimum, "raw" is the base without running minimum).
  (b) relocation   RL-{mt, pol, ppol, amp}-{n, w}: the atom is moved to the maximum of the statistic within 12 km
                   (epicentral, 3 km grid, all depths) using its own vectors (n) or the wide re-read (w), capped at 6;
                   scores and order unchanged, hits recomputed. "strict": a relocated atom within 5 km of an earlier
                   atom counts as a FALSE event (the registered metric lets repeated hits go free).
                   RLIN-mt / RLIN-pol: the same move INSIDE the loop, before exclusion and deflation (MT-FW both,
                   BOMP-PK mt only): the atom (n, t, I) becomes (n', t, I).
  (c) extra term   MT-FW+PEN: selection statistic s - 0.5 (|v|^2 / 2 - s) (unexplained energy penalised).
                   MT-FW+SELPOL: s x rho_pol; MT-FW+PENPOL: s - 0.5 (polarization-inadmissible energy) / 2 (post hoc).
  ORACLE variants  RL-mtos: relocation with the rows of G multiplied by the TRUE amplitude factors of the window;
                   worlds POL0 (dir_deg 0), AMP0 (site_sd 0), BOTH0 (both), regenerated from the same streams (same
                   sources, mechanisms, pulses, clutter, noise); +SC corrections of the nominal catalogue.
  Sample per design: 20 networks x 5 windows x 3 cells x 2 regimes = 600 windows (DENSE 2388 events, STD 444 events).
  CEIL-GD (verif_2e ceiling, c6_ceil) on the nominal DENSE and STD windows.
P5: section 6.

2. WHAT WAS FIXED BEFORE THE RESULTS AND WHAT WAS NOT
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Fixed before any detection run: all statistics of section 1, the relocation radius 12 km, the wide half-width 1.5 s,
  the cap 6 inside the relocation statistics, the penalty weight 0.5 of PEN, the designs STD, DENSE, DENSE-POL0,
  DENSE-AMP0, DENSE-BOTH0, STD-BOTH0, the strict duplicate rule.
Chosen on validation data (network 199, noise-free): the minimum P-S gap of the blind reader, 12 samples instead of
  the registered MIN_PS = 6 (with 6 the pair was the S peak and its own side lobe).
Post hoc, after a first look at the DENSE thin results (300 windows, 60 bootstrap replicates):
  (a) the exponents RR-pol^2, RR-pol^4 and RR-pol-only (rank by rho_pol alone);
  (b) MT-FW+SELPOL and MT-FW+PENPOL (c9_extra.py; windows regenerated from the same streams; the MT-FW records of the
      regeneration are identical to the main run, enforced by an assertion in c9_analyze.load);
  (c) the worlds DENSE-POL20 and DENSE-POL45 (dependence on the polarization scatter);
  (d) c9_pairs.py (differences across bases and per cell).
  The extras were first queued for five designs and cut to DENSE, STD and DENSE-POL45 for time; SELPOL has no result at
  0 and 20 deg. SELPOL is therefore the best of about 50 candidate records per base, selected after seeing that rho_pol
  discriminates hits; its estimate carries a selection bias. No multiplicity correction anywhere.
Looks at partial data: DENSE thin (60 replicates), DENSE pooled and STD pooled (150), DENSE-BOTH0 (100). They decided
  (a)-(d) and nothing else. Network 130 was used for two timing pilots (2 events, 2 windows per cell); the same data
  are part of the main sample.
Not done: BOMP-PK versions of PEN / SELPOL; relocation radii other than 12 km; a per-station (rather than global)
  polarization scatter; the P = S site case; persistent site factors (C8 "PERS" world).

7. JUDGEMENT PER CHANNEL
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Criterion of the task: a candidate counts if it reaches +0.05 recall with an interval excluding 0, or gives a clear
relocation gain.
  P1 polarization triangulation: NOT a plausible contribution. Standalone 12.7-15.2 km (19.8 km azimuth-only), 0.15-
     0.18 inside 5 km; bound 7.8-9.2 km; relocation by P polarization -0.139 to -0.152; no gain at 0 deg either. It
     would need SNR x 10 and <= 2 deg. On the real network (polarization reliable at 1 of 5 stations) one reliable
     station gives one line, not a location.
  P2 amplitude-only location: NOT a plausible contribution. The moment-tensor projection with free times is the best
     time-free locator (7.7-8.4 km, 0.29-0.33 inside 5 km; 5.4-7.5 km with no factors and no deviation) and is still an
     order of magnitude behind the times; relocation by it -0.107 to -0.123, with the TRUE factors -0.116 to -0.132.
     The pure 1/r fit is inconsistent even noise-free. The amplitude-factor sd changes little (0.5-2 km) because the
     waveform noise and the unknown mechanism dominate.
  P3 S/P ratio and S-P geometry: NOT a plausible contribution for location. The ratio has no distance dependence in a
     medium without attenuation and the bound says it carries nothing (10.5-11.8 km against a 12.25 km prior). S
     transversality alone locates to 14-16 km; together with P, 8.2-9.8 km. On real data attenuation would give the
     ratio a distance dependence, but through Q, which is at least as uncertain as the amplitude factors here.
  P4 relocation and in-loop relocation: NEGATIVE, clearly and in every variant, oracle included. Do not pursue.
  P4 re-ranking / extra term by polarization admissibility (RR-pol, SELPOL): SMALL and CONDITIONAL. Criterion not met:
     the best estimate is +0.046 [+0.007, +0.070] over MT-FW and +0.045 [+0.003, +0.086] over BOMP-PK in dense windows
     (below +0.05, post hoc, best of about 50), +0.002 [-0.041, +0.064] over BOMP-PK in standard windows, nothing at 4
     stations, 0.12 [0.01, 0.24] of the headroom. It is a clutter discriminator, not location physics, and it needs
     the polarization scatter to be <= 10 deg: the re-ranking gain is +0.003 at 20 deg and negative at 45 deg. With
     polarization reliable at 1 of 5 stations on the real network, the honest expectation there is zero or negative.
     It also does not answer the question that motivated C9 (atoms mislocated by 5-10 km): hit errors are unchanged.
  P5: the benchmark is time-dominated by a factor 50-100 in information at 4, 6 and 8 stations; a joint estimator is
     bounded at a 4-9% error reduction.
Caveats that must travel with any number above. (i) The 10 deg polarization scatter and the lognormal 0.5 amplitude
factors are our own generator choices; the generator applies one deviation and one factor per station and phase per
window, shared by all events of the window. (ii) The result that these two knobs matter little for location is
conditional on the benchmark's signal-to-noise ratio (P about 1.7 noise sd at 20-45 km); at 10 times that SNR the
knobs would decide. (iii) The dependence of the only positive effect on the scatter is steep (gone at 20 deg), and the
real network had reliable polarization at 1 of 5 stations. (iv) The hit rule ignores origin time (2F verification).
(v) One seed, 20 networks per cell and regime; MT-FW's own dense gain did not replicate at 0.25 in this sample.
Novelty: polarization filters and rectilinearity weights are standard in single-station location and in polarization-
weighted back-projection; nothing here is a new method. The reportable result is the negative one with its bound:
in a sparse network at local distances and low SNR, a pick-free moment-tensor detector cannot be relocated by
amplitude or polarization evidence, because the Fisher information of the epicentre is 50-100 times smaller than that
of the times, and the residual 5-10 km mislocation is therefore not recoverable by travel-time-free physics.
Recommendation: close the line. Report P5 (table of section 6) and the relocation negative as a boundary statement of
the thesis ("what the missing headroom is not"). Do not spend a confirmatory run on SELPOL unless the thesis wants a
registered statement about polarization weighting; if it does, section 8 is the design, and the 20 deg arm must be
part of it.
