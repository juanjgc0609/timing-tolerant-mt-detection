Part (ii) adds 25 new networks per cell, seed and regime:

  catalogue [s, 2003, ci, j, ri], windows [s, 2004, ci, j, ri].
- Windows 0-11 of each new network.
- Methods MT-FW+SC, QM-3C+SC, BOMP+SC and MTSTACK+SC.
- For 2C-c, networks 0-4 come from 2A plus part (i) (20 windows each) and networks 5-29 from part (ii)
  (12 windows each): 30 networks and 400 windows per cell.

Common to all methods:
- The same station corrections as 2A (station_corrections, 20-event catalogue per network and regime).
- The default_grid() nodes (675) and DEFAULT_TAUS (31) grid.
- 5 km epicentral exclusion, at most 5 events per window, and fw_record's hit rule (epicentre within 5 km of a
  true source; each source counted once).

Common matched-filter domain for BOMP+SC, MTSTACK+SC and GL-FW+SC:
- MFn = matched(D) / sd.
- Predicted sample of station i, phase b: int((d/V + tau + corr_ib) / DT), clipped (certified_fw._idx_node, the
  same index node_vectors_c uses before its peak search).
- A block is a (node, tau) pair with 6 columns, one per moment-tensor component. Column k predicts
  G_bi[:, k] x AC(t - t_bi) at the exact predicted sample (no peak search, no cap).
- This is the matched-filter image of the data-domain dictionary a_k = G[:, k] (x) ricker(t - t_bi) / sqrt(E_R),
  with y = D sqrt(E_R) / sd.
- Hence A^T y is MFn sampled at the predicted times and contracted with G. The Gram is
  K_kl = sum over stations and phase pairs of G_pi,k G_qi,l AC(t_pi - t_qi); P/S overlap of the same station is
  included. Deflation subtracts the block's image from MFn. Section 4 verifies these identities.

BOMP+SC (block OMP, Vera Rodriguez, Sacchi and Gu 2012), at each step:
- Select the non-excluded block with the largest e = h^T K_bb^+ h, the energy of the projection of the residual
  onto the block, with h = A_b^T r.
- Score = e / 2 at entry (the WAVE-MT / MT-FW convention), made monotone by the running minimum as in fw_record.
- Jointly refit all active blocks by least squares, u = K_SS^+ A_S^T y (orthogonal), and recompute the residual
  from MFn.

MTSTACK+SC (Anikiev et al. 2014):
- At every node and tau, m = pinv(G) v, where v holds the MFn amplitudes at the exact predicted samples (6ns).
- Stack value = ||G m||^2 / 2 (prediction energy).
- The node field is the maximum over tau. The catalogue comes from greedy NMS at 20 km (froc_exact.greedy_record),
  at most 5 events, with no deflation.

GL-FW+SC (grid group BLASSO on BOMP's dictionary):
- Problem: min 1/2 ||y - A u||^2 + lambda sum_b ||A_b u_b||. The group norm is the block-whitened norm
  sqrt(u_b^T K_bb u_b), so the dual certificate is c_b = sqrt(h_b^T K_bb^+ h_b), exactly BOMP's selection
  statistic, and lambda_max = max_b c_b(y).
- Solver: fully corrective Frank-Wolfe (active set). Each FW step adds the block with the largest certificate
  whenever it exceeds lambda (1 + 1e-6). The restricted problem is solved exactly, by block-coordinate descent
  (exact group soft-threshold in whitened coordinates) plus damped Newton on the non-zero groups, to a relative
  KKT violation <= 1e-9.
- Duality gap: dual point theta = s r, s = min(1, lambda / max c(r)), with
  gap = 1/2 (1-s)^2 ||r||^2 + lambda Omega(u) - s u^T A^T r.
- Path: 30 geometric values from lambda_max to lambda_max / 30, with warm starts.
- Score of a block = the largest grid lambda at which its group norm is > 0.
- Record: blocks in decreasing entry lambda (ties broken by larger group norm at entry), skipping blocks within
  5 km of an accepted one, at most 5.

Statistics (analyze_2c.py):
- Recall at <= 0.1 and <= 0.25 realised false events per window, on the exact FROC, with the operating point
  chosen on the evaluation itself.
- 2C-a and 2C-b intervals, 2000 replicates each:
  - Paired window bootstrap, default_rng([s, ci, ri]). These are exactly analyze_2a.py's resamples.
  - Network bootstrap, 5 networks with replacement, default_rng([s, ci, 77]) as in cluster_boot_verif.py.
- 2C-c: hierarchical bootstrap, 2000 replicates, default_rng([s, ci, ri, 2603]):
  - 30 networks drawn with replacement, then each drawn network's windows drawn with replacement.
  - The false rate is computed over the resampled windows.
  - The operating point (and, in 2C-a, the maximum over comparators) is re-chosen in every replicate.
  - Point estimate: all 400 windows of the cell pooled.
- Holm:
  - One-sided bootstrap p = (1 + #{d* <= 0}) / 2001, at level 0.025 (the level of the lower bound of a two-sided
    95% CI).
  - The family is the 36 comparisons of each regime: 3 comparators x 2 caps x 3 cells x 2 seeds.
  - The smallest attainable p is 1/2001, i.e. an adjusted 0.018 for the first step.


3. REVIEW OF THE INHERITED CODE (task2c_methods.py, task2c_run.py) AND CHANGES MADE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The inherited methods matched the preregistered definitions. The dictionary, the Gram with AC, BOMP's selection,
refit and score, MTSTACK, the group-lasso certificate and the gap formula were derived again and are correct. The
following problems were found and fixed before any 2024/7331 run.

(a) Missing validation. task2c_run.py 'validate' imported task2c_validate, which did not exist. It was written
    (section 4).

(b) OpenBLAS kernel handling was wrong for the current machine. The inherited check only compared the environment
    variable with "SkylakeX". On this machine numpy's OpenBLAS now defaults to SkylakeX, not Prescott as during
    2A/2B, so an unset variable would have run the 42 "default" networks with the wrong kernel.
    Fixed:
    - OPENBLAS_CORETYPE is set explicitly per process: SkylakeX for the 18 networks listed in task2b_report.txt,
      Prescott for all others (the other 2A networks, all part-(ii) networks and seed 999).
    - Each network checks the kernel actually loaded by numpy (threadpoolctl) and records it in its part file.
    - run_all launches only the networks whose required kernel matches the process.
    With OPENBLAS_CORETYPE=Prescott, scipy's separate OpenBLAS selects "Katmai", whereas in 2A the default gave it
    SkylakeX. scipy.linalg is not used by MT-FW, the station corrections or the 2C methods. All exact controls
    passed (section 5).

(c) GL-FW restricted solver: the tolerance could not be reached in floating point. The Newton polish targeted a
    relative KKT of 1e-11, with the gradient stop at 1e-12 lambda. The Armijo test then failed at rounding level
    and the solver looped up to 200 outer iterations: 39 s for 6 lambdas of one window, and minutes for longer
    paths.
    Fixed:
    - Restricted tolerance 1e-9, which is still 1000x tighter than the FW admission tolerance 1e-6.
    - The Armijo test gets a rounding slack of 1e-14 |F|.
    - The O(n^3) dense whitening Ti^T K Ti is replaced by a blockwise O(n^2) product.
    - The RuntimeError on "active block with certificate > lambda" is replaced by a bounded re-solve counter,
      recorded per lambda. It never triggered: 0 re-solves in 1200 windows.

(d) Path early stop. The inherited path stops at the first grid lambda at which the record has 5 accepted events.
    This is kept: later blocks enter at a strictly smaller lambda and sort after the 5 accepted ones, so the record
    is identical to the full path's. It was checked empirically by solving 2 more lambdas in 24 windows (section 4).
    The full 30-lambda path is not computed. Its support grows to hundreds of blocks and was measured at more than
    1 minute per lambda at lambda / lambda_max ~ 0.5 in one window. The duality gap is therefore reported for every
    SOLVED lambda (2-14 per window, median 3), not for all 30. This is a declared deviation from "the final duality gap
    at each lambda is reported".

(e) Records now also store each GL-FW event's group norm at entry. This was used only for the exploratory
    tie-broken variant.

Reading:
- With sources on the nodes, BOMP beats MT-FW and GL-FW comes close. The BOMP and GL-FW implementations are
  therefore sound.
- Their collapse comes from reading the matched-filter output at the exact predicted sample. With a 3 km node
  spacing and 0.1 s origin steps, an off-node source produces station-dependent moveout errors of a sizeable
  fraction of the 2 Hz autocorrelation main lobe, and the residual station-correction errors (rms 0.12-0.28 s,
  analyze_2a_out.txt) add to them. Amplitudes sampled there are attenuated or sign-flipped, which destroys the
  moment-tensor fit.
- Better corrections (E2) barely help, so the dominant term is grid discretisation, not correction error.
- MT-FW's +-0.25 s peak search absorbs both.

GL-FW ties. The preregistered score (grid lambda of entry) is coarse: adjacent lambdas differ by 12%.
- 1694/3000 (thin) and 1654/3000 (strong) recorded events share their score with another event of the same
  window.
- A tie-broken variant (GL-FW+SC-TB: order by group norm at entry) changes recall by at most 0.015. The ties
  are not what puts GL-FW below BOMP.
- The more likely cause is the lasso's shrinkage: a strong event is under-fitted, and the energy it leaves behind
  lets nearby spurious blocks (> 5 km) enter before weaker true events. BOMP's orthogonal refit removes that
  energy.


9. HONEST INTERPRETATION
------------------------

- 2C-a holds, and by large margins. What it establishes is narrower than "MT-FW beats sparse inversion".
  - On the shared 3 km / 0.1 s grid, with exact predicted times as preregistered, [129]-style BOMP and
    [122]-style MT stacking lose most of their recall to timing errors. MT-FW's peak search tolerates those
    errors.
  - The preregistered attribution therefore goes to MT-FW's own elements: the peak search (E3 shows the cap is
    not needed on nodes).
  - It does not show that MT-FW would beat a block-sparse inversion run on a finer grid or with time-shift
    tolerance, as [129] would do on real data. The claim in the proposal should say "robust to grid and timing
    errors on a coarse grid", not "better than sparse moment-tensor inversion".
- 2C-b is a negative result for the convex formulation as a practical detector.
  - On the grid, the group BLASSO solved to certified optimality (gaps <= 1.4e-8, KKT verified) is 0.2-0.6 recall
    below its greedy approximation. It is at or slightly below BOMP.
  - The thesis's convex problem is not what delivers MT-FW's performance. The performance comes from non-convex
    ingredients (peak search, cap, greedy deflation) that the convex problem does not contain.
  - The proposal should present the convex problem as the motivating and certifying framework, not as the method
    that performs best. Its grid discretisation should be listed as an open problem: an off-the-grid or
    time-tolerant convex formulation would be needed.
- 2C-c holds in both regimes, with and without Holm.
  - The advantage over QuakeMigrate generalises across 30 networks per cell, with the network as the unit of
    replication. This answers the auditor's point about inference.
  - The weakest case is strong clutter with 4 stations (seed 7331: +0.10 [+0.03, +0.18], Holm p 0.044, which
    passes under the literal reading but not under Holm).
  - In strong clutter with 4 stations, MT-FW+SC is not significantly better than BOMP+SC or MTSTACK+SC after Holm,
    in either seed.


10. DEVIATIONS AND PROBLEMS
---------------------------

1. GL-FW path.
   - The path stops at the first grid lambda that completes the 5-event record. The record is identical to the
     full path's (argument plus a 24-window check).
   - Gaps are therefore reported for the solved lambdas (2-14 per window), not all 30.
   - The full 30-lambda path was too expensive (support in the hundreds).
2. GL group norm = ||A_b u_b|| (block-whitened). The preregistration did not fix the weighting; this choice makes
   the certificate equal BOMP's statistic (and WAVE-MT's at exact times).
3. BOMP score = the projection energy of the residual on the entering block alone (e / 2). This is our reading of
   "energy explained by the block on entering"; it is not the drop in residual energy after the joint refit.
4. MTSTACK uses the plain least-squares inversion pinv(G) (it ignores the rare P/S temporal overlap that the BOMP
   Gram includes) and has no cap. Both follow the preregistered description.
5. Tolerances: FW admission lambda (1 + 1e-6); restricted KKT 1e-9. The inherited 1e-11 was unattainable.
6. Kernel.
   - OPENBLAS_CORETYPE is set explicitly (SkylakeX / Prescott) because the machine's default changed.
   - scipy's OpenBLAS differs from 2A on the Prescott networks (Katmai vs SkylakeX); it is unused, and all exact
     controls passed.
   - Part (ii) networks use Prescott.
7. Holm.
   - The family and level are our reading (36 comparisons per regime, one-sided 0.025).
   - Bootstrap p-values have resolution 1/2001.
   - 2C-c is reported under both the literal reading (CI only) and the Holm reading; they agree on the verdict.
8. 2C-c point estimates pool windows, so the 5 2A networks weigh 20/12 relative to the new ones; the hierarchical
   bootstrap weights networks equally.
9. BOMP/MTSTACK/GL-FW were not re-run on the 2A networks for part (ii): nets 0-4 in 2C-c use the part-(i) records
   of the same windows.
10. Sections 8-9 contain post-hoc exploratory analyses, declared as such. The verdicts do not depend on them.
