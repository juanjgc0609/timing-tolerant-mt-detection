STATION TIME CORRECTIONS FROM CATALOGUED EVENTS - Component C
Code: station_corrections.py (generate_c, peak_delays, catalogue, estimate_station_corrections, froc_recall,
boot_diff; `python station_corrections.py [out.txt]` reruns everything, about 7.5 min on one core)
======================================================================================================

SET-UP
* Networks: make_network('random', 4 | 6) and make_network('onesided', 8), 4 networks per cell (seeds
  77000 + 100 net + 0/10/20).  Station statics N(0, 0.4 s) per station and phase, drawn ONCE per network (as
  real station terms are) and applied to every window (generate_c = generate_y with fixed statics; the
  ~10-degree direction and lognormal site perturbations and the weak clutter remain per window).
  |static| > 0.25 s (outside the +-0.25 s MT search window) in 12-62% of station-phases per network.
* Catalogue: events uniform in [-15,15]^2 x [5,13] km, origin 5.5-7.5 s, random double couple, same
  generator.  An event is "catalogued" (a network would have detected it) if >= 3 station/phase arrivals
  exceed the energy gate; 26-121 events were drawn per 20 catalogued.  Known location and origin time
  (exact; a sensitivity run adds catalogue errors of 1 km per coordinate and 0.1 s).
* estimate_station_corrections: for each catalogued event, station and phase, the peak of the 3-component
  matched-filter energy within +-1 s of the predicted time (parabolic sub-sample refinement), gated at energy
  25 (5 noise sd), then the median over events (0 if no event passes the gate).  P and S peaks are chosen
  JOINTLY (max E(kP) + E(kS) with P >= 0.375 s before S).  In development, independent searches let the
  stronger S arrival fall in the P window of a station near the events (S-P < 2 s): 7 of 13 P delays at one
  station were S, and the median was wrong by 0.9 s.  (With the final catalogues the independent search gave
  the same medians, row '20 indep'; the joint search removes the failure mode rather than relying on the
  median to outvote it.)
* Evaluation: 40 fresh windows per network (draw_sources: 0-2 events), same network and statics; MT-FW
  (fw_record, run through certified_fw's oracle with the corrections added to the predicted P and S times;
  checked against a dense corrected loop and, uncorrected, against fw_record itself).  Recall at realized
  false events per window <= 0.1 / <= 0.25 on the exact FROC curve, pooled over the 4 networks of a cell
  (160 windows, 134-138 events); paired window-bootstrap 95% intervals for the differences.

RESULTS
1. Residual static (estimated - true), rms over station-phases (uncorrected: 0.40 s):
        K = 5:  pooled rms 0.171 s (per network 0.008-0.324, median 0.123); 9.1% of station-phases still
                beyond 0.25 s; 22% have no gated delay.
        K = 10: pooled rms 0.101 s (0.005-0.257, median 0.059); 3.9% beyond 0.25 s; 9.5% no estimate.
        K = 20: pooled rms 0.088 s (0.002-0.242, median 0.007); 2.8% beyond 0.25 s; 2.8% no estimate.
        K = 20 with catalogue errors (1 km, 0.1 s): pooled rms 0.173 s (0.067-0.276); 11.5% beyond 0.25 s.
   Where a station-phase has gated delays, the estimate is within ~0.01 s (exact wavelet, homogeneous
   medium).  Essentially all the residual comes from station-phases that NO catalogued event illuminated
   above the gate (distant stations, weak P), whose correction stays 0.  These are also the arrivals that
   contribute least to the detection statistic.  Catalogue location/origin errors propagate directly: with
   1 km / 0.1 s errors, K = 20 is no better than K = 5 with exact locations.

ASSESSMENT
* Unmodelled statics of sd 0.4 s cost MT-FW 0.18-0.35 in recall in the 4-station and one-sided cells (none:
  0.31-0.33 / 0.40-0.41 against oracle 0.51-0.66 / 0.67-0.75), consistent with Y.11 (0.37 / 0.42).
* Corrections estimated from 5-20 catalogued events recover the oracle performance.  The differences to the
  oracle are +0.01 to +0.05 and never significant, and K = 5 already captures most of the gain there.
  The station-correction step removes the fragility named in Y.11 / Y.13 item 6 under these conditions.
* In the 6-station cell neither the estimated nor the oracle correction gives a significant gain
  (oracle - none +0.07 / +0.08, intervals include 0).  On these 4 networks the uncorrected MT-FW was
  already less hurt, and one network (77310) did worse with the oracle correction (0.17 / 0.24) than
  without (0.20 / 0.37).  With 40 windows per network, per-network recall is noisy: tiny changes of the
  predicted times (0.01 s) move floor(t/DT) across a sample boundary, change which peak enters the +-4-sample
  window, and reorder individual Frank-Wolfe records.  Only the pooled, paired comparisons should be read.
* Limits: exact wavelet and homogeneous medium (real catalogue relocations and 3-D structure will leave
  larger residuals, about the 0.17 s of the catalogue-error run); statics constant in time; catalogue events
  drawn from the same region and generator as the evaluation events; 4 networks per cell.
