1. Generator model (default cfg in square brackets)
-----------------------------------------------------------
Interface and seeds. Same as generate_y (task1y_clutter): picks per station = 2*nsrc true arrivals (P and S
of each source, in order) followed by the clutter picks; amp = amplitude per pick in units of SNR*A/r, so
that degrade_y applies unchanged. rng_h gives delays, deviations and site factors in the same order as
generate_y; rng_m gives one double couple per source (rand_dc) likewise; rng gives clutter and noise in the same order. All
the new randomness (source function, coda, spikes) comes from rng_h.spawn(1), which does not alter the sequence of rng_h.
Verified consequence: with cfg={"g": 0, "VS0": 3.0} the times and amplitudes of all picks (true and
clutter) coincide with generate_y (difference 0 and 3e-16); only the waveforms change.

(a) 1D linear-gradient medium, v(z) = v0 + g z, z depth [VS0 = 2.8 km/s, g = 0.04 1/s, VP = 1.73 VS at all
    depths, i.e. VP0 = 4.844 km/s and gP = 0.0692 1/s]. Analytic time
        t = (1/g) arccosh(1 + g^2 r^2 / (2 v(z_s) v(z_r))),  r = source-station distance,
    evaluated as (2/g) asinh(x) = r/sqrt(v_s v_r) * asinh(x)/x, x = g r/(2 sqrt(v_s v_r)), stable for g -> 0.
    Circular rays centred at depth -v0/g; in the vertical plane of the ray (h horizontal from the source):
        g h_c = (g X^2 + (z_r - z_s)(v_s + v_r)) / (2X),   g R_c = hypot(g h_c, v_s),
        n_s = (v_s e_h + g h_c e_z)/(g R_c),   n_r = (v_r e_h - (g X - g h_c) e_z)/(g R_c).
    Everything is multiplied by g, so that g = 0 is exactly the straight ray. P and S have the same ratio g/v0,
    hence the same ray. P polarization = n_r (incidence of the curved ray) times the take-off factor n_s.M.n_s.
    S = transverse part of M n_s at take-off, decomposed into SV (e_SH x n_s) and SH (e_z x e_h, constant along
    the ray because the ray does not leave its plane) and recomposed with e_SH x n_r: rotation about e_SH, which in
    a 1D isotropic medium is the parallel transport of the polarization frame. Normalization CP, CS from task1w_mech.
    Geometrical spreading 1/r (as in generate_y). Vertical ray (X = 0): no rotation.
    Size of the mismatch with respect to the homogeneous medium (VP = 5.19, VS = 3.0) of the detectors, 2400
    source-station pairs with the benchmark geometry: t_grad - d/V for P median -0.007 s (p5 -0.22, p95 +0.14,
    extremes -0.44/+0.17); for S median -0.012 s (p5 -0.38, p95 +0.24, extremes -0.76/+0.30); near stations arrive late
    (v0 < VS) and far ones early (the ray dips to higher velocities). Angle between the straight ray and the curved arrival:
    median 12.4 degrees, p95 19.0, maximum 22.5.
(b) Delay per station and phase N(0, static_sd) [0.15 s]; deviation of the polarization direction by adding
    N(0, tan(dir_deg)) per component to the unit vector [dir_deg = 10, that of generate_y]; lognormal site factor per
    station and phase exp(N(0, site_sd)) [0.5]. All three with rng_h in the order of generate_y.
(c) Source function per event, common to P and S and to all stations: Ricker with centre frequency U[fc_range]
    [1.4, 2.8] Hz (the matched filter of the detectors stays at 2 Hz), phase-rotated by U[-phase_max_deg, +] [45]
    degrees: w = cos(phi) r - sin(phi) H[r], with H[r](t) = (2s + (2 - 4s^2) F(s))/sqrt(pi), s = pi fc t, F the Dawson
    function (closed form; error 1.8e-9 against scipy.signal.hilbert). The rotation preserves the pulse energy,
    integral of w^2 = 0.75 sqrt(pi/2)/(pi fc). phase_max_deg = 0 disables the rotation. meta=[] returns fc and phase.
(d) Scattered coda after each true P and S arrival: independent 3-component Gaussian noise with envelope
    exp(-(t - t_a)/coda_tau) from the pulse centre, CAUSAL Butterworth filter of order 4 in coda_band [1-4 Hz] (there
    is no energy before the arrival), scaled so that its untruncated energy is coda_ratio [0.3] times that of the direct
    arrival (|vec|^2 x integral of w^2). coda_tau [3 s]; length 6 coda_tau + 1 s. Checked on a noise-free
    window: coda/direct ratio 0.301. Clutter carries no coda.
(e) Clutter identical to generate_y: n_clutter 2 Hz Ricker pulses, amplitude SNR*clut_amp/median(d), random station,
    time U[2, 36] s and random isotropic polarization, random P or S label. Optional spikes [spikes = 0]: one
    sample on a random component, amplitude +-spike_amp [10] times the noise standard deviation; they do not enter the picks.
Noise: white Gaussian with standard deviation SNR*noise [0.02], that of generate_y (NOISE_SD = 0.14).

2. Numerical verification (self-test of gen_realistic.py and external control)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  g -> 0 (g = 1e-9, 1e-7, 0): relative error of the time against r/v0 <= 2.3e-7 (the 1e-7 case dominates; 2e-9 with
    1e-9 and exact 0 with g = 0); 1 - cos(direction, straight ray) <= 3.5e-13 at take-off and arrival.
  asinh form against (1/g) arccosh(...): relative error 1.9e-15. | |n| - 1 | <= 2.2e-16 (take-off and arrival).
  Numerical ray tracing (kinematic equations dx/dt = v^2 p, dp/dt = -grad v / v in 3D, DOP853 with
  tolerances 1e-12 and shooting on the take-off angle), 3 pairs x P and S, default gradient:
      X = 5.0 km, z_s = 5 km:  P 1.40970 s (straight with v0 1.45976), S 2.43878 s, incidence 43.0 degrees
      X = 40.2 km, z_s = 13:   P 7.90423 s (straight 8.71465), S 13.67431 s, incidence 57.4 degrees
      X = 47.2 km, z_s = 9:    P 9.17673 s (straight 9.91347), S 15.87575 s, incidence 61.6 degrees
    relative time error <= 2.0e-13; angular difference at take-off and arrival <= 1.2e-6 degrees.
  Independent eikonal (second-order fast marching, scikit-fmm 2025.6.23, 2D mesh (h, z)), same 3 pairs:
    relative error 8.2e-5 / 4.0e-5 / 4.6e-5 with step 0.04 km and 4.1e-5 / 1.9e-5 / 2.3e-5 with 0.02 km (converges with
    the step). Required criterion < 1 %: met with four orders of magnitude of margin.
  S transport (50 random mechanisms and geometries): the norm is preserved (5.6e-16), the arriving S is transverse
    to the arriving ray (1.7e-16), and with g = 0 the P and S radiations are those of generate_y (1.8e-15).
  Seed consistency with generate_y: see above (identical times and amplitudes with g = 0 and VS0 = 3).

3. Real picker
--------------
3.1 PhaseNet (feasible, method="phasenet", default)
  ORIGINAL pretrained model from the AI4EPS/PhaseNet repository, model/190703-214543 (model_95.ckpt), commit
  62005c6195638f88d077f8870ea895971062715a, with the network code from the repository (phasenet/model.py), on
  tensorflow-cpu 2.15.1 (last version with Keras 2; tf.compat.v1.layers does not work with Keras 3). Installed in
  ~/.cache/phasenet/tf215 with pip --target (the system has protobuf 7, which onnxruntime uses, and TF 2.15 requires
  protobuf < 5: the shared environment was not touched); repository in ~/.cache/phasenet/PhaseNet. Set-up time
  ~10 min. Preprocessing as in PhaseNet's predict.py with continuous data: E = x, N = y, Z = -z (z is
  depth), resampling 16 -> 100 Hz (resample_poly 25/4), normalize_long (3000 samples, step 1500), the whole
  4000-sample trace; picks = probability maxima >= threshold separated by >= 0.5 s.
  Pipeline check: on labelled samples from the repository the maxima fall within 0-10 samples of itp/its
  (P 0.94-0.98); on NOISE-FREE generate_real windows the network gives 0.8-0.9 to almost all P and S arrivals at the
  correct time. With the generator noise it barely responds: a P arrival with amplitude/noise 6.7 gives 0.09, and with
  noise 4 times smaller 0.43. The white noise of the generator occupies 0-8 Hz after resampling, ABOVE the band of
  the signal (1.4-2.8 Hz), the opposite of the real seismic noise (red, microseism < 1 Hz) of its training. I tried
  prefilters (bandpass 1-4, 1-6, 0.7-3.5 Hz, lowpass 4 Hz): none improves the F1 in calibration.
3.2 Classic STA/LTA (method="stalta")
  Zero-phase Butterworth bandpass of order 4, 100 Hz, ObsPy classic_sta_lta + trigger_onset on the vertical (P) and
  the horizontal norm (S); onset = trigger, or refined with AIC (aic_simple) or Baer-Kradolfer (pk_baer) in
  [-1.5, +0.5] s; optional resolution of simultaneous P/S triggers by the vertical energy fraction. AIC, pk_baer and
  the P/S resolution worsened the F1 in calibration and were left disabled (the trigger with calibrated offset is
  better for Ricker pulses in white noise).
3.3 Calibration (seed 999, windows evbase 1000, 20 per cell, 80 windows, 840 arrivals; the report uses other
  windows, evbase 25000; never 2024 or 7331). Criterion: F1 per phase of true arrivals matched one to one
  within 0.3 s. Offset = -(median of t_pick - t_true within 1 s), refined once: the pickers mark
  the ONSET and the true time of the generator is the CENTRE of the pulse (PhaseNet picks ~0.35 s earlier).
    PhaseNet PICK_CFG: threshold P 0.25, S 0.03 (grid 0.02, 0.03, 0.05-0.95), offset P +0.353 s (only 5 residuals:
      unreliable), S +0.328 s, mpd 0.5 s. Calibration F1 P 0.025, S 0.135.
    STA/LTA CLASSIC_CFG: band 1-4 Hz (chosen over 1-6); P: STA 0.3 s, LTA 8 s, trigger 6.0; S: STA 0.6 s,
      LTA 8 s, trigger 3.0 (grid STA 0.3/0.6 x LTA 4/8 x trigger 2-8 x refinement none/aic/baer), end 1.0,
      offset P +0.026 s, S +0.016 s, no P/S resolution. Calibration F1 P 0.126, S 0.193.
  The optimal thresholds ended up in the interior of their grids (after widening the first ones, where they fell on the edge);
  STA and LTA are on the edge of grids of only two values.

5. Reading
----------
  - degrade_y is optimistic relative to both real pickers: it detects half of the arrivals at a ~ 2 (its law
    Phi((a-2)/0.5)), the calibrated STA/LTA at a ~ 5 and PhaseNet at a ~ 8-10. In these windows that is 0.42 of the
    arrivals picked against 0.14 (STA/LTA) and 0.04 (PhaseNet). The timing error of degrade_y (3 times the
    Cramer-Rao bound, median 0.03 s) is ~2.5 times smaller than that of the real pickers (0.08 s; p90 0.20-0.22 against
    0.11 s, with the tail truncated at 0.3 s by the definition).
  - False picks: degrade_y only produces those from strong clutter (~28 per window with clut_amp 1.75, almost none with
    0.35) and almost none from noise or swapped phase. The real pickers produce 1.4-2.3 false picks per window from
    noise and coda ("other") and 0.25-1.5 from swapped phase even without visible clutter. The STA/LTA with a high P trigger
    only fires on 7-8 of the ~28 strong clutter pulses and PhaseNet almost never (0.4-0.5), because an isolated Ricker
    pulse does not resemble an earthquake.
  - The S phase dominates the real detections (amplitude 1.5 times the P, plus the coda); the P is picked very little
    (PhaseNet 0.4 %, STA/LTA 5 %), whereas degrade_y picks 29 % of the P arrivals.
  - PhaseNet outside its domain (an isolated Ricker pulse in 0-8 Hz white noise) is much worse than a
    calibrated STA/LTA; this does not reflect its performance on real data. For the benchmark, method="stalta" is the most
    informative real picker; PhaseNet remains as a pessimistic bound and as a check that the result does not depend on
    a particular picker.

6. Limitations
--------------
  - White noise (that of generate_y), without microseism or red noise, and signal limited to 8 Hz by the 16 Hz sampling:
    this is the main cause of PhaseNet's poor result. Noise with a realistic spectrum (and a higher sampling rate)
    would change the figures of the real pickers; degrade_y does not see the noise spectrum.
  - No attenuation (Q), no different corner frequency for P and S, source function common to all stations.
  - Spreading 1/r with the straight distance (not the geometric one of the curved ray); no free surface (the polarization
    at the station is that of the incident ray, with no conversion or surface amplification), no converted,
    reflected or head waves (the medium is a continuous gradient, without Moho); random station delays, not
    correlated with the geometry.
  - Coda isotropic in polarization and with a simple exponential envelope; it starts at the centre of the direct pulse.
  - Clutter = isolated 2 Hz Ricker pulses (as in generate_y); spikes are disabled by default.
  - PhaseNet: original 2019 model without retraining; P offset estimated with only 5 residuals (P bias in the report
    -0.06 s). Calibration of both pickers within seed 999, with windows different from those of the report; the
    figures are from 160 windows (1608 arrivals), without confidence intervals.
  - The tail of the timing error is truncated at 0.3 s by the definition of a true pick.
