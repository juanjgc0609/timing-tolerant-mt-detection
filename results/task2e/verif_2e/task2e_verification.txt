1. CODE REVIEW OF THE CONTINUOUS ATOM MODEL AGAINST THE GRID MODEL                               OUTCOME: PASS
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What I read: task2e_methods.py in full, the BOMP-PK code in task2d_methods.py (peak_table, PKDict, bomp_pk_record),
and the helpers it uses (task2c BlockDict / ac_at / hit_of, certified_fw._idx_node, task0_common constants). I also
read the data generator (gen_realistic.py) and draw_sources.

Consistency points confirmed by reading the code:
- MF domain. Atom prediction a_bi AC(t - T_bi), a = G(x) m. The residual is MFn minus the joint prediction, as in
  BOMP-PK. The correlation b uses the exact band-limited MF interpolation, and the Gram uses the continuous AC.
- Arrival times. T = d/V + t0 + corr + delta. Station corrections are added with the same orientation as BOMP-PK:
  corr is (ns, 2) with columns P and S, used as corr.T in both codes. P and S have independent shifts per station,
  like BOMP-PK's independent peak search per phase.
- Gauge. The common shift is exactly confounded with t0, because T depends only on t0 + delta. This is a flat
  direction for L-BFGS-B. It does not change the predictions.
- Bounds. x, y in [-21, 21]. The outermost grid column is at 21.37 km and is clipped by 0.37 km, which is irrelevant
  because sources lie within +-15 km. z is in [1, 20] and t0 in [4.5, 8.5].
- Score. The registered score is the grid oracle value s_k (the uncapped MT-FW statistic on the residual). The
  continuous fit therefore never enters the ranking directly. This follows the registry and is not a bug, but it
  matters for interpretation (section 6).

Independent numerical checks (check1_verif.txt). This is my own code; it does not reuse task2e_validate.py.
1A. Theta on the grid node and shifts equal to the peak offsets: the TS-SFW atom reproduces the BOMP-PK block. I used
    120 BOMP-PK atoms, i.e. the full 5-atom least-squares solutions of 24 real seed-999 windows (network 1, 4 windows
    per cell and regime). Maximum relative differences: sampled prediction (MFn minus the BOMP-PK residual vs
    predict_sampled) 1.4e-15; Gram 9.0e-16; correlation 5.7e-16; joint least-squares objective 9.9e-16. The two are
    IDENTICAL.
    The refinement energy at the grid start equals the oracle value to 6.1e-14. The oracle value differs from
    BOMP-PK's e/2 only by the P/S cross term, a relative difference of at most 6.7e-14.
    One small asymmetry: BOMP-PK reads idx + [-4, 4] samples with idx = floor(T0/DT). Its window is therefore
    [-0.25 - frac, 0.25 - frac] s, while TS-SFW clips to [-0.25, 0.25]. This affects 182 of 1440 peak offsets
    (0.126), each by less than 1 sample (0.0625 s). The effect is registered-consistent and negligible.
1C. Objective and gradient vs an independent data-domain implementation. The check uses my own Ricker function, my
    own travel times and task1x_mtglrt.radiation_operator, and takes central differences of the independent J. Over
    6 cases (1-3 off-grid atoms, lambda 0 and 1.7):
    - J matches to at most 2.3e-13 relative.
    - The refinement energy with 1-2 active atoms matches to at most 2.3e-13.
    - The analytic gradient matches the finite differences of the independent J to at most 4.5e-8, which is the
      finite-difference truncation level for these steps.
1D. Sign conventions, tested on noise-free data built with my own code. The source is on a grid node, the origin on
    the tau grid, the real network corrections are used, and I injected delta = +0.12 s on P at station 0 and
    -0.08 s on S at station 1. Results in 2 cells:
    - BOMP-PK's peak offsets equal round(T_true/DT) - idx for every station and phase (+4 and +1 samples).
    - The TS-SFW refinement recovers all arrival times to within 1e-4 s, with a position error of 0.03 km.
    - The joint gradient at delta = 0 has the correct sign for both injected shifts, and its norm is 7e-7 at the
      truth.
Verdict for check 1: no implementation error. The continuous model is the exact continuous extension of the BOMP-PK
block. Station corrections, shift signs, P/S windows, bounds and gauge are consistent.

3. BUDGET HANDICAP                                                                                OUTCOME: NO HANDICAP
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The registered diagnostics give reason to suspect a budget problem: in the evaluation, the sliding L-BFGS-B stops at
its 50-iteration cap in 73-83% of steps, the refinement in 83-97%, and the alternations hit 5 in about 100% of cases.
I therefore reran TS-SFW+SC (f = 0.01, same oracle score) on seed-999 calibration windows in four variants:
  REG    the registered budget.
  X10    caps x10: 500 iterations for refinement and sliding, 20 alternations.
  MS     9-start refinement: the chosen node at t0 and t0 +- 0.1 s, plus its 6 axis neighbours (+-3 km in x and y,
         +-4 km in z). The shifts of every start are re-initialised from the residual peaks, and the start with the
         best energy is kept.
  X10MS  both.
Batch 1 ran all 4 variants on 90 windows (windows 0-2 of each of the 30 networks). Batch 2 ran REG and X10MS on 180
more windows (windows 3-8). The pooled comparison uses 270 windows with 208 events.

Reproducibility. REG recomputed equals the stored calibration record bit-for-bit in 88/90 windows. The hit pattern
is identical in 270/270 windows; the rest are floating-point path differences.

Convergence. With x10 caps, no L-BFGS-B call reaches its cap (0.00, against 0.80 for REG). The objective does
improve:
- Joint J after atom 1, X10MS vs REG: median -0.05%, P10 -7%; 35% of windows improve by more than 1%.
- J after atom 5: median -1.9%.
- Shifts at the +-0.25 s bound: 0.30 for REG, 0.33 for X10.

Detection (exact FROC, recall at 0.1 / 0.25, 95% CI from the hierarchical bootstrap):
  pool of 270 windows:  REG 0.351/0.462   X10MS 0.346/0.447   BOMP-PK+SC 0.351/0.457   BOMP-PK-SLIDE+SC 0.356/0.462
      X10MS - REG        -0.005 [-0.072, +0.045]   -0.014 [-0.052, +0.038]
      X10MS - BOMP-PK+SC -0.005 [-0.100, +0.096]   -0.010 [-0.068, +0.087]
  thin (135 windows):   X10MS - REG -0.010 / +0.000;  strong (135 windows): X10MS - REG -0.018 / +0.000
  Distinct true events ever hit: X10MS gains 6 and loses 4 of 208. The hit pattern changes in 25/270 windows.
  Batch 1 alone (90 windows, 72 events): X10, MS and X10MS vs REG +0.042/+0.014 in the pool. That is 3 events of
  72, it has no counterpart in the 270-window pool, and the event-level counts there are X10 +1/-0, MS +3/-0 and
  X10MS +2/-0.

Verdict for check 3: the registered budget truncates the optimiser in the numerical sense, since the objective keeps
improving with more iterations and more starts. It does not handicap detection: a 10x budget plus multi-start changes
recall by -0.014 to +0.000 at cap 0.25, far below the 0.05 threshold of 2E-a.


4. HEADROOM / CEILING                                                         OUTCOME: LARGE HEADROOM (NOT SATURATED)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Data: all 600 seed-999 calibration windows (5 networks x 20 windows per cell and regime). For each window I
regenerated the event signal exactly (events and coda, without clutter and noise), so D - D_signal is the exact
clutter+noise-only part; a full regeneration check passed in 600/600 windows.

Oracle ("genie") detectors, all scored with an exact FROC:
  CEIL-G     Each true event is scored by the best BOMP-PK block statistic e/2 (grid, +-0.25 s peak reading) over the
             nodes within 5 km of it. The false candidates are the greedy block maxima of the clutter+noise-only
             data, more than 5 km from every source. This assumes perfect localisation and perfect deflation, so it
             is a generous ceiling for any grid block detector.
  CEIL-GD    Same true scores. The false candidates are greedy maxima of the full data after an ORACLE joint
             least-squares deflation of the true events, so it keeps the false alarms caused by event-model mismatch.
             This is the more realistic ceiling.
  CEIL-C     Continuous version, equivalent to TS-SFW initialised at the true source. True events are scored by the
             single-atom off-grid energy (500 iterations from the truth). False candidates are refined the same way
             on the clutter+noise-only data.
  CEIL-G+BF  CEIL-G true scores plus BOMP-PK's own realised false atoms.

Reading. BOMP-PK is NOT within 0.05 of the ceiling in any cell. At cap 0.25 it is 0.16-0.37 below the generous
ceiling and 0.08-0.24 below the realistic one (thin 0.156, strong 0.162 pooled). The benchmark has room for a 0.05
gain, so the negative result is NOT an artefact of a saturated benchmark. CEIL-C is about the same as CEIL-G (all 600
windows: 0.699 vs 0.692 at 0.25): scoring with the continuous off-grid energy does not separate events from
clutter/noise better than the grid statistic, because it raises the noise scores as well.

Where the headroom is:
- 89% of events have a near-truth grid score above every clutter+noise-only candidate of their window. BOMP-PK ever
  places an atom within 5 km of only 77% of those; for TS-SFW the figure is 74%.
- Among the detectable events that are never hit (99 for BOMP-PK, 110 for TS-SFW), the nearest atom lies at median
  7.3 / 6.8 km. For 80% / 85% of them it lies 5-10 km from the event (anatomy_verif.txt). The event is absorbed by
  an atom mislocated just beyond the 5 km hit tolerance.
- The data generator has a 1D gradient medium, per-event source spectra and phase rotations, statics and site
  factors, while the detector assumes a homogeneous medium. Even a refinement started at the true source drifts to a
  median 3D distance of 2.35 km (P90 6.7 km).
- The registered TS-SFW moves atoms from their entry node by a median of 0.7 km, and by more than 5 km in only
  0.4-0.6% of atoms (task2e_explore.txt). It cannot recover events whose grid oracle maximum is 5-10 km away.

6. WHY THE METHOD DOES NOT WIN (STRUCTURE, NOT A BUG)                           EXPLORATORY, FROM STORED PARTS ONLY
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- The TS-SFW record score is the grid oracle value, identical to BOMP-PK's statistic up to the P/S cross term.
  Off-grid modelling can change only two things: where the next atom enters (through a better residual), and whether
  an atom's final slid position is within 5 km of an event.
- Decomposition of TS-SFW - BOMP-PK. Mean over cell and seed pools, recall at 0.1 / 0.25, split into total =
  relocation (hit judged at the final position vs the entry node) + residual/ordering (reloc_verif.txt):
    evaluation, thin:    +0.050 / +0.026 = +0.027 / +0.008 relocation + +0.023 / +0.017 residual
    evaluation, strong:  +0.023 / +0.014 = +0.009 / +0.006 relocation + +0.014 / +0.008 residual
    seed 999, thin:      +0.036 / +0.030;   seed 999, strong: +0.052 / +0.028 (same order of magnitude)
  Both channels are real but small, a few hundredths.
- The group penalty is inert at the calibrated f = 0.01 (lambda is about 4.5% of the first certificate; no zero
  groups). At larger f the deflation is suppressed and recall collapses. Consequently TS-SFW = BOMP-PK-SLIDE (2E-b)
  and BOMP-PK-SLIDE - BOMP-PK is also below 0.05.


VERDICT
-------
TRUSTWORTHY NEGATIVE. No implementation error was found, and there is no budget handicap.
- The continuous atom reproduces the BOMP-PK block to machine precision on the grid.
- The objective and gradient agree with an independent data-domain implementation.
- Signs, station corrections, P/S windows, bounds and gauge are consistent.
- The reported numbers are reproduced exactly from the parts.
- The registry chain and ordering are intact.
- A 10x iteration budget with 9-start refinement lowers the objective but changes recall by -0.014 to +0.000 at cap
  0.25 (6 events gained, 4 lost, of 208).

The benchmark is not saturated. Oracle ceilings sit 0.08-0.37 above BOMP-PK at cap 0.25, so the failure of 2E-a is
not a headroom artefact. It is a property of the registered method. The headroom comes mostly from events absorbed
by atoms mislocated 5-10 km away because of the medium/source mismatch. A method that scores on the grid oracle and
then slides locally (median 0.7 km) cannot reach them, and scoring with the continuous energy does not separate
signal from noise better either (CEIL-C is about CEIL-G).

The refutation of G1-M as registered stands. The headroom indicates that a winning method would need global
relocation or association, or a better travel-time and source model, rather than local off-grid sliding.

Limitations:
- The new compute is seed 999 only, with 100 windows per cell for the ceilings, so the CIs are wide; every CEIL-G
  headroom CI still excludes 0.
- The ceilings are genie constructs (known source positions, exact noise-only data), not attainable bounds. CEIL-GD
  is the realistic reference.
- The budget test uses 270 windows (208 events), enough to exclude a +0.05 budget effect at the pooled level but not
  within individual cells.
