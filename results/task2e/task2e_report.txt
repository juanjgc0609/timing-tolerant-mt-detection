2. DESIGN AS IMPLEMENTED
------------------------

Data. Per seed (2024, 7331), regime (thin, strong) and cell (ladder_4, ladder_6, unilateral_c40): 30 networks = the 5
networks of 2A (20 windows each) + the 25 networks of 2C part (ii) (12 windows each) = 400 windows; 4800 windows in
total. Regenerated bit-exactly with task2b_ablation.setup_net and task2a_confirm.window_rngs/generate_window, with the
OpenBLAS kernel of 2C (SkylakeX on the 18 seed-7331 networks of 2A listed in task2b_report.txt, Prescott elsewhere;
checked with threadpoolctl at the start of every network and stored in every part). Same station corrections, grid
(675 nodes, 3 km / depths 5, 9, 13 km), origin times DEFAULT_TAUS, +-0.25 s tolerance, 5 km exclusion, at most 5
events per window and 5 km epicentral hit rule as in 2A-2D.

Continuous atom model (matched-filter domain, MFn = MF / sd). An atom has theta = (x, y, z, t0) and shifts delta
(2 phases x ns stations). Arrival time T_bi = d_i(x)/V_b + t0 + corr_ib + delta_bi (continuous, no rounding to samples).
Prediction a_bi AC(t - T_bi) with a_bi = G_bi(x) m, G = task1x_mtglrt.radiation_operator evaluated at continuous x, and
AC(s) = F(s)/F(0), F(s) = sum_{|l|<=16} ricker(s + l DT) KER_l, the continuous function whose samples are
task1x_mtglrt.AC (max difference 1.1e-16). This is exactly the matched-filter image of the data-domain atom
G m (x) ricker(t - T) / sqrt(E_R) used in 2C. "Continuous interpolation of the matched-filter traces" is implemented
as the exact band-limited evaluation MFc_i(T) = sum_n Dn_i[n] ricker(n DT - T) (|n DT - T| <= 1 s, Dn = D / sd),
which equals MFn at sample times (max difference 8.9e-16) and is the true correlation of the data with the
continuously shifted wavelet. Objective J = 1/2||y||^2 - m^T b + 1/2 m^T K m + lambda sum_k sqrt(m_k^T K_kk m_k)
with b_k = sum_bi G_kbi^T MFc_i(T_kbi), K_kl = sum_i sum_pq G_kpi^T G_lqi AC(T_kpi - T_lqi) (block-whitened group
norm as GL-FW of 2C). Analytic gradients w.r.t. all (x, y, z, t0, delta) (dG by complex step).

TS-SFW+SC, per window, k = 1..5:
 (a) oracle: max over non-excluded grid nodes and origin times of ||Q(g)^T v||^2 / 2, v = the 3-component residual
     vectors at the energy peak within +-4 samples (0.25 s) of each predicted station/phase sample (the uncapped MT-FW
     statistic, i.e. MT-FW-NOCAP+SC's, vectorised with task2d_methods.peak_table). Nodes within 5 km (epicentral) of
     the CURRENT position of any accepted atom are excluded. Value s_k.
 (b) the new atom starts at the chosen node and origin with delta_bi = clip(peak sample time - T_bi, -0.25, 0.25) and
     is refined by L-BFGS-B maximising E = v^T G (G^T G)^+ G^T v / 2 of the continuous residual (other atoms fixed);
     bounds x, y in [-21, 21] km (grid extent), z in [1, 20] km, t0 in [4.5, 8.5] s, delta in [-0.25, 0.25] s;
     at most 50 iterations.
 (c) sliding: alternate (i) the convex step in the amplitudes of all atoms with theta fixed (block-whitened group
     lasso, task2c_methods.solve_restricted, with the relative duality gap computed and required <= 1e-6; lambda = 0:
     least squares) and (ii) L-BFGS-B on (theta, delta) of all atoms with m fixed (full J including the penalty; at
     most 50 iterations); at most 5 alternations (stop early if the local step lowers J by <= 1e-10 |J|); a final
     convex step after the last local step.
 (d) sampled residual = MFn minus the joint prediction.
 lambda = f * s_1 (s_1 = first oracle value of the window). Record: running minimum of s_k; hit evaluated with the
 final (x, y) of each atom after the last iteration.
BOMP-PK-SLIDE+SC: the same with lambda = 0.
BOMP-PK+SC: task2d_methods.bomp_pk_record unchanged. On networks 0-4 the 2D records are used (the 2E recomputation is
identical in 1200/1200 windows); on networks 5-29 it was computed in 2E with the same code.
MT-FW+SC: stored records (task2a_parts for networks 0-4, task2c_parts/p2_* for 5-29). Regenerated with
certified_fw_record and asserted exactly equal on windows 0 and 1 of every network (720/720). Additionally
mtfw_sc_locs (same loop, returning nodes, for location errors) reproduced the stored record in 4800/4800 windows.

Analysis (analyze_2e.py): exact FROC, operating point chosen per method on the evaluation, recall at <= 0.1 and
<= 0.25 false events per window; hierarchical bootstrap (30 networks with replacement, then windows within network;
2000 replicates; default_rng([seed, cell, regime, 2801]); same resamples for all methods; operating point re-chosen in
each replicate); one-sided p = (1 + #{d* <= 0}) / 2001; Holm at 0.025 over the 6 cell x seed tests per regime.

7. DEVIATIONS, IMPLEMENTATION CHOICES AND PROBLEMS
---------------------------------------------------

D1 Units of lambda. The registry defines lambda = f x "the value of the oracle in the first iteration", and the oracle
   value as the energy of the projection, "the MT-FW statistic without cap". I used that statistic in the MT-FW
   convention (||Q^T v||^2 / 2, the same number as the record score). The group-lasso certificate is
   c = sqrt(2 s) in amplitude units, so lambda / c_1 = f sqrt(s_1 / 2), which varies by window (median 0.045 at
   f = 0.01). Reading the oracle value as the certificate c (lambda = f c_1) would have given a different effective
   grid. It was not run. Because the chosen f already makes TS-SFW almost identical to lambda = 0, and lambda = 0 also
   fails 2E-a, this does not change the conclusion.
D2 The 5 km oracle exclusion is applied around the current (slid) position of each accepted atom. The registry says
   "around accepted atoms" and does not specify grid or continuous positions.
D3 Choices not specified by the registry: bounds x, y in [-21, 21] km (grid extent) and t0 in [4.5, 8.5] s; internal
   scaling of time variables (0.2 s per unit) for L-BFGS-B; the refinement (b) capped at 50 iterations (the registry
   gives the 50 cap only for step (c)); the initial shifts from the peak samples clipped to +-0.25 s (a peak sample can
   be up to 0.3125 s before the continuous predicted time because of the floor in the grid index); the early stop of
   the alternation (J decrease <= 1e-10 |J|, which almost never triggered) and a final convex step.
D4 The refinement energy uses G^T G (no P/S overlap term), consistent with the oracle statistic. The convex and local
   steps use the full Gram K with the P/S overlap through AC, as GL-FW of 2C.

8. INTERPRETATION
-----------------

The primary result is negative. With 30 networks per cell and the network as the replication unit, the off-grid group
BLASSO with per-station/phase shifts (TS-SFW+SC) gains +0.000 to +0.039 recall at 0.25 over block orthogonal search
with the same peak tolerance (BOMP-PK+SC). The gain is positive in all 12 cells but below the preregistered 0.05 in
every cell, and no confidence interval excludes 0. Holm rejects nothing. Per the registry, hypothesis G1-M is refuted in
this synthetic design, and the proposal has to say so and reformulate its methodological contribution.

The group penalty is inert. With the calibrated f = 0.01, lambda is about 4.5% of the first certificate, no atom is
zeroed, and TS-SFW+SC differs from its lambda = 0 control by -0.020 to +0.013 at 0.25 (2E-b fails with 0 cells). The
calibration itself pushed lambda to the lowest registered value: larger penalties (f = 0.1, 0.3) zero atoms, stop the
deflation and cut recall by more than half. The small positive TS-SFW - BOMP-PK differences are therefore due to
sliding and the continuous refit, not to the convex penalty. Descriptively, sliding alone (BOMP-PK-SLIDE+SC -
BOMP-PK+SC) gives +0.003 to +0.042 at 0.25, with every CI containing 0.

Against MT-FW+SC (descriptive), TS-SFW+SC is within -0.026 to +0.049 at 0.25 in every cell, again with every CI
containing 0. It is lower at the strict end in thin 2024 (-0.02 to -0.12 at 0.1), where MT-FW's per-station cap helps.

Location improves only marginally: the median epicentral error of hits is 1.79 km versus 1.88 km for the grid methods
in thin, and 2.01 versus 2.15 km in strong. The continuous solution is too deep by about 0.3-1.3 km in most cells.

Why the off-grid machinery does not pay off here (the first two points are supported by this task's data; the third is
a plausible reading, not tested):
 (i) Local optimisation from a 3 km grid with 2 Hz data cycle-skips. Even in a matched, noise-free model (V4), it
     reaches < 0.5 km / < 0.02 s in only about 13% of sources. In the evaluation, atoms slide a median 0.7 km from
     their entry node (P90 1.8 km), and sliding changes the hit status of only about 1% of atoms (thin: 98 false->hit,
     62 hit->false out of 12,000; strong: 62 and 64; task2e_explore.txt, exploratory).
 (ii) The registered iteration budget binds: 73-83% of local steps stop at 50 iterations and almost every iteration
     uses all 5 alternations, so the sliding step is generally not at a stationary point. About 25-35% of shifts end
     at the +-0.25 s bound.
 (iii) With free per-station/phase shifts, the arrival times no longer constrain the location, which then rests on
     amplitude and polarization. Those are exactly what the generator perturbs (1.4-2.8 Hz wavelet with phase
     rotation, coda, ~10 degree polarization deviation, lognormal site factors, curved rays), and the continuous atom
     model does not represent those perturbations.
What the data support is that, with the same +-0.25 s tolerance, the grid block search already captures essentially
all the detection performance available to moment-tensor subspace methods in this design. The convex off-grid
formulation adds cost (about 3x BOMP-PK's time per window) and no detectable recall. This is consistent with 2D, where
the advantage of MT-FW over [129]/[122] was attributed to the peak search.
