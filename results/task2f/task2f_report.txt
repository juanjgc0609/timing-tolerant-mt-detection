7. INTERPRETATION (per the registry, with the limits that apply)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
H-CAP is confirmed in this synthetic design, in both regimes and both independent seeds: in windows with 3-5
overlapping events, MT-FW+SC detects more events than BOMP-PK+SC at equal realised false-event rate (+0.04 to +0.07
recall at cap 0.25, +0.06 to +0.09 at cap 0.1), and the gain is due to the per-station cap: removing the cap brings
MT-FW exactly down to BOMP-PK (difference within +-0.002), and adding the cap to BOMP-PK's selection statistic recovers
most of the gap. Joint refitting (OMP) versus sequential deflation, and the P/S cross term, make no measurable
difference here. In standard 0-2 event windows the cap does no harm beyond the 0.05 margin.
The pilot effect was not a selection artefact: the confirmatory estimates (thin +0.072/+0.074, strong +0.051/+0.043)
are close to the pilot point estimates (+0.074 thin, +0.051 strong) on fresh seeds with 16x more networks.
Limits, stated plainly:
  - The gain is modest in absolute terms: even with the cap, recall at 0.25 false events per window is only 0.22-0.29
    in dense windows; most overlapping events are still missed by both detectors.
  - 2F-c passes, but narrowly in seed 8888 (p = 0.0405 for the lower margin; its 95% CI reaches -0.055), and the
    point estimates in the strong regime of the standard arm are negative (-0.026, -0.032; per cell as low as -0.067
    on the 4-station ladder, all CIs including 0). A small cost of the cap for isolated events in strong clutter
    (of order 0.03) is not excluded by this arm (16 networks per cell).
  - Synthetic design only (gen_realistic generator, homogeneous detector model, catalogue corrections). The cap value
    CAPV = 6 was fixed in 1x and not tuned here.
  - Novelty, as declared in the registry: clipping or normalising per-station contributions is known practice in
    robust stacking; the contribution is the measured and explained effect of the cap in moment-tensor subspace
    detection of overlapping events, and the negative results that joint refitting and the P/S Gram term do not matter.
  - The hypothesis was selected among several explored candidates (C1-C7, mostly null) on seed 999; the confirmatory
    seeds 4242/8888 were untouched by that selection.
