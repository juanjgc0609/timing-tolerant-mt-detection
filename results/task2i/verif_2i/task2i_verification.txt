6. HONEST SCOPE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Synthetic data only, one generator family (gen_realistic, i.i.d. per-window site factors with log-sd 0.5, three
  network cells, two clutter regimes); the two seeds are independent draws from that same family, not independent
  evidence about real data. Secondary arms (other thresholds, site sd 0.25 / 0.8) were still running; not verified.
- Huber-weighted fitting is standard robust statistics. The contribution is the measured design result: inside a
  moment-tensor subspace detector, a per-station/phase Huber fit whose level is fixed by rule from the noise
  (c = sqrt(chi2_3 0.95) = 2.7955) recovers the gain of the calibrated cap 6 over BOMP-PK in dense windows
  (non-inferior within 0.03) without a tuned constant, at about 9x the runtime of the capped detector.
- HUBER-N was the best of 11 pilot candidates (selection declared); the confirmatory effect (+0.058 / +0.053) is close
  to the pilot's +0.064, so little regression.
- Absolute recalls are low (0.18 -> 0.24-0.25 in dense windows at 0.25 false/window; ~0.10 with the 0.5 s origin rule).
- Operating points are chosen in-sample per method (registered, symmetric); no held-out threshold was tested.
- The size of the gain depends on the hit rule (+0.05-0.06 location-only; +0.02-0.03 with origin time within 0.5 s),
  is weakest for 4-station networks under strong clutter, and the mechanism is mixed (score reliability and first-atom
  selection), not purely score reliability.
