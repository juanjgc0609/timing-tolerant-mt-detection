Author decision 1: the quality rule qc1 and the station rule are accepted without changes. qc1, on the raw 25 Hz counts of each channel: every sample of a run of >= 25 identical consecutive values (>= 1 s) is invalid, and so is every sample with |x - median| > 1e4 robust deviations (1.4826 MAD, computed without the flat runs); invalid samples are treated like the gaps of C16 (no slot with an invalid sample of any station of the set within 60 s is eligible). Station rule (t2m_stations.py): S = all stations with files for the day; while |S| > 8, remove the station whose removal most increases the eligible slots E(S), only if E increases by at least 20 %; the day is used if |S| >= 8 and E(S) >= 300. Both rules were written after seeing quality and level statistics of the noise of the new days (without injecting events or running any detector) and are declared as such. The 1e4-deviation threshold only catches digital spikes; real transients, which are the mechanism under test, pass.
