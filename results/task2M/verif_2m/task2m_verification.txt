6. OTHER POSSIBLE SOURCES OF A FALSE POSITIVE - FINDINGS AND CAVEATS
   a) None of the checks above found a defect that inflates the contrast. The positive is real for the registered endpoint.
   b) Scope (as declared, worth repeating): the effect is concentrated in hours with station-local transients (hours 0-7.3:
      MT-BOMP +0.033 with lower bound -0.044); in quiet windows of the same day the cap's advantage is small and not
      significant, while HUBER-N keeps +0.12. "Real noise with transients" is the correct scope; the result is one day, one
      network, synthetic signals.
   c) The advantage arises from the pooled operating point (one score threshold across windows) combined with BOMP-PK's
      unbounded energies: a per-window or robustly normalised BOMP score would likely recover much of the gap. This is the
      registered comparator and the very mechanism G1-R claims, so it is not a defect, but the result should be read as
      "bounded influence vs an unbounded energy statistic", not as a general superiority over sparse-recovery detectors.
   d) qc1 lets through extreme transients (up to ~1.2e4 robust sd in the MF domain, STD window of unilateral_c40 2806);
      whether such samples are natural transients or digital glitches is unknown. This affects only BOMP's ranking and is
      covered by the declared scope.
   e) The registered bootstrap ignores the sharing of noise hours across cells (declared); hour-clustered and moving-block
      bootstraps widen the MT-BOMP interval (lower +0.088 at worst) but keep both primary bounds well above 0.
   f) The 3141 MT-BOMP rule (+0.057, lower +0.004 registered; +0.005 and -0.001 with my strata-cell bootstraps of seeds 20261016 and 31415;
      +0.010 with the hour-clustered bootstrap) is marginal; it is secondary and does not enter the claim.
   g) No side effects: no file outside t2m_verif was written by this verification (the frozen t2m_lib was only imported to
      reproduce the two catalogue failures).
