    returns to the S0 level. The result is not shown for larger, biased or unknown catalogue errors, nor for
    catalogues of fewer than 20 events.

6. HONEST SCOPE OF THE CONFIRMED CLAIM
    Synthetic data only, one generator family (gen_realistic defaults, statics sd 0.3 s fixed per network, three
    geometries, two clutter regimes); the detectors' velocity model differs from the generator's only as built in.
    Joint hypocentre determination with station terms is classical in pick-based location; what is new here is
    estimating the terms without picks and measuring their effect inside the pick-free detector at a fixed false rate.
    The gain is conditional on the T3 refinement: 2L has no S2 arm without T3, and in the pilot (seed 999) the terms
    did nothing measurable with the hard rule alone. The registered superiority is for the origin-time hit rule
    (LOC+T, 0.5 s); under the epicentre-only rule only non-inferiority was registered (the observed gain there,
    +0.036 / +0.050 standard, is descriptive). It assumes a 20-event catalogue with 1 km / 0.1 s errors of known size.
    The size to quote is about +0.06 recall in standard and +0.03 in dense windows at 0.25 false events per window,
    concentrated in the one-sided 8-station geometry, with no demonstrated gain for 4 stations, and it captures only
    part of the oracle gain (0.28-0.61 in the report).
