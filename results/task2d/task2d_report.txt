2. DESIGN AS IMPLEMENTED
------------------------

Part (i). The 1200 windows of 2A (2 seeds x 2 regimes x 3 cells x networks 0-4 x 20 windows), regenerated bit
for bit exactly as in 2C (task2b_ablation.setup_net, task2a_confirm.window_rngs / generate_window), with the same
station corrections, the default_grid() nodes (675) x DEFAULT_TAUS (31), 5 km epicentral exclusion, at most 5
events per window and fw_record's hit rule. Kernel pinning as 2C (section 4).

Peak sampling ("PK"). For a residual R and the predicted sample idx = int((d/V + tau + corr)/DT) (clipped; the
index node_vectors_c uses), the sample read is the argmax of the 3-component energy ||R_i[:, k]||^2 over the window
clip(idx + o), |o| <= 4 samples (0.25 s), first maximum on ties: exactly node_vectors' rule. It is tabulated per
residual (peak_table, the formula of certified_fw.LeafTables) and verified equal to node_vectors_c on every node,
origin, station and phase (V1). No cap is applied in the PK variants (the 2C methods had none).

An atom is (node n, origin t, read samples I (2 x ns)). Its column k predicts G_bi[:, k] AC(s - I_bi) in the
matched-filter domain; the Gram between atoms is 2C's with I in place of the predicted samples; the block Gram
K(I) = G^T G + P/S cross terms with AC(I_S - I_P). With I = predicted samples everything reduces to 2C (V2).

- BOMP-PK+SC: at each step every non-excluded block (n, t) is read at the peaks of the CURRENT residual,
  h = G^T v, e = h^T K(I_pk)^+ h (eigen-floor pseudo-inverse as BlockDict; recomputed only for blocks whose K
  changes, V3). The block with the largest e enters with its read times FIXED at those peaks. Score e/2 at entry
  (running minimum). Orthogonal least-squares refit of all active atoms at their fixed times; residual recomputed
  from MFn.
- MTSTACK-PK+SC: v = MFn at the peaks of MFn (no cap), m = pinv(G) v, stack ||G m||^2 / 2, max over tau, greedy
  NMS at 20 km, at most 5, no deflation. (Its field equals WAVE-MT without cap; V4.)
- GL-FW-PK+SC: 2C's group lasso (block-whitened group norm, 30-value geometric lambda path from lambda_max to
  lambda_max/30, fully corrective Frank-Wolfe, exact restricted solver, score = entry lambda, early stop when the
  5-event record is complete) on a GROWING dictionary: the FW oracle evaluates the certificate
  c = sqrt(h^T K(I_pk)^+ h) of every block at the peaks of the current residual; the entering atom keeps those
  times fixed; the restricted problem is over the fixed atoms. lambda_max = max c on MFn (so BOMP-PK's first
  score = lambda_max^2 / 2, V4). The duality gap is reported for every solved lambda, w.r.t. the realised
  dictionary (active atoms at their fixed times plus the peak atoms of the final residual).
- MT-FW-EX+SC: fw_record (the loop of certified_fw.dense_fw_record_c) with node_vectors' peak search disabled:
  each station and phase is read at the exact predicted sample, with the CAPV cap, and deflation is centred there.
- MT-FW-NOCAP+SC: fw_record with the peak search and without the CAPV cap.
- MT-FW+SC and QM-3C+SC are the stored 2A records; BOMP+SC, MTSTACK+SC, GL-FW+SC the stored 2C records (context).

Part (ii). Split-conformal thresholds.
- Calibration: the 5 seed-999 calibration networks of 2A per cell (same stations, statics and station corrections,
  regenerated with setup_net(999, ...)), 40 empty windows per network = 200 per cell and regime, generated with
  generate_real with no sources (clutter count and amplitude of the regime, noise) on new streams
  [999, 2701, ci, j, ri, 41 | 233 | 61].
- Scores: the per-window maximum of MT-FW+SC (first score of certified_fw_record, full record stored) and of
  QM-3C+SC (frozen qm_recal_2a.json configuration with the corrections in the LUT; -inf when QuakeMigrate
  triggers no event).
- Threshold q = the ceil((n+1)(1-alpha))-th smallest of the n = 200 maxima (181st for alpha 0.1, 151st for 0.25);
  an event is declared if its score > q. Frozen per cell, regime, method and alpha, and applied unchanged to the
  2A evaluation windows (100 per cell and seed; 36-45 empty windows per cell).
- Reported: recall; false events per empty window (the rule's "rate"); fraction of empty windows with any false
  event (the quantity the conformal guarantee bounds); false events per window over all windows.

Statistics (analyze_2d.py). Hierarchical bootstrap, 2000 replicates, default_rng([s, ci, ri, 2704]): the 5
networks of the cell with replacement, then each drawn network's windows with replacement; same resamples for all
methods of a cell. In part (i) the operating point and the max over comparators are re-chosen in every replicate;
in 2D-c the thresholds stay fixed. Window bootstrap (analyze_2a's resamples) as a secondary reading. Holm:
one-sided p = (1 + #{d* <= 0}) / 2001, level 0.025, family per regime = 2D-a (difference with the max) and 2D-b at
both caps plus 2D-c at both alpha, x 3 cells x 2 seeds = 36.

8. HONEST INTERPRETATION
------------------------

- 2D-a fails, clearly. Given the same +-0.25 s tolerance, block-OMP moment-tensor inversion in the manner of [129]
  (BOMP-PK+SC) is statistically indistinguishable from MT-FW+SC: at 0.25 the difference is -0.16 to +0.09, every
  hierarchical CI contains 0, and in strong clutter BOMP-PK+SC is equal or better in 5 of 6 cells. The large 2C-a
  margins (+0.08 to +0.52) were almost entirely the missing tolerance. As preregistered, MT-FW's advantage over [129]
  and [122] is attributed to the peak search and to no other element of the method, and the proposal must say so.
  The moment-tensor stacking of [122] with the tolerance (MTSTACK-PK+SC) stays below MT-FW+SC (+0.21 to +0.35 in
  thin, +0.01 to +0.16 in strong): what separates them is deflation, not the tolerance.
- 2D-b holds: removing the peak search costs 0.06-0.41 recall at 0.25 in all 12 cell x seed cases. The sign is
  consistent, but with 5 networks per cell the hierarchical CIs are wide: the lower bound exceeds 0 in 7 of 12 cases
  and Holm rejects in 2 of 12. The rule is decided by point estimates as preregistered; the network-level
  statistical support for individual cells is weak.
- The cap (descriptive): MT-FW-NOCAP+SC is within -0.09 to +0.13 of MT-FW+SC (all CIs include 0); the cap helps a
  little in thin (5 of 6 negative differences) and not in strong. MT-FW-NOCAP+SC and BOMP-PK+SC have almost
  identical recall in every cell: without the cap, MT-FW is essentially BOMP with peak reading (they differ only by
  the P/S Gram terms and by deflation vs orthogonal refit). The cap is therefore MT-FW's only element beyond
  [129] + tolerance, and its effect is small and not significant.
- GL-FW-PK+SC (descriptive): the convex method with the same tolerance improves over 2C's GL-FW+SC (+0.03 to
  +0.44) but remains below MT-FW+SC (-0.11 to -0.30 at 0.25 in 11 of 12 cases) and below BOMP-PK+SC (-0.04 to
  -0.27). The 2C conclusion stands with equal tolerance: the convex problem on the grid, solved to certified
  optimality for its (realised) dictionary, is not what delivers the performance. Caveat: the PK oracle is a
  heuristic per-station peak, so GL-FW-PK is certified only for the dictionary it builds, not over all time shifts.
- With the tolerance, [129]-style inversion also beats QuakeMigrate (+0.17 to +0.49 at 0.25). The advantage over
  the state of the art established in 2A/2C belongs to peak-tolerant moment-tensor migration with deflation as a
  family, not specifically to MT-FW.
- 2D-c holds under the per-cell reading (strong also under the strict reading; thin misses the strict reading by
  one false event in one cell). Two qualifications are necessary:
  (a) The threshold is calibrated on empty windows, so it controls only false alarms in windows without events.
      At these thresholds MT-FW+SC produces 1.2-2.2 false events per window WITH events in thin (mostly 5-20 km
      from a real event; E1), so its false rate over all windows is 0.7-1.5 per window in thin, 3-8 times
      QuakeMigrate's. The recall gain in 2D-c is partly bought with these event-adjacent false detections. As a
      deployable operating point for the full catalogue, the 2A-e thresholds (calibrated on all windows) remain the
      honest reference; the conformal threshold is deployable only for the question "is there an event in this
      window", not for a false-event-rate budget.
  (b) The conformal guarantee assumes the evaluation windows are exchangeable with the calibration windows. They
      come from different networks: the realised empty-window rate exceeded alpha in 3 of 6 thin cells at 0.1
      (up to 2.2 alpha) and 4 of 6 at 0.25 (up to 1.7 alpha), and on 30 networks (E3) it is close to or slightly above
      alpha. Five calibration networks per cell do not capture the network-to-network variation of the score
      distribution; the preregistered 2 alpha tolerance absorbed it except in one thin cell.
- What the proposal can claim after 2D: moment-tensor migration with a +-0.25 s peak search and greedy deflation
  (MT-FW, or equivalently BOMP with peak reading) is robust to grid and timing errors and beats QuakeMigrate on
  this benchmark; the peak search is the decisive element (2D-b), the cap is marginal, and MT-FW is not superior to
  [129] once [129] is given the same tolerance (2D-a). The peak search is a trade-off, not a free gain: with
  exact timing and on-node sources it costs 0.10-0.14 recall on 6-8 stations (E2). The convex formulation
  remains a motivating framework, not the best-performing method.


9. DEVIATIONS AND PROBLEMS
--------------------------

1. PK variants carry no cap (the 2C methods had none; only the reading time changes). BOMP-PK and GL-FW-PK use the
   block Gram at the peak read times, including the P/S AC cross-term at the peak lag; this is our reading of
   "identical to 2C except that the sample ... at the ... peak".
2. GL-FW-PK: the dictionary grows with the fixed-time atoms; two atoms with the same (node, origin) and different
   times may coexist; the record deduplicates by node (5 km). The duality gap is w.r.t. the realised dictionary,
   and the per-station peak oracle is not a global maximiser over time shifts. FW steps per lambda capped at 400
   (never reached).
3. MT-FW-EX and MT-FW-NOCAP use the dense fw_record loop, not the certified branch and bound (the bound would need
   adapting). The same loop with peak and cap reproduces the 2A records exactly (V2 and all 120 part-(i) controls).
4. 2D-c readings fixed before seeing results (analyze_2d.py docstring): events declared at score > q (strict);
   "realized false-event rate on empty windows" = false events per empty window (>= the fraction of empty windows
   with a false event, also reported); the per-cell reading is primary, the strict reading is reported; the
   calibration uncertainty is not in the CI (thresholds fixed in the bootstrap).
5. Empty windows: new stream tag 2701 (not used before), 40 per calibration network; thresholds pool the 5
   networks of a cell.
6. Hierarchical bootstrap with only 5 networks per cell is coarse (as in the network bootstrap of 2C part (i)).
   Bootstrap p-values have resolution 1/2001; the first Holm step can reach at best 36/2001 = 0.018.
7. Holm family (36 per regime) and level (one-sided 0.025) are our reading of "Holm within regime"; 2D-a
   enters Holm through the difference with the max. Descriptive quantities are not in the family.
8. Kernels as in 2C; this machine's default OpenBLAS kernel is now Cooperlake, so the kernel is always set
   explicitly and checked.
9. analyze_2d.py was modified once after the part files existed: a diagnostics-only line crashed (the BOMP-PK
   shift lists have per-atom length 2 ns and needed flattening). The fix changes that one line; no rule,
   threshold, bootstrap or table computation changed. Hash before: 110aa893...73a7 (task2d_code_hashes.txt,
   02:41:59 UTC); after: 8dd5013b...d2b4.
10. V6 is descriptive and small; one of 12 PK-vs-exact comparisons is negative (MTSTACK-PK, one-sided, -0.08).
    E2 repeats it with 90 windows.
11. Sections 7 and parts of 8 use post-hoc exploratory analyses (E1-E3), declared as such. The verdicts do not
    depend on them.
